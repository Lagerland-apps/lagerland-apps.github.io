Why do most productivity apps fail people?

They're built to raise engagement numbers, and finishing your work is secondary. A flame icon that goes out when you miss one day makes you feel guilty. A feed of "progress" shared with strangers pulls your attention away from the task. A free app that paywalls the parts that make it useful keeps you stuck in it.

Calm productivity plans for the day you will actually have. The apps reward you quietly, charge a clear price, and leave you alone the rest of the time.

Questions

FAQ

What is a calm productivity app?
A calm productivity app helps you finish a realistic day. It has no streaks, points, engagement metrics, or feed, and it doesn't use guilt to bring you back. Taskful Day is built around this idea.
What's the best alternative to streak-based planners?
Taskful Day is a daily planner without streaks that focuses on getting a realistic list done. Missing a day costs you nothing: there is no streak counter and no red X. It suits people who find that streak anxiety gets in the way.
Are these apps good for people with ADHD?
Many users with ADHD prefer simple, single-purpose apps to large productivity suites. Taskful Day's realistic-day planning and Soon's visual countdowns both aim to cut down on overwhelm.
Do any of these apps sell my data?
No. None of the apps contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, none require accounts, and all user data stays on-device. Paid downloads and optional Pro upgrades pay for them.
Is there a calm daily planner that works on Mac as well as iPhone and iPad?
Yes. Taskful Day is now a native Mac app as well as an iPhone and iPad app, and iCloud sync shows the same day on every device. One Universal Purchase covers all three platforms, so there is no separate Mac license.
