Audience → stack

Apps matched to
how you live and work.

Four short pages, each matching a few of our apps to one kind of schedule or job and naming the app to start with.

{% include quick-answer.html %}
For shift workers
Nurses, paramedics, and anyone on nights or rotations: sleep measured from one shift to the next, a daily Readiness score, and tasks and bills that don't slip when your hours move.
For Apple Health power users
Your Apple Watch already records sleep, HRV, and heart data. Observa turns it into a daily Readiness score with the reason, and the analysis stays on your device.
For indie iOS developers
Two Mac apps and an iPhone app for App Store Connect: listing edits with a diff before you push, screenshots in all 50 App Store languages, and sales and reviews on your phone. Your API key stays in the Keychain.
For calm productivity
A day planner that moves unfinished tasks forward without a red overdue list, and countdowns to the dates you care about.
