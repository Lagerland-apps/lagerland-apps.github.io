how you live and work.

Four short pages, each matching a small privacy-first app stack to one kind of schedule or job, with the trade-offs stated plainly.

{% include quick-answer.html %}
For shift workers
Nurses, first responders, and rotating-schedule workers: track sleep around shifts, understand recovery, and plan calm days, all on-device.
For Apple Health power users
You already collect HRV, sleep, and activity data on Apple Watch. These apps turn it into plain-language answers without uploading any of it.
For indie iOS developers
Native Mac apps for App Store Connect metadata, revenue dashboards, and App Store screenshots. You work outside the browser, and credentials stay in your Keychain.
For calm productivity
A planning stack without streaks or ads, covering realistic days, calm reading, and private countdowns. There are no flame counters or guilt mechanics.
