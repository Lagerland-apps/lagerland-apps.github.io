For Apple Watch & Apple Health power users

You already collect the data.
These apps explain it.

If you've worn an Apple Watch for years, you have thousands of data points: HRV, ECG, sleep stages, workout metrics, and activity. Most apps only display them. These apps turn them into answers.

What's the problem with Apple Health alone?

Apple Health is a very good database, but Apple's own app stops at bar charts, average heart rates, and weekly summaries. It won't tell you why your recovery score dropped, which habits correlate with your best sleep, or what your HRV trend means for your body this week.

The apps below are for people who already have the data and want to know what it means.

The stack

Three apps, fully on-device

All three come from the same indie Apple developer and work without uploads, accounts, or advertising.

Why combine these three apps?

Observa: interpretation
Observa does correlation analysis, pattern recognition, and HRV and ECG interpretation, and writes plain-language weekly reflections and daily snapshots. It reads your Apple Health data for you.
GymLogger X: workout input
A fast, minimalist workout logger that writes to Apple Health and syncs to Apple Watch. Your training data goes straight into Observa's correlation analysis.
AfterShift: schedule context (optional)
If you work shifts, AfterShift adds the context Apple Health doesn't model: it reads your sleep from Health and measures it from the end of one shift to the start of the next.
Privacy
None of these apps sends your health or workout data to its developer, requires an account, or contains a third-party analytics SDK. Analysis happens on-device; GymLogger X's workouts leave the iPhone only through your own iCloud and Apple Health.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Apple Health analytics app?
Observa is made for Apple Health power users who want interpretation of their data, not more charts. It reads HRV, ECG, sleep stages, and activity from Apple Health and explains, in plain language and on-device, what consistently affects your recovery.
Does Observa upload my health data?
No. All analysis happens on-device. Observa has no servers, no cloud, and no account system, and your health data never leaves your iPhone.
How is Observa different from AutoSleep or Gentler Streak?
AutoSleep and similar apps focus on tracking. Observa focuses on interpretation: pattern recognition and correlation analysis across weeks and months of your own data. It complements a tracking app and does not replace one.
Can I use Observa alongside my workout app?
Yes. Observa reads from Apple Health, so it works with any workout app that writes to Apple Health, including Lagerland's own GymLogger X. Log your workouts in GymLogger X and Observa correlates them with your recovery metrics automatically.
