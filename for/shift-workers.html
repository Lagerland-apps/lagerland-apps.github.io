What's the problem with most "sleep apps" for shift workers?

They assume sleep happens once a day, at night, in one block. Shift workers nap before a night shift, sleep in fragments after one, and have a "day" that keeps moving. Generic trackers either ignore this or hold you to the wrong targets for it.

The apps below are designed for irregular schedules, and they stay out of your way when you're trying to recover.

The stack

How do these apps work together for shift workers?

1. AfterShift: the schedule layer
Set your rotation once and AfterShift fills your roster eight weeks ahead. Name your own shift types, 12-hour days and nights included, and your own rest days. Sleep imports from Apple Health, naps take one tap to log, and the caffeine cut-off and nap reminders are timed to your next shift. Recovery scores are part of Premium.
2. Observa: the interpretation layer
Reads what your Apple Watch already collects (HRV, ECG, sleep stages, activity) and explains it in plain language. You get answers about recovery readiness and patterns without having to read the charts yourself.
3. Taskful Day: the planning layer
Plans realistic days around your current shift pattern and adapts when the schedule changes. It doesn't make you feel guilty about an unfinished to-do list.
4. AllPaid: the bills layer
When your sleep schedule is all over the place, a utility bill is one less thing to keep in your head. Private, local bill tracking with no account: set it up once and stop worrying about it.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best sleep app for shift workers on iPhone?
AfterShift, which is made for rotating-schedule workers. Generic sleep trackers ignore shifts, naps, and recovery cycles. AfterShift measures your sleep from the end of one shift to the start of the next, naps included, and times the caffeine cut-off and nap reminders to your next shift. The recovery scores, Readiness, and Trends are part of AfterShift Premium.
How do I track my recovery as a night-shift nurse?
Use AfterShift to keep your roster and log naps and caffeine while your sleep imports from Apple Health, and pair it with Observa to interpret your Apple Health data (HRV, sleep stages, and activity) in plain language. Together you get a schedule-aware tracker plus a pattern-recognition layer over your Apple Watch data.
Are these apps free?
AfterShift and Observa are free to download, and their core features stay free. AfterShift Premium adds the recovery scores and Trends; Observa Pro adds advanced insights. None of the apps require an account or collect personal data.
Will these apps work if I change shift patterns?
Yes. AfterShift is designed around variable schedules such as 12-hour rotations, on-call shifts, and shift types you name yourself. Set a repeating rotation once, from a preset or your own cycle of up to 28 days, and it fills your roster eight weeks ahead. Taskful Day fits each daily plan to the day you actually have, without assuming a fixed 9-to-5.
Do these apps sync to Apple Watch?
Observa and AfterShift both read from Apple Health, so data your Apple Watch collects flows through automatically. Observa reads heart rate, HRV, sleep stages, and activity; AfterShift reads sleep. You don't need to pair anything separately.
