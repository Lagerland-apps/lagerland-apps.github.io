What is the best AppMockUp alternative?
For Mac users, Mockly. It makes the same kind of device-framed, multi-language App Store screenshots in a Mac app: six 3D device models, all 50 App Store languages in one project, AI translation with your own key, exact-size PNG or HEIC export, and upload to App Store Connect. It costs {P_APP@mockly} once, with no subscription.
Does Mockly add a watermark like AppMockUp's free tier?
Mockly has no free tier. It's a paid app, and every editor feature, export and upload included, comes with the {P_APP@mockly} purchase. AppMockUp Studio's free tier typically adds a watermark or limits, which a paid plan removes.
Is AppMockUp free, and why pay for Mockly?
AppMockUp's free browser tier is useful when you need a quick framed screenshot. Mockly is {P_APP@mockly} once and suits developers who make a full set every release: 3D devices, 50 languages in one project, AI translation on their own key, and upload to App Store Connect without dragging files into the browser.
Does Mockly support the same device sizes as AppMockUp?
Mockly has artboard presets for iPhone 6.9-inch and 6.5-inch, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, landscape variants, and a 1080×1920 Google Play size. Save Images exports every page as PNG or HEIC at the artboard's exact pixel size, for one language or all of them, and the App Store Connect upload reads the display type from the artboard.
Does Mockly render on-device unlike a web tool?
Yes. Mockly renders and exports on your Mac, and editing and exporting work offline. Text leaves the Mac only when you run AI Translate, going with your key to the provider you chose, and screenshots leave it only when you upload them to App Store Connect. AppMockUp is browser-based.
Does Mockly translate screenshots like AppMockUp?
Yes. AI Translate translates the text blocks on one page or all pages into the languages you pick, using your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google key, and skips blocks marked Do not translate. You can also export a CSV for a translator and import it back. AI output is machine translation, so read it before you ship.
