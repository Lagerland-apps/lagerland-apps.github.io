How do Taskful Day and Productive compare?

Core mechanic
Taskful Day: Plan the day and finish what you can. Unfinished tasks carry forward to today the next time you open the app, and a recurring task that slips keeps its original rhythm.

Productive: Streaks and chains; missing a day breaks the chain.
Free tier
Taskful Day: Up to 50 tasks in total (deleting tasks doesn't reset the count), one workspace, the Journal, weekly Analytics, and free Lock Screen widgets. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro free with no card.

Productive: The free tier is limited to a handful of habits, with more behind a subscription.
How progress is shown
Taskful Day: Analytics shows completion rate, trends, categories, and a current and best streak, with the footer "This is just a pattern, not a judgment."

Productive: Streak-driven momentum and reminders.
Privacy and platforms
Taskful Day: No account, ads, or analytics SDKs. Tasks stay on the device unless you turn on iCloud Sync (Pro), which uses your own private iCloud. Runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

Productive: iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, with the usual freemium app disclosures.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Productive app alternative without streaks?
If streaks work against you, Taskful Day plans the day instead of tracking chains. Unfinished tasks carry forward to today the next time you open the app, recurring tasks keep their rhythm when they carry over, and the Today list shows no overdue badge. The streak is a stat in Analytics (plus a Pro watch complication), never a counter on the Today list. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, with no account.
Does Taskful Day limit how many habits I can track for free?
Yes, in a different way. The free version lets you create up to 50 tasks in total, and deleting tasks doesn't reset the count. Pro removes the limit and adds iCloud Sync, the Day Clock, Energy River, and Day Map views, the Schedule week grid, the focus timer, and 21 Home Screen widgets. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro free with no card, so you can try it before you decide.
Why switch from Productive to Taskful Day?
Productive's streak system works well for people who respond to chains. If a single missed day kills your motivation, or you want to plan whole days rather than a habit list, Taskful Day fits better. You can type a task as a sentence, speak it on iPhone or iPad, or dictate it on Apple Watch, and what you don't finish moves to today on its own.
Does Taskful Day require an account?
No. Taskful Day works without an account and carries no ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs. Your tasks stay on the device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, a Pro feature that uses your own private iCloud.
Is Taskful Day available on Mac?
Yes. Taskful Day runs on Mac with macOS 14 or later, with a toolbar, menus, keyboard shortcuts, and desktop widgets, and it is free to download there as on iPhone and iPad. With Pro, iCloud Sync keeps your Mac and your other devices on the same plan. It also has an Apple Watch app, which needs the iPhone app.
How does Taskful Day's pricing compare to Productive?
Taskful Day is free to download and lets you create up to 50 tasks in total for free. Productive is freemium, with a habit-count limit on the free tier. Its US App Store listing showed Premium at $10.99 a month, with yearly plans from $19.99 to $79.99, when we checked on 29 October 2026.
