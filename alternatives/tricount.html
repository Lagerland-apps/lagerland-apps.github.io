How does RightSplit compare to Tricount?

Tricount tracks a shared trip; RightSplit closes out one bill.

Workflow
RightSplit: Scan the receipt, assign items, share each person's total, and you're done.

Tricount: Create a group, log expenses as they happen, and settle at the end of the trip.
Receipt OCR
RightSplit: On-device Apple Vision framework; the photo never leaves your phone.

Tricount: Manual entry of each expense; no receipt OCR as a core feature.
Group setup
RightSplit: None. Type the names of who's at the table.

Tricount: Create a tricount, invite participants, and track expenses as you go.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Tricount?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • You mostly split a single dinner, not a multi-day trip
  • You'd rather scan the receipt than type 12 line items
  • You want item-level fairness with proportional tip math
  • You don't want everyone at the table to need an account
  • You prefer a lifetime price option to a subscription
Stick with Tricount if…
  • You're on a group trip and want a running balance everyone in the group can see and add to
  • Several people need to log who paid for what from their own phones
  • Your group is comfortable with everyone using the same app
  • You don't need OCR because manual entry is fast enough

FAQ

What is the best Tricount alternative for one-off bill splitting?
RightSplit is a privacy-first iPhone alternative to Tricount for moments when a single bill needs splitting now. It scans receipts on-device with Apple's Vision framework, splits item-by-item with proportional tip distribution, and sends each person their total via iMessage or WhatsApp, without a group, a shared ledger or an account.
Does RightSplit replace Tricount for group trips?
Not if the whole group keeps the tab. Tricount is a shared trip ledger: everyone joins, logs expenses from their own phone and sees the balance over days or weeks. RightSplit settles one bill at a time, and with Pro+ a trip Space gathers the trip's receipts and expenses on your phone, with each person's balance, a who-pays-whom settle-up plan, and mixed currencies converted at European Central Bank rates. Nobody else needs the app, but nobody else can add to it either. If the group wants a running balance everyone can see and add to, Tricount is the right tool.
How is RightSplit's pricing different from Tricount Pro?
Tricount is freemium with a Pro subscription for advanced features. RightSplit is free for equal splits and your first receipt scan, with unlimited scans and item-based splitting on a {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/year or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} lifetime Pro+ tier. The lifetime option is one purchase with no recurring charge and no auto-renew.
