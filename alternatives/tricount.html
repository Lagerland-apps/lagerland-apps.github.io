How does RightSplit compare to Tricount?

Tricount tracks a shared trip; RightSplit closes out one bill.

Workflow
RightSplit: Scan the receipt, tap who had each item, and send each person their amount with a tap-to-pay link.

Tricount: Create a group, log expenses as they happen, and settle at the end of the trip.
Reading the receipt
RightSplit: Read on your iPhone: by Apple Intelligence on an eligible iPhone with it turned on (iOS 26 or later), by an on-device scanner on every other iPhone. Receipts in 16 languages; the photo never leaves your phone.

Tricount: Manual entry of each expense; no receipt OCR as a core feature.
Group setup
RightSplit: None. Type the names of who's at the table; only you need the app.

Tricount: Create a tricount, invite participants, and track expenses as you go.
Pricing
RightSplit: Equal splits and your first scan are free. Pro+ is {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/yr or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once.

Tricount: Free, plus a Pro subscription.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Tricount?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • You mostly split a single dinner, not a multi-day trip
  • You'd rather scan the receipt than type 12 line items
  • You want each person to pay for what they ordered, with shared dishes divided among the people who shared them
  • You don't want everyone at the table to need an account
  • You want each person's message to carry a link to pay you by Venmo, PayPal.me, Revolut, Wise, IBAN, or Apple Pay
Stick with Tricount if…
  • You're on a group trip and want a running balance everyone in the group can see and add to
  • Several people need to log who paid for what from their own phones
  • Your group is comfortable with everyone using the same app
  • You'd rather type expenses as they happen than scan receipts

FAQ

What is the best Tricount alternative for one-off bill splitting?
RightSplit is an iPhone alternative to Tricount for when a single bill needs splitting now. The receipt is read on your iPhone, you tap who had each item, and each person gets a message with their exact amount and a tap-to-pay link. There is no group to set up, no shared ledger, and no account. Splitting by item is part of Pro+.
Does RightSplit replace Tricount for group trips?
Not if the whole group keeps the tab. Tricount is a shared trip ledger: everyone joins, logs expenses from their own phone, and sees the balance over days or weeks. RightSplit settles one bill at a time, and with Pro+ a trip Space gathers the trip's receipts and expenses on your phone, with each person's balance and the fewest transfers to settle up. A trip in several currencies settles in the one you choose, using daily European Central Bank rates (30 of the 58 currencies have one). Nobody else needs the app, but nobody else can add to it either, so if the group wants a balance everyone can see and add to, Tricount is the right tool.
How is RightSplit's pricing different from Tricount Pro?
Tricount is free with a Pro subscription for extra features. RightSplit is free for equal splits with tip, fair rounding, and your first receipt scan. Pro+ adds unlimited scans, item splits, Spaces, and the payment tracker, for {P_YEARLY@rightsplit} a year after a {TRIAL@rightsplit}, or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once. The lifetime purchase does not renew.
