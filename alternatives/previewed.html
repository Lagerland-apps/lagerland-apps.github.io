FAQ

What is the best Previewed alternative?
For Mac developers who want a one-time price, Mockly. It makes device-framed, multi-language App Store screenshots with six 3D device models, keeps all 50 App Store languages in one project, translates with your own AI key, and uploads to App Store Connect. It costs {P_APP@mockly} once instead of a subscription.
How does Mockly's pricing compare to Previewed?
Mockly is {P_APP@mockly}, paid once, with no subscription, and the price includes every editor feature. Previewed has a free tier and paid subscription plans. If you use AI Translate in Mockly, your AI provider bills you for that directly.
Does Mockly render on-device unlike a web tool?
Yes. Projects are files on your Mac, and editing and exporting work offline. Two things leave the Mac, and only when you start them: when you run AI Translate, the text of your text blocks goes with your key to the provider you chose, and when you upload, the rendered screenshots go to Apple. Lagerland Apps runs no server for Mockly and never sees either.
Does Mockly support the same device sizes as Previewed?
Mockly has artboard presets for iPhone 6.9-inch and 6.5-inch, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, landscape variants, and a 1080×1920 Google Play size. Save Images exports every page as PNG or HEIC at the artboard's exact pixel size, for one language or all of them, and the App Store Connect upload reads the display type from the artboard.
Does Previewed do things Mockly doesn't?
Yes. Previewed has a broad web toolkit with templates, social media mockups, and animations, and it runs in any browser. Mockly is a Mac app for App Store screenshots only. It has no templates and no video or PDF export. If you want an all-in-one web design tool, Previewed covers more.
Does Mockly handle translation like Previewed?
Yes, in a different way. Mockly's AI Translate sends the text of your text blocks straight from your Mac to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google with your own key, and Lagerland Apps never sees it. You can also export a CSV for a translator. Previewed translates inside its hosted service.
