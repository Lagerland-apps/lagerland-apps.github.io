A Whoop alternative
without a second band on your wrist.

Whoop is excellent dedicated recovery hardware, and it comes as a band you wear plus a membership and a cloud account. Observa is software only. It reads the sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and workouts your Apple Watch saves to Apple Health, gives you a morning Readiness score with the reasons, and answers "Should I train today?" on your wrist. The analysis runs on your device, and there is no account to create.

How does Observa compare to Whoop?

Whoop sells hardware with a membership; Observa is software that works on data your Apple Watch already collects.

Hardware
Observa: None of its own. It reads Apple Health, with an Apple Watch as the recommended source, and has its own Apple Watch app with complications.

Whoop: A dedicated band with its own sensors.
What it gives you
Observa: Readiness from 0 to 100 with "Why this score?", a Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest call, training load over 7 and 28 days, and an Effort Score for each workout.

Whoop: A daily recovery and strain score with cloud coaching.
Privacy
Observa: No account and no analytics SDKs; the analysis runs on your device. Privacy Manifest.

Whoop: Account- and cloud-based; data stored on its servers.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Whoop alternative without a subscription or band?
If you already wear an Apple Watch, Observa. It builds a daily Readiness score from the sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and workouts your watch saves to Apple Health, shows the reasons, and gives a Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest call. The daily scores are free, and Pro has a lifetime option that never renews.
Do I need an Apple Watch to use Observa, or can it use my Whoop data?
Observa reads Apple Health, whichever device saved the data, and an Apple Watch is the recommended source. Whoop can share some metrics to Apple Health, but Observa has not been checked against Whoop data, so it may not fill every score. Look in Apple Health first to see what the band saves there.
Can Observa replace my Whoop band?
It can if you wear an Apple Watch and want a recovery answer without a second device. Whoop's band has the edge for granular, high-frequency sensor data. Observa adds no sensors; it builds Readiness from your Apple Watch data, explains it, and puts the training call on your wrist.
Is Observa cheaper than Whoop?
For Apple Watch owners, yes. Whoop is a ~$30/month membership including hardware. Observa's daily scores are free, and Pro costs {P_MONTHLY@observa}/month, {P_YEARLY@observa}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@observa} once.
Is Observa more private than Whoop?
Observa has no account and no analytics SDKs, and the app makes no network calls of its own, so your health data stays on your device. Whoop is account- and cloud-based.
Does Observa give a recovery score like Whoop?
Yes. Readiness runs from 0 to 100 from sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and training load against your own typical range, banded Ready, Moderate, or Low, and "Why this score?" shows what moved it. In place of a strain score, training load compares your last 7 days with the last 28, and each workout gets an Effort Score. The method differs from Whoop's, so the numbers won't match.
Does Observa need any extra hardware?
No. In place of a band, Observa uses the Apple Watch you already wear and reads what it saves to Apple Health. An Apple Watch is recommended because sleep stages and HRV only appear when a device records them. It runs on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.
