Looking for a
Brick alternative?

Brick is a physical tag you tap to lock your phone, and the friction comes from leaving the tag somewhere else. For people who want a tangible ritual it works well. It is also one more object to buy and keep track of. EarnLock is only software. The apps you pick stay locked until Apple Health shows you've moved, halfway to your goal opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight.

When should I pick Brick instead?

Brick is a strong tool, and for some people the hardware is the point. Pick it if:

  • You want a physical commitment device. Tapping a tag and walking away from it is a more deliberate ritual than any software gate.
  • You don't want your screen time tied to movement, and you're happy to manage a small object.
  • You want one solution across iPhone and Android.
  • You'd rather buy a device once and keep the app free.

EarnLock suits people who don't want another gadget to carry and would rather earn the unlock with a walk or a workout. Apple's Screen Time controls do the locking, and Apple Health does the counting.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Brick?
If you want a commitment without hardware, EarnLock is a Brick alternative built around movement. It locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. Halfway opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight. Locking one app with a step goal is free for good, and there is no account. If a physical ritual is what works for you, Brick is the better pick.
How is EarnLock different from Brick?
Brick ties the unlock to a physical device: tap to lock, tap again to unlock, so leaving it at home keeps you blocked. EarnLock ties the unlock to your daily activity, measured by Apple Health, and a locked app shows what's left, such as "2,026 steps until unlock". EarnLock starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked. There's nothing to buy or carry.
How much does EarnLock cost compared with Brick?

Do I need to carry anything with EarnLock?
No. Brick depends on its device: leave it at home and your apps stay blocked, which is the point, and it also means you have an object to manage. EarnLock's key is your daily activity goal, read from Apple Health on your iPhone. With Premium, the Apple Watch app shows your progress on your wrist.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, makes no network connections of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Apple Health is read on your iPhone and never written to or sent anywhere, and Apple's Screen Time picker gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. EarnLock is built for limiting your own iPhone and has no parent controls.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Brick can?
On iPhone, for apps, yes. EarnLock locks any app or whole category you can choose in Apple's Screen Time picker: one on the free tier, as many as you like with Premium. They differ in what opens the lock.
