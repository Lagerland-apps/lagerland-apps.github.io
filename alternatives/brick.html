When should I pick Brick instead?

Brick is a strong tool, and for some people the hardware is the whole point. Pick it if:

  • You want a physical commitment device. Tapping a tag and walking away from it is a more deliberate ritual than any software gate.
  • You don't want anything tied to activity or movement, and you're happy to manage a small object.
  • You want one solution that spans iOS and Android via NFC.
  • You like that there's no recurring cost once you own the device.

EarnLock suits people who don't want another gadget to buy, carry, and lose, and would rather pay for the unlock with a walk or a workout. Apple's Family Controls enforces the block, Apple Health confirms the activity, the count shows on your wrist, and it costs {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} once with nothing physical to track.

What is the best alternative to Brick?
EarnLock is a Brick alternative that needs no hardware and unlocks your apps when you move. Brick is a physical NFC tag (around $59) you tap to start and end focus sessions. EarnLock shields your chosen apps through Apple's Family Controls until you reach a real daily activity goal measured by Apple Health (steps, active minutes, or active energy), and every blocked app shows a live progress ring. Lifetime is {P_LIFETIME@earnlock}, paid once, and there is nothing to carry. EarnLock has no account, analytics SDK, or server.
How is EarnLock different from Brick?
Brick is hardware. You tap your phone to a physical NFC tag to block or unblock, and the friction comes from leaving the tag somewhere else. EarnLock is software only. The friction is a daily activity goal your body has to meet, measured by Apple Health, and the apps unlock automatically when you reach it. There is nothing to buy, charge, carry, or lose, and the count also shows on your Apple Watch. Brick gives a stronger physical commitment to people who want a tangible object involved.
Is EarnLock cheaper than Brick?
Yes. The Brick app is free but does nothing until you buy the physical device (commonly around $59). EarnLock is software only: free to start, then {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime (one-time), with no hardware to buy. If you would rather not pay for a gadget you then have to keep track of, EarnLock costs less. Check Brick's current device price before buying.
Do I need to carry anything with EarnLock?
No. Brick depends on its physical tag. Leave it at home and your apps stay blocked, which is the point, but it also means you have an object to manage. EarnLock has nothing to carry. The gate is your daily activity goal, read from Apple Health on the iPhone and Apple Watch, so the only thing you need to bring is yourself.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, no server, and no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. HealthKit data is read on the device and never leaves it. Apple's App Privacy label has shown Data Not Collected for every release. EarnLock is for restricting yourself and is not a parental-control app.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Brick can?
Yes. EarnLock can block any iOS app or category you choose in Apple's Family Controls picker, which covers the same kinds of apps Brick removes during a session. The unlock is what differs: Brick needs a tap on the physical tag, and EarnLock unlocks automatically when you reach your daily activity goal.
