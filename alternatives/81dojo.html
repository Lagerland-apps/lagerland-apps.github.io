How does Shogiful compare to 81Dojo?

One is a community and the other is a coach, so they cover different parts of learning shogi.

What it is
Shogiful: Native single-player training app with AI opponents, engine analysis, and adaptive training.

81Dojo: International online shogi server with rated human games, teaching games, tournaments, and a community.
Feedback on your play
Shogiful: Automatic. YaneuraOu analyzes every game, and each significant mistake is classified and explained in sentences.

81Dojo: Human. Post-game reviews and teaching games happen when stronger players are available and willing.
Kanji barrier
Shogiful: Western piece sets with movement indicators, progressive kanji introduction.

81Dojo: Traditional presentation; the international community helps, but the board assumes piece literacy.
Offline & privacy
Shogiful: Fully offline, no account, no tracking SDKs.

81Dojo: Online server; account required to play.
Structured training
Shogiful: Tsume puzzles plus exercises generated from your own mistakes, scheduled with spaced repetition across five tracked skills.

81Dojo: Improvement comes from playing and community study; there is no built-in training system.

FAQ

What is the best 81Dojo alternative for solo training?
Shogiful is the closest alternative for structured solo improvement. YaneuraOu analyzes every game on-device, mistakes are explained in plain language, and training is generated from your own weaknesses with spaced repetition. It runs fully offline on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with no account.
How is Shogiful different from 81Dojo?
81Dojo is built around people: rated games, teaching games, and its community. Shogiful is built around a loop of play, analyze, understand, and train, with an engine and coaching layer that works offline after every game, whenever you ask.
Is Shogiful good for beginners who can't read kanji?
Yes, they are the players it was made for. Western piece sets with movement indicators make the board readable from the first game, and kanji is introduced gradually as you train.
How much does Shogiful cost compared to 81Dojo?
Shogiful is free with all 10 opponents, 2 analyses/day, and 3 puzzles/day; Premium is {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month, {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} lifetime. 81Dojo is free with optional supporter memberships.
Does Shogiful have teaching games like 81Dojo?
Not with humans. Shogiful has tiered AI opponents from true beginner to advanced, full analysis of every game with plain-language explanations, and training aimed at the weaknesses it detects. It is available any time, with nothing to schedule.
Can I use both 81Dojo and Shogiful?
Yes, they work well together. Play rated games on 81Dojo; analyze your habits, drill tsume, and fix recurring mistakes in Shogiful.
