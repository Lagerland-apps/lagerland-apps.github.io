How does Observa compare to Athlytic?

They do different jobs. Athlytic scores your recovery and Observa interprets it. You can run both, or pick the one that answers the question you actually have.

What it does
Observa: Reads Apple Health data, builds a 60-day personal baseline for each metric, and writes up single-variable (free) and multi-variable (Pro) correlations as plain-English sentences with sample size and confidence.

Athlytic: Combines HRV, RHR, sleep, and strain into a daily recovery percentage and effort score in the WHOOP visual style.
What you ask it
Observa: "Which of my habits is consistently moving my recovery?" "Why was this week different?" "Is my training load sustainable?"

Athlytic: "How recovered am I today?" "What's my strain score?" "Should I train hard or take it easy?"
Data handling
Observa: Fully on-device, with no server, account, or analytics SDKs. Apple's App Privacy nutrition label lists zero data collected.

Athlytic: Reads Apple Health; check the current App Store privacy disclosures for any cloud or analytics.
Pricing
Observa: Free, with Pro from {P_MONTHLY@observa}/mo or {P_LIFETIME@observa} lifetime.

Athlytic: Typically a one-time purchase or annual subscription via the App Store.

When to pick Observa over Athlytic

  • You already understand what a recovery score is and want to know what keeps moving yours
  • You care more about your personal HRV (SDNN) and sleep-efficiency baseline than about a 0 to 100 dashboard
  • You want named multi-variable correlations (training load × caffeine timing × sleep) with sample size and confidence
  • You want all computation to happen on your device, with the App Privacy nutrition label to prove it
  • You'd rather pay {P_LIFETIME@observa} once than subscribe every year for a score you already understand

When to keep Athlytic instead

  • You want a single daily score and don't need the reasoning behind it
  • You like the WHOOP-style visualisation and use the recovery percentage as your main signal
  • You train at high volume and plan each day around a strain score

FAQ

What is the best Athlytic alternative?
If you want interpretation more than a single score, Observa is the closest match. It reads the same Apple Health data (HRV (SDNN), RHR, sleep stages, ECG, workouts) and explains in plain English which habits keep moving each metric against your personal 60-day baseline. It is free, runs fully on-device, and needs no account.
Does Observa replace the Athlytic recovery score?
Not as a number. Observa doesn't compute a single 0 to 100 percentage. It scores each metric on its own against your baseline and tells you what's moving it. If you only want a daily dashboard score, Athlytic is the better fit; if you want to know why that score changed, use Observa.
Is Observa more private than Athlytic?
Yes. Observa runs entirely on-device with no servers, no account, and no third-party analytics, and Apple's App Privacy nutrition label lists it as collecting zero data. Athlytic has historically been a privacy-friendly app, but check its current App Store disclosure for any cloud sync or analytics.
Can I use Observa and Athlytic together?
Yes. Both read from the same Apple Health pool, so running them side by side gives you Athlytic's daily score and the named correlations behind it from Observa.
