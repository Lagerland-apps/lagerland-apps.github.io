How does Soon. compare to Countdowns by Apalon?

Both apps count down. They differ in what each one ships in the binary and what that binary sends back to advertisers.

Ads & tracking SDKs
Soon.: No ads on any tier, and no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.

Countdowns (Apalon): Banner and interstitial ads on the free tier. Standard advertising and analytics SDKs are typical for the publisher.
Account requirement
Soon.: No account at all. Events live on your iPhone.

Countdowns (Apalon): Optional account for cross-device sync.
Event-type planning (Plan Mode)
Soon.: Built-in templates for Trip, Wedding, Birthday, Pregnancy, Graduation, and Move, with each task scheduled backward from the event date.

Countdowns (Apalon): Generic countdown events; no event-type checklist templates.
Destination weather
Soon.: Apple WeatherKit forecast at the destination (Premium), using coarse location only.

Countdowns (Apalon): No weather integration.
Widgets
Soon.: Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy in every iOS size.

Countdowns (Apalon): Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; StandBy not advertised.
One-time purchase
Soon.: {P_LIFETIME@soon} lifetime option in addition to monthly / yearly.

Countdowns (Apalon): Subscription-only Premium typical for the publisher.

Who should pick Soon. vs Countdowns by Apalon?

Pick Soon. if…
  • You won't put up with ads in a utility app you open every day
  • You don't want analytics or advertising SDKs in a countdown app's binary
  • You want event-type planning, destination weather, and StandBy widgets
  • You prefer a one-time purchase to an open-ended subscription
  • You want privacy claims you can check on Apple's App Privacy nutrition label
Stick with Countdowns (Apalon) if…
  • You use a lot of Countdowns' theme library and don't want to migrate
  • You need explicit account-based cross-device sync now
  • You're fine with ads as the price of the free tier

FAQ

What is the best Countdowns (Apalon) alternative for iPhone without ads?
Soon. is the most direct privacy-first alternative to Countdowns by Apalon. Both turn dates into countdowns. Soon. has no ads on any tier, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no account, and it adds event-type Plan Mode checklists, Apple WeatherKit destination forecasts, and StandBy widgets that the Apalon app does not offer.
Why is Countdowns by Apalon ad-supported?
Apalon is a large mobile-app publisher whose business model on most consumer apps relies on a combination of ad inventory and subscription unlocks. That is a legitimate business choice, and it means the free tier of Countdowns includes banner and interstitial ads, and the app loads third-party advertising and analytics SDKs. Soon. is built by a one-person studio with no advertising or analytics SDKs in the binary at all.
Does Soon. have widgets like Countdowns by Apalon?
Yes, and in more places. Soon. supports Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy widgets in every iOS size: small, medium, large, ring, minimal, and timeline. Countdowns by Apalon ships Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; StandBy support is not advertised on its current App Store listing.
How much does Soon. cost compared to Countdowns?
Soon. is free with 5 events, one widget, three themes, calendar import, memories, and reflection mode, and it shows no ads. Premium is {P_MONTHLY@soon}/month, {P_YEARLY@soon}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@soon} once for lifetime access with a 7-day free trial. Countdowns by Apalon is free with ads, with a Premium subscription that removes ads and unlocks additional features.
Does Soon. count down to multiple events like Countdowns?
Yes. The free tier supports 5 simultaneous events; Premium is unlimited. Each event can have its own photo background, location, Plan Mode checklist, daily note, and widget assignment. No ads or upsell screens sit between events to slow you down.
