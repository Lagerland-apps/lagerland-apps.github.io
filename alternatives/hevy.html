How does LiftLog compare to Hevy?

Logging a set works much the same in both. The pricing, the account, and the social side differ.

Pricing model
LiftLog: Free for 7 days with no card, then one payment at the tier you choose, from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}. Every tier unlocks the same app, and Family Sharing is on.

Hevy: Freemium. Hevy Pro unlocks advanced features for $2.99 or $3.99 a month, $23.99 a year, or $74.99 as a lifetime purchase (US App Store, checked 29 October 2026).
Programs
LiftLog: 40 ready-made programs based on well-known routines, plus your own. With a progression scheme (Double progression, Linear, % of training max, or RPE / RIR), each set arrives with the weight to lift.

Hevy: Routines library, share-with-friends programs, large community templates.
Logging a set
LiftLog: A docked keypad that never covers the set you're on. Each row shows last session's numbers, the rest timer starts when you check a set off, and a PR badge appears the moment you beat your estimated 1RM history.

Hevy: Set logging with previous values, a rest timer, and PR tracking, plus an Apple Watch app.
Social
LiftLog: None. It is a tool for training solo. Workout Complete can save a summary image if you want to share one.

Hevy: Friends, social feed, workout sharing.
Privacy and account
LiftLog: No account, analytics, or ads. Workouts stay on your iPhone and every feature works offline. See the Privacy Manifest.

Hevy: Account required for sync and social.
Devices
LiftLog: iPhone only, with no iCloud sync. Move your history to a new iPhone with a JSON backup.

Hevy: Syncs your log through your account across devices.

Who should pick LiftLog vs Hevy?

Pick LiftLog if…
  • You train solo and don't need a social feed
  • You're tired of fitness subscriptions and want to pay once
  • You want a ready-made program that suggests your next weight
  • You'd rather your workout history stay on your iPhone than on someone else's server
Stick with Hevy if…
  • You use the friends, feed, and sharing features
  • You rely on the community routines library
  • You want to log sets on Apple Watch, or sync between devices
  • You have years of Hevy history: LiftLog can't import it

FAQ

What is the best Hevy alternative for iPhone?
LiftLog suits lifters who like Hevy's logging but want to skip the account and the social feed. It fills each set from your last session, starts the rest timer when you check a set off, and shows a PR badge the moment a set beats your estimated 1RM history. It has 1,500 exercises with animated demos, 40 ready-made programs that can suggest your next weight, 10 widgets, and 6 Siri actions. It is free for 7 days with no card, then a one-time purchase from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}.
Is LiftLog a one-time purchase?
Yes. LiftLog is free for 7 days with no card required. After that you buy it once at the tier you choose: {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} (Fair), {P_IAP:Suggested@liftlog} (Good), {P_IAP:Supporter@liftlog} (Generous), or {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog} (Patron). Every tier unlocks the same app, nothing renews, and Family Sharing is on. If you never buy, your history, charts, and exports stay readable.
How is LiftLog different from GymLogger X?
Both are strength trackers from Lagerland Apps. LiftLog runs on iPhone only and is a one-time purchase after a 7-day trial, with 40 ready-made programs, smart weight suggestions, and 10 widgets. GymLogger X is a separate app with its own Apple Watch app. Pick GymLogger X if you want to log sets from your wrist.
Does LiftLog track me?
No. LiftLog needs no account and carries no analytics, ads, or third-party SDKs. Workouts stay on your iPhone, every feature works offline, and there is no cloud sync. You can export your history as JSON or CSV at any time.
