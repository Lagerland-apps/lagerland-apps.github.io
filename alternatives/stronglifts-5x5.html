FAQ

Is there a StrongLifts 5x5 alternative without a subscription?
GymLogger X is one. Its core logging is free, and Pro is {P_MONTHLY@gymlogger-x} a month, {P_YEARLY@gymlogger-x} a year, or {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} once for lifetime access. StrongLifts' US App Store listing says the app is free to download but needs a subscription to use, with Pro at $11.99 a month, $29.99 a quarter or $59.99 a year, plus a five-year option. Check the App Store for current prices in your country.
Can I run a 5x5 program in GymLogger X?
Yes. GymLogger X's program library includes Linear 5×5 Foundation in the free tier: two alternating workouts, three days a week, five sets of five with one heavy deadlift set. Pro adds other 5×5-style programs such as Intermediate 5×5 Ramp, Heavy / Light / Medium 5×5 and the Texas Method, along with programs that have nothing to do with 5×5.
Does GymLogger X add weight automatically like StrongLifts?
No. StrongLifts adds weight when you complete all your sets and lowers it automatically after repeated failed workouts, with the increment and the deload amount under your control. GymLogger X shows the weight you used last time and leaves the next increase to you. If you want the app to decide every weight, StrongLifts does that better.
Do both apps work on Apple Watch?
Yes. StrongLifts' Watch app logs sets with one tap, runs rest timer notifications, has warm-up and plate calculators and shows heart rate and workout duration. In GymLogger X you start the session on the iPhone, then log each set's reps and weight, run the rest timer and watch live heart rate from the wrist, with the phone staying in your bag.
What does GymLogger X do beyond 5x5?
Its Smart Program Creator (one program on the free tier, unlimited with Pro) builds Full Body, Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs, Push/Pull, Arnold and Bro Split programs with linear, undulating, block or wave periodization, reps-in-reserve targets and a deload week every 4 to 6 weeks depending on your level. Pro also flags plateaus with a published rule based on your estimated 1RM trend and volume.
Which is better for a complete beginner?
For someone who wants to start barbell training with no decisions to make, StrongLifts is hard to beat: one program, weights chosen for you, form videos and automatic resets. GymLogger X suits beginners who want a free log, a free linear 5×5 to start on, and room to move to other programs later without switching apps.
