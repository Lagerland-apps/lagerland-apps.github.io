When should I pick ScreenZen instead?

ScreenZen is excellent, and for a lot of people it's the right choice:

  • You want a completely free tool with no paid tier at all. ScreenZen is free, the developer has committed to keeping it that way, and EarnLock can't beat free.
  • The escalating pause works for you. If a delay of 10, then 30, then 60 seconds is enough to break the habit, you don't need a harder block.
  • You need Android too. ScreenZen is cross-platform; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only.
  • You don't want activity goals tied to movement. EarnLock's free tier reads step data; ScreenZen asks nothing of your body.

EarnLock is for the person who has waited out the pause one too many times and wants to pay for the unlock with movement, enforced by Apple's Family Controls and counted by Apple Health. If ScreenZen's friction already does the job for you, keep using it.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to ScreenZen?
EarnLock is a movement-based, privacy-first alternative to ScreenZen. ScreenZen is free and adds an escalating pause before distracting apps open, plus time limits and schedules. EarnLock shields your chosen apps through Apple's Family Controls until you hit a real daily activity goal (steps, active minutes, or active energy) measured by Apple Health, and paints a live progress ring on every blocked app. EarnLock is free to start, with a {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime tier. It has no account, no analytics SDK, and no server.
How is EarnLock different from ScreenZen?
ScreenZen uses a pause that gets longer the more you reopen an app, plus per-app limits, and you can wait both out. EarnLock keeps the app shielded until you hit a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health, so you pay for the unlock with movement. EarnLock also paints a live progress ring on each blocked app and has a full Apple Watch app. ScreenZen is fully free and cross-platform; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only.
Is ScreenZen really free?
Yes. ScreenZen is fully free, with no premium tier, subscription, or in-app purchases, which is a real advantage. EarnLock is also free to start (one blocked app with steps-based unlocks, custom shield, widgets, streaks, emergency unlock). Its optional Premium tier, at {P_MONTHLY@earnlock}/mo, {P_YEARLY@earnlock}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime, adds unlimited apps, active-minute and calorie goals, the Apple Watch app, schedules, and Week and Month insights. If a completely free pause-based tool is all you need, ScreenZen is a good pick.
Does EarnLock enforce a harder block than ScreenZen?
Yes. ScreenZen adds an escalating delay and limits that you can eventually get past. EarnLock keeps the app shielded until your daily activity goal is met, with one emergency unlock per day behind a math problem. If you've found pause-based tools easy to wait out, EarnLock's movement requirement is harder to dodge.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, no server, and no third-party SDKs of any kind, analytics and advertising included. HealthKit data is read on the device and never leaves it. Apple's App Privacy nutrition label shows Data Not Collected for every release. EarnLock is for restricting yourself and is never a parental control.
Can EarnLock block the same apps ScreenZen can?
Yes. Both apps build on Apple's Family Controls / Screen Time APIs, so you pick the apps to block in the same iOS-provided picker. Any app or category ScreenZen can slow down, EarnLock can shield. ScreenZen adds a pause you can wait out, while EarnLock keeps the app locked until you move.
