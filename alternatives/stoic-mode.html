When should I pick Stoic Mode instead?

Pick a Stoic-style intervention tool if any of these matter more to you than a lock tied to movement:

  • You think awareness and friction change habits better over time than a lock does. For many people that's true.
  • You want reflection and mindfulness at the center of how you limit screen time.
  • You don't want your screen time tied to movement, or you can't rely on activity data.
  • Its focus periods and interventions fit your routine better. Check the current feature set first.

EarnLock is for someone who has tried the gentle approach and wants the lock to hold until they've moved. The reflective step stays: tap Reflect when you reach for a locked app and log why.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Stoic Mode?
If you want a lock that holds, EarnLock is a Stoic Mode alternative built around movement. It locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. Halfway opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight. Locking one app with a step goal is free for good, and there is no account. If reflection and friction already work for you, a Stoic-style tool is the gentler choice.
How is EarnLock different from Stoic Mode?
Stoic-style tools ask you to reconsider and leave the decision with you. EarnLock keeps the app locked until Apple Health shows you've moved enough that day, with a math-gated emergency unlock once a day. A locked app shows what's left, such as "2,026 steps until unlock". EarnLock also starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked, and its Reflect button lets you log why you reached.
Does EarnLock enforce a harder block?
Yes. Intervention-first tools are designed to make you reconsider, and they don't stop you outright. EarnLock keeps the app locked until your daily activity goal is met, with one emergency unlock per day behind a two-digit math problem. iOS still lets you turn off EarnLock's Screen Time access, so it's a commitment you choose.
How much does EarnLock cost?
Locking one app or category with a step goal is free for good, with partial and full unlocks, Shadow Week, streaks with a weekly rest day, the Insights Day view, every widget, and the emergency unlock. Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, makes no network connections of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Apple Health is read on your iPhone and never written to or sent anywhere, and Apple's Screen Time picker gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. EarnLock is built for limiting your own iPhone and has no parent controls.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Stoic Mode can?
For apps, EarnLock locks any app or whole category you can choose in Apple's Screen Time picker: one on the free tier, as many as you like with Premium. They differ in what it takes to get back in.
