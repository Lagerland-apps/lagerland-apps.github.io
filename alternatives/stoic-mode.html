When should I pick Stoic Mode instead?

Pick a Stoic-style intervention tool if any of these matter more to you than an enforced block tied to movement:

  • You think awareness and friction change habits better over time than a hard wall does. For many people that's true.
  • You want reflection and mindfulness as part of how you limit screen time.
  • You don't want a block tied to movement, or you can't rely on step / activity data.
  • Its focus-period and intervention features fit your routine better. Check the current feature set first.

EarnLock is for someone who has tried the gentle approach and wants the block to hold. The unlock costs a walk or a workout; Apple's Family Controls does the blocking, Apple Health does the counting, and the count sits on their wrist.

How is EarnLock different from Stoic Mode?
Stoic-style tools add friction and awareness so you choose differently, with focus-period shields on top.
