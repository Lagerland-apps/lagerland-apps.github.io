A Lishogi alternative

How does Shogiful compare to Lishogi?

Lishogi is a platform; Shogiful is a coach.

What it is
Shogiful: A native single-player training app: you play AI opponents, get coached, and train your weaknesses.

Lishogi: A free, open-source web platform with online matchmaking, server analysis, puzzles, and tournaments.
Native app vs browser
Shogiful: Native on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with one Universal Purchase and Mac keyboard shortcuts. No browser tab.

Lishogi: Web only; no official native iOS app.
Analysis & coaching
Shogiful: On-device YaneuraOu plus coaching that sorts mistakes into shogi-specific categories and explains them in sentences.

Lishogi: Server-side engine analysis with an evaluation graph that you interpret yourself.
Offline support
Shogiful: Fully offline. Engine, puzzles, opponents, and training are all on-device.

Lishogi: Requires a connection for play and analysis.
Kanji barrier
Shogiful: Western piece sets with movement indicators, progressive kanji introduction, piece guide in every game.

Lishogi: Also has Western-friendly notation and piece-set options, and works well for non-Japanese players.
Pricing
Shogiful: Free tier; Premium {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/mo, {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} lifetime.

Lishogi: Entirely free and donation-funded, with no premium tier.

Questions

FAQ

Is there a native iPhone or Mac app like Lishogi?
Lishogi is a web platform without an official native iOS app. Shogiful is the closest native equivalent for the training side: a native iPhone, iPad, and Mac app with on-device YaneuraOu analysis, plain-language mistake explanations, tsume puzzles, and adaptive training, fully offline with no account.
How is Shogiful different from Lishogi?
Lishogi is a platform for online play, server analysis, puzzles, and tournaments, free and open-source. Shogiful is a coach: it analyzes every game, classifies your mistakes into shogi-specific categories, explains each one in sentences, and builds a spaced-repetition training queue from your actual weaknesses.
Is Shogiful free like Lishogi?
Shogiful has a permanent free tier (all opponents, 2 analyses/day, 3 puzzles/day, no ads); Premium is {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month, {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} lifetime. Lishogi is entirely free and donation-funded, so if cost is all that matters, Lishogi wins. Shogiful's case rests on the native offline app and the coaching.
Can I play shogi offline with Shogiful?
Yes, completely. The engine, analysis, coaching, puzzles, and all 10 AI opponents run on your device. Lishogi requires a connection; Shogiful keeps working on a flight.
Does Shogiful have puzzles like Lishogi?
Yes: a verified tsume-shogi library scheduled with spaced repetition, mixed with exercises generated from the mistakes in your own games. An expanded ~500-problem library is on the public roadmap.
Can I use both Lishogi and Shogiful?
Yes. Play humans on Lishogi and train offline in Shogiful. KIF import, which would let you bring Lishogi games into Shogiful's analyzer, is under consideration on the roadmap.
