Apple Health insights · Private · On-device · No account

A Welltory alternative
that keeps your analysis on your device.

Welltory measures HRV (often through the camera), syncs health data to its servers, and puts much of the analysis behind a subscription and an account. Observa takes no HRV readings of its own. It uses the HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep your Apple Watch already saves to Apple Health, turns them into a morning Readiness score and a stress read against your own baseline, and shows which signals moved each one. There is no account, and the app makes no network calls of its own.

FAQ

Do I need an Apple Watch, or does Observa work with Oura, Garmin, and Fitbit?
Observa reads Apple Health, whichever device or app saved the data there. An Apple Watch is recommended, because sleep stages, HRV, blood oxygen, ECG, and VO₂ max only appear when a device records them. Observa has not been checked against every third-party wearable, so look in Apple Health first to see which metrics your ring or watch actually saves there.
What is the best Welltory alternative without an account?
Observa, if you want your data explained without a cloud account. It reads the HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep in Apple Health and gives you Readiness, a Sleep Score, and a stress read with its drivers named, all against your own baseline and computed on your device.
Is Observa more private than Welltory?
Observa has no account and no analytics SDKs, and the app makes no network calls of its own, so your health data stays on your device. Welltory syncs data to its cloud behind an account.
Does Observa measure HRV with the camera like Welltory?
No. Observa takes no HRV readings of its own; it uses the HRV (SDNN) your Apple Watch saves to Apple Health. Use Welltory for spot camera readings and Observa for a daily read from data you already have.
Does Observa avoid Welltory's subscription?
Observa's daily scores, "Why this score?", the training call, and 7-day trends are free. Pro costs {P_MONTHLY@observa}/month, {P_YEARLY@observa}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@observa} once, and lifetime has no recurring cost. Welltory is freemium, with much of its analysis behind a subscription.
What does Observa tell me that Welltory doesn't?
Observa is built around one morning question: how recovered are you, and what should you do today? Readiness weighs sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and training load, "Why this score?" names what moved it, and "Should I train today?" answers Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest. Welltory offers a broader stress and energy dashboard.
Can I use both Observa and Welltory?
Yes. Take camera HRV readings in Welltory if you like, and use Observa for the daily Readiness score, the training call, and sleep debt against your own goal.
