Forest alternative?

When should I pick Forest instead?

Pick Forest if any of these matter more to you than a lock tied to movement:

  • Growing trees, unlocking species, and building a forest keep you going.
  • You want your focus time to fund real tree planting. Forest's partner program does that; EarnLock does nothing like it.
  • You need more than one platform. Forest runs on iOS, Android, and as a browser extension; EarnLock is an iPhone app.
  • You want group focus sessions with friends, and an account is fine with you.
  • You want apps blocked for set focus sessions or on a schedule, without tying the unlock to exercise.

EarnLock suits someone who wants the unlock to cost a walk. The lock stays on until you've moved, halfway to your goal still earns an hour, and with Premium the count shows on your Apple Watch.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Forest?
If you want your apps to open only after you move, EarnLock is a Forest alternative built around movement. It locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. Halfway opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight. Locking one app with a step goal is free for good, and there is no account. For focus timers and real tree planting, Forest is the better pick.
How is EarnLock different from Forest?
Forest blocks apps for a focus session or a scheduled window, and a tree grows while you stay off the phone. EarnLock locks the apps you pick with Apple's Screen Time controls until Apple Health shows you've moved enough that day, with a math-gated emergency unlock once a day. A locked app shows what's left, such as "2,026 steps until unlock". EarnLock also starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked, and Premium includes an Apple Watch app. Forest plants real trees and runs on Android and in the browser; EarnLock is an iPhone app.

Does Forest actually block apps?
Yes. On iOS 16 or later, Forest blocks apps outside your Allow List during a focus session, and its Time Guard feature blocks apps on a schedule. Those blocks end with the session or the scheduled window. EarnLock's lock ends when your daily activity goal is met, or when you use your one emergency unlock of the day, which asks you to solve a two-digit math problem.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, makes no network connections of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Apple Health is read on your iPhone and never written to or sent anywhere, and Apple's Screen Time picker gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. EarnLock is built for limiting your own iPhone and has no parent controls.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Forest can?
EarnLock locks any app or whole category you can choose in Apple's Screen Time picker: one on the free tier, as many as you like with Premium. During a Forest session, apps outside your Allow List are blocked. The bigger difference is what opens them: with EarnLock, it's steps or other activity measured by Apple Health.
