When should I pick Forest instead?

Pick Forest if any of these matter more to you than an enforced block tied to movement:

  • Growing trees, unlocking species, and building a forest keep you motivated.
  • You want your focus time to fund real tree planting. Forest's partner program has planted well over a million real trees; EarnLock does nothing like it.
  • You need more than one platform. Forest runs on iOS, Android, and as a browser extension; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only.
  • You want group focus sessions with friends, and you don't mind an account.
  • A gentle nudge works for you and you don't want a hard system-level shield.

EarnLock suits someone who found the tree easy to abandon and wants to pay for an unlock with movement, with Apple's Family Controls doing the blocking and Apple Health doing the counting.

What is the best alternative to Forest?
EarnLock is a movement-based, privacy-first alternative to Forest. Forest grows a virtual tree that dies if you leave the app, a gentle, gamified kind of accountability. EarnLock uses Apple's Family Controls to shield your chosen apps until you hit a real daily activity goal (steps, active minutes, or active energy) measured by Apple Health. The custom shield on every blocked app shows a live progress ring with the exact count remaining. It is free to start, or {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime (one-time), and has no account, analytics SDK, or server.
How is EarnLock different from Forest?
Forest is a gamified focus timer: a tree grows while you stay off the phone and withers if you leave. The accountability is emotional, and nothing physically stops you from leaving. EarnLock enforces a real block through Apple's Family Controls and unlocks only when your body hits a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. It also has a full Apple Watch app, so the count runs without the iPhone. Forest plants real trees and works cross-platform; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only.
Is EarnLock cheaper than Forest?
Not on the upfront price. Forest is roughly a $3.99 one-time purchase on iOS and is free to start, which is hard to beat. Does Forest actually block apps?
Forest's core mechanic is a focus timer with a virtual tree that dies if you leave the app. It works as a motivational nudge and does not block apps at the system level. EarnLock uses Apple's Family Controls framework to shield the apps you pick across the whole device, so they cannot be opened until your daily activity goal is met (or you spend your one emergency unlock, which asks you to solve a math problem).
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, no server, and no third-party SDKs of any kind, analytics and advertising included. It reads HealthKit data on the device and never sends it anywhere. Apple's App Privacy nutrition label shows Data Not Collected for every release. It is for restricting yourself and is not a parental control app.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Forest can?
EarnLock can block any iOS app or category you choose in Apple's Family Controls picker, such as Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Reddit, or games, across the whole device. Forest's focus sessions encourage you to stay off the phone entirely and do not shield specific apps at the system level. If your goal is "these specific apps stay locked until I move," EarnLock is the more direct fit.
