FAQ

Does WalkMyScreen work on iPhone?
Not yet. WalkMyScreen is on Google Play for Android, and its website lists the iPhone version as coming soon. On iPhone today, EarnLock locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health.
What is the best WalkMyScreen alternative for iPhone?
EarnLock works on the same idea on iPhone: your movement unlocks your apps. It locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily goal in steps (free) or active minutes or active calories (Premium). Halfway opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight. It needs no account, and locking one app is free for good.
How does EarnLock compare to WalkMyScreen?
Both use steps to unlock screen time, and both need no account. WalkMyScreen earns you minutes as you walk on top of a baseline, runs on Android, and is free with optional ads for bonus minutes. EarnLock uses one daily goal with an hour at halfway, runs on iPhone with an Apple Watch app in Premium, and starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked.
Is there a free WalkMyScreen alternative for iPhone?
Yes. Locking one app or category with a step goal is free for good, with partial and full unlocks, Shadow Week, streaks with a weekly rest day, the Insights Day view, every widget, and the emergency unlock. Why does EarnLock use Apple's Screen Time controls?
On iPhone, Apple's Screen Time controls are the way an app can lock other apps. You choose what to lock in Apple's own picker, which gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. iOS also lets you turn that access off in Settings, so the lock is a commitment you choose.
