How does AfterShift compare to OffShift?

We're not claiming AfterShift is universally "better" — OffShift is a serious app with a clear point of view. The honest framing is where the two apps emphasize different things. Here are the differences that matter in practice.

How naps factor into your recovery
AfterShift: Naps count toward the Shift Recovery Score as real sleep — a 90-minute pre-shift nap actually moves the number. Nap timing and grogginess patterns are shown separately in Premium.

OffShift: Tracks naps and has nap-window guidance as part of the sleep plan, but core night sleep is the dominant score driver.
How caffeine timing is calculated
AfterShift: Calibrated to your actual next shift. The cut-off reminder moves with your next shift start, your caffeine sensitivity, and how strong your usual drink is. No fixed 2 p.m. rule.

OffShift: Caffeine cutoffs are part of the personalised sleep plan and shift around your block. Both apps share this idea; AfterShift's framing is more aggressively shift-specific.
Rotation pattern awareness
AfterShift: Rotation presets — three-shift (day → evening → night), 4-on-4-off, 4-on-3-off, 2 days / 2 nights, Panama/Pitman (2-2-3), fixed nights or days — plus custom cycles of up to 28 days (enough for DuPont) and on-call shift types. A sleep-debt trend shows which cycles in a block left you furthest behind.

OffShift: Has rotation templates and detects shift patterns from your history. Strong for power users who set up templates once and reuse them.
Privacy model
AfterShift: No account, no third-party SDKs, no health-data resale. Calendar and health data never leave the device. Privacy Manifest.

OffShift: Also no account, no ads, no tracking. The two apps are roughly equivalent on privacy posture.
Apple Watch & widgets
AfterShift: Apple Health-driven; iPhone-first, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island. No Watch app — an Apple Watch adds sleep-stage detail through Apple Health but isn't required.

OffShift: Has a dedicated Watch app, complications, Live Activity, and Dynamic Island. If you live on your Watch, this is OffShift's territory.
Pricing
AfterShift: Free with Premium at $3.99/month, $24.99/year, or $39.99 once for lifetime.

OffShift: Free with Pro at $3.99/month, $29.99/year, or a one-time lifetime option. Both apps offer a lifetime purchase.

Who should pick AfterShift vs OffShift?

Pick AfterShift if…
  • You rely on naps and want them treated as recovery, not as a tracked footnote
  • You want caffeine recommendations that move when your next shift moves, not a daily cutoff
  • You work a repeating rotation (2-2-3, 4-on-4-off, or a custom cycle like DuPont) and want it to fill your roster for you
  • iPhone-first is fine — you don't need an Apple Watch app to be the centre of gravity
Stick with OffShift if…
  • You wear an Apple Watch and want a strong dedicated Watch app + complications
  • Your rotation is fixed and complex, and OffShift's template engine is the feature you use most
  • You already have months of OffShift sleep history you don't want to lose

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to OffShift on iPhone?
AfterShift is built for the same audience — nurses, paramedics, firefighters, pilots, factory workers — and shares the same on-device, no-account, no-tracking privacy posture. The differences are in emphasis: AfterShift treats naps as first-class recovery (counted directly in the Shift Recovery Score), times caffeine to your actual next shift rather than a fixed daily cutoff, and fills your roster from rotations like 2-2-3, 4-on-4-off, or your own cycle of up to 28 days — long enough for DuPont.
How is AfterShift different from OffShift?
OffShift and AfterShift overlap heavily in audience and privacy model. The substantive differences: (1) AfterShift counts nap sleep directly in the Shift Recovery Score rather than tracking it as a separate signal; (2) AfterShift's caffeine timing is calibrated against your scheduled next shift rather than a textbook 2 p.m. cutoff; (3) AfterShift scores each window between your actual shifts, whatever the pattern — with rotation presets such as 2-2-3 and 4-on-4-off, custom cycles of up to 28 days, and day, evening, night, and on-call shift types.
Does AfterShift have an Apple Watch app?
No. AfterShift has no Apple Watch app — it reads the sleep your Apple Watch (or another tracker) already saves to Apple Health, and you can log naps by hand. A Watch adds sleep-stage and continuity detail, but is not required. The full app experience runs on iPhone, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island.
Does AfterShift sell my health data?
No. AfterShift has no ads, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no data resale. Sleep, naps, and shift data stay on your device. Health data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export a summary.
Is AfterShift free?
Yes. Sleep and nap tracking, basic recovery summaries, calendar shift import, next-shift widgets, and shift-timed reminders — including the caffeine cut-off — are free. Premium ($3.99/month, $24.99/year, or $39.99 lifetime) unlocks the full Shift Recovery Score, fatigue and fragmentation insights, cycle-to-cycle trends, automatic calendar sync with overtime tracking, and a shareable PDF report.
