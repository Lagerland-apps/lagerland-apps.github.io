Shift-worker sleep · Privacy-first · On-device

Looking for an
OffShift alternative?

OffShift is a well-made shift-worker sleep app for the same people AfterShift serves: nurses, paramedics, firefighters, pilots, and factory workers on rotating, night, or on-call shifts. Both apps keep data on-device and have no account, ads, or analytics SDKs. If OffShift fits your routine, keep using it. AfterShift is built around two ideas: naps count as real recovery, and caffeine is timed to your actual next shift instead of a fixed daily cutoff.

How does AfterShift compare to OffShift?

OffShift is a serious app with a clear point of view, and AfterShift isn't better at everything. What follows is where the two apps emphasize different things in daily use.

How naps factor into your recovery
AfterShift: Naps count toward the Shift Recovery Score as real sleep, so a 90-minute pre-shift nap moves the number. Nap timing and grogginess patterns are shown separately in Premium.

OffShift: Tracks naps and has nap-window guidance as part of the sleep plan, but core night sleep is the dominant score driver.
How caffeine timing is calculated
AfterShift: Calibrated to your actual next shift. The cut-off reminder moves with your next shift start, your caffeine sensitivity, and how strong your usual drink is, and there is no fixed 2 p.m. rule.

OffShift: Caffeine cutoffs are part of the personalised sleep plan and shift around your block. Both apps share this idea; AfterShift ties it more tightly to each specific shift.
Rotation pattern awareness
AfterShift: Rotation presets: three-shift (day → evening → night), 4-on-4-off, 4-on-3-off, 2 days / 2 nights, Panama/Pitman (2-2-3), fixed nights or days, plus custom cycles of up to 28 days (enough for DuPont) and on-call shift types. A sleep-debt trend shows which cycles in a block left you furthest behind.

OffShift: Has rotation templates and detects shift patterns from your history. It suits power users who set up templates once and reuse them.
Privacy model
AfterShift: No account, no third-party SDKs, no health-data resale. Calendar and health data never leave the device. Privacy Manifest.

OffShift: Also has no account, ads, or tracking. On privacy the two apps are roughly equal.
Apple Watch & widgets
AfterShift: Reads Apple Health and runs on iPhone, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island. There is no Watch app; an Apple Watch adds sleep-stage detail through Apple Health but isn't required.

OffShift: Has a dedicated Watch app, complications, Live Activity, and Dynamic Island. If you live on your Watch, OffShift is stronger here.

OffShift: Free with Pro at $3.99/month, $29.99/year, or a one-time lifetime option. Both apps offer a lifetime purchase.

Who should pick AfterShift vs OffShift?

Pick AfterShift if…
  • You rely on naps and want them counted as recovery
  • You want caffeine recommendations that move when your next shift moves
  • You work a repeating rotation (2-2-3, 4-on-4-off, or a custom cycle like DuPont) and want it to fill your roster for you
  • An iPhone app is enough, and you don't need a dedicated Apple Watch app
Stick with OffShift if…
  • You wear an Apple Watch and want a strong dedicated Watch app + complications
  • Your rotation is fixed and complex, and OffShift's template engine is the feature you use most
  • You already have months of OffShift sleep history you don't want to lose

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to OffShift on iPhone?
AfterShift is made for the same people (nurses, paramedics, firefighters, pilots, factory workers) and has the same privacy model: on-device, no account, no tracking. It puts the weight in different places. AfterShift counts naps as real recovery in the Shift Recovery Score, times caffeine to your actual next shift instead of a fixed daily cutoff, and fills your roster from rotations like 2-2-3, 4-on-4-off, or your own cycle of up to 28 days, which is long enough for DuPont.
How is AfterShift different from OffShift?
OffShift and AfterShift share most of their audience and the same privacy model. Where they differ: (1) AfterShift counts nap sleep directly in the Shift Recovery Score instead of tracking it as a separate signal; (2) AfterShift times caffeine against your scheduled next shift, with no textbook 2 p.m. cutoff; (3) AfterShift scores each window between your actual shifts, whatever the pattern, with rotation presets such as 2-2-3 and 4-on-4-off, custom cycles of up to 28 days, and day, evening, night, and on-call shift types.
Does AfterShift have an Apple Watch app?
No. AfterShift has no Apple Watch app. It reads the sleep your Apple Watch (or another tracker) already saves to Apple Health, and you can log naps by hand. A Watch adds sleep-stage and continuity detail but is not required. The whole app runs on iPhone, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island.
Does AfterShift sell my health data?
No. AfterShift has no ads, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no data resale. Sleep, naps, and shift data stay on your device. Health data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export a summary.
Is AfterShift free?
Yes. Sleep and nap tracking, basic recovery summaries, calendar shift import, next-shift widgets, and shift-timed reminders, including the caffeine cut-off, are free.
