How does Chessful compare to Lichess?

The two overlap, but each solves a different problem.

Where the analysis runs
Chessful: Stockfish runs on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and needs no connection, so it works on a flight or on the subway.

Lichess: Stockfish runs in the cloud. It is free and unlimited but needs a connection.
How mistakes are explained
Chessful: A card for each mistake: what you played, what was best, why, and the refutation line, worded for your coaching level and tagged with one of 10 mistake types.

Lichess: Evaluation graph and engine lines. Excellent and free, but reading them well takes practice.
Training
Chessful: Today's Training builds puzzles from your own mistakes, and spaced repetition brings misses back. The library adds 15,000 puzzles in 53 themes, drawn from the Lichess open puzzle database and bundled for offline use.

Lichess: Puzzle Storm, Puzzle Racer, and a large themed puzzle pool, not derived from your games.
Live human play
Chessful: None. You play 40 built-in opponents from 400 to 2400 Elo, each with a play style, strengths, and weaknesses.

Lichess: Live games against people at any time of day, which is what it is best known for.
Account and privacy
Chessful: No account or sign-up. No ads, analytics, or tracking, and your games stay on your device.

Lichess: Optional account for rated play, minimal analytics, no ads. As good as it gets for an online platform, though it is still online.
Price
Chessful: Free with daily limits (2 analyses, 3 puzzles, 2 coach hints). Premium {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/mo, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} lifetime.

Lichess: Free, funded by donations to the non-profit.

Questions

FAQ

Is there a Lichess alternative that works offline?
Chessful works offline for everything you do with chess, though it has no live games against people. Lichess runs Stockfish in the cloud and gives free unlimited analysis when you're online. Chessful runs Stockfish on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac with no internet and no account, explains each mistake in plain language, and builds your training from your own games. Only purchases and Game Center need a connection.
Is Chessful a replacement for Lichess?
No. Lichess is the right tool for rated games against people, live matchmaking, Puzzle Storm, and its large opening database. Chessful has none of those. It is a training and analysis app to use alongside Lichess or Chess.com: play your games there, then bring them to Chessful to see what went wrong.
Can I import my Lichess games into Chessful?
Yes, as PGN files. Export your games from Lichess, then open the file in Chessful from Files, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop, or paste the PGN text. One import takes up to 500 games, flags duplicates, and lets you pick which side you played. Chessful doesn't connect to Lichess or fetch games by username.
Why use Chessful instead of just Lichess's free analysis?
Lichess's analysis shows an evaluation graph and engine lines. Chessful adds the words: each mistake card shows what you played, what was best, why the better move works, and the line that punishes yours, worded for the coaching level you pick. Your mistakes then become the puzzles in your daily training, and spaced repetition brings back the ones you miss. It all works offline, with no account.
Is Chessful free like Lichess?
Partly. Chessful is free with daily limits: 2 analyses, 3 puzzles, and 2 coach hints a day, plus all 156 lessons, every mistake explanation, and the first 10 opponents.
