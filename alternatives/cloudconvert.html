How does MediaKit compare to CloudConvert?

A web service against an app that does the work on your iPhone or Mac.

Where conversion happens
MediaKit: On your iPhone or Mac. Files never leave the device, and there is no upload to wait for.

CloudConvert: On remote servers, after an upload.
Privacy
MediaKit: No account, and no analytics SDK. See the Privacy Manifest.

MediaKit: The common formats across six kinds of file. No WebM, and no MP3 output.

Questions

FAQ

What is a CloudConvert alternative that doesn't upload files?
MediaKit is an iPhone and Mac app that does the everyday conversions on your device. CloudConvert processes files on its servers, so you upload each one first. MediaKit has more than 150 tools for video, audio, image, PDF, archive, and ebook files, and it needs no account.
Is MediaKit more private than CloudConvert?
With CloudConvert your file goes to a remote server for processing. MediaKit runs every tool on your iPhone or Mac, and the app makes no network requests of its own, so a contract, a scan, or a family video stays on the device. There is no account, and the app has no analytics SDK. MediaKit's Privacy Manifest is on the transparency page.
Does MediaKit have daily conversion limits like CloudConvert?
MediaKit has no daily quota or conversion minutes. Its limits are by tool instead: five tools stay free for good, one file at a time, and your first 3 days unlock every other tool for single-file runs. Pro unlocks every tool, batch processing, and unlimited exports. CloudConvert's free tier allows a set number of conversions per day before you pay for minutes.
Can MediaKit convert as many formats as CloudConvert?
No. CloudConvert's strength is a very large catalogue of format pairs in any browser. MediaKit covers the common ones: video including MKV, audio to M4A (AAC), WAV, AIFF, ALAC, or CAF, images including HEIC, WebP, and camera RAW input, PDFs, ZIP and other archives plus RAR extraction, and DRM-free ebooks. It does not support WebM and cannot write MP3. For an unusual format, CloudConvert is the better pick.
Does MediaKit run on iPhone as well as Mac?
Yes. MediaKit runs on iPhone and Mac, and one purchase covers both. There is no iPad version. CloudConvert runs in any browser, including on Windows, Android, and iPad.
Does MediaKit need an internet connection?
No. Every tool runs on your device, so MediaKit works offline. The one exception is subtitle translation, which uses Apple's Translation language packs; macOS downloads them the first time. CloudConvert needs a connection and an upload for every job.
How much does MediaKit cost compared to CloudConvert?
Five MediaKit tools are free for good: Video Compress, Image Compress, Image Resize, Audio Convert, and PDF Compress. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@mediakit}/month or {P_YEARLY@mediakit}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@mediakit}, or {P_LIFETIME@mediakit} once with no renewal. CloudConvert is free up to a daily limit, then charges for conversion minutes.
