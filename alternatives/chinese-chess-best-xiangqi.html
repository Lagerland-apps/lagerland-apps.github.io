FAQ

What is a good alternative to Chinese Chess - Best XiangQi?
Xiangqiful, if you want your games explained as well as played. Both apps let you play xiangqi offline against the computer. Xiangqiful adds a review of every game: Pikafish grades each move on your device, names mistakes such as a screenless cannon or a missed Iron Bolt mate, and turns them into drills and mate problems. It has no ads and collects no data.
Does Xiangqiful work offline like Best XiangQi?
Yes. The engine, the analysis, the lessons, the puzzles, and all the opponents run on your device, so it plays the same in airplane mode. There is no account and no server of its own; only purchases and the optional Game Center use Apple's servers.
Is Best XiangQi free? Is Xiangqiful?
Chinese Chess - Best XiangQi is free with no in-app purchases; its privacy label lists identifiers used for tracking and third-party advertising. Xiangqiful is free to download with no ads: unlimited games against the first 10 of its 40 opponents, all 80 lessons, the first drill set in every drill module, 2 game analyses, 3 training puzzles, and 2 coach hints a day. On price alone, Best XiangQi wins.
Which app has more puzzles?
They count different things. Best XiangQi lists more than 600 endgame puzzles. Xiangqiful has 902 mate problems (consecutive-check puzzles from mate-in-1 to mate-in-7, 708 from eight classical manuals, the Shiqing Yaqu supplying the most, and 194 original compositions) plus 150+ drills across nine modules. Xiangqiful's own solver re-checked every mate line, and book problems that don't work were left out.
Can I play with western pieces in both apps?
Yes. Best XiangQi offers western symbols as well as Hanzi and vintage pieces. Xiangqiful has three sets: traditional characters, western symbols, and a hybrid that shows both so you learn the characters as you play. It also offers traditional or simplified characters and WXF or Chinese notation.
How strong are Xiangqiful's opponents?
There are 40 opponents in eight tiers, from Novice to Master, on Xiangqiful's own strength scale, not an Elo rating. Each plays a style, such as aggressive, defensive, cannoneer, or endgame grinder. Shīfu sits on rung 33 and the Sage at the top. The first 10 are free with unlimited games, and Premium opens all 40. Game Review uses Pikafish, a strong open-source engine.
