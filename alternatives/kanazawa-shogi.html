FAQ

What is the best Kanazawa Shogi alternative in English?
Shogiful is a modern alternative with full English text. Like Kanazawa Shogi it plays offline, against 40 opponents. After the game, YaneuraOu reviews every move on your device, and each mistake gets a card with the better move, the cost in pawns, and a coach's tip. You can play with kanji pieces, kanji with movement arrows, or chess-style pieces with no kanji. It runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
How is Shogiful different from Kanazawa Shogi?
Both let you play a computer opponent offline. The difference comes after the game. Kanazawa Shogi gives you a result and the next level. Shogiful gives you a move-by-move review that sorts your mistakes into 12 shogi mistake types, such as Drop Mistake, Promotion Mistake, Castle Weakness, and Missed Tsume, with a card for each one. Train These Weaknesses then opens practice on the mistakes you repeat, and the daily plan brings back the puzzles you missed.
Does Shogiful have difficulty levels like Kanazawa Shogi's 100 levels?
Shogiful has 40 named opponents in eight tiers of five, from Koishi to Meijin, where Kanazawa Shogi has a 100-step strength dial on one engine. The ladder runs from gentle beginner opponents to master level. Each opponent plays one of seven styles, such as aggressive, castle builder, or drop tactician, and you unlock the next by beating the current one twice.
Can I play Shogiful without reading kanji?
Yes. Choose the Chess piece style for chess-style pieces with no kanji, or International for kanji with arrows that show how each piece moves. Traditional kanji pieces are there when you want them, and moves can be shown in Western (P-7f) or Japanese notation. Traditional shogi programs like Kanazawa Shogi use kanji pieces.
How much does Shogiful cost compared to Kanazawa Shogi?
Shogiful is free to download, and play is never limited: you get unlimited games against all 40 opponents, which unlock as you win, plus 2 game analyses, 3 training puzzles, and 2 coach hints a day, with no ads. Premium removes those limits for {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month or {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, each with a {TRIAL@shogiful}, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} once, and Family Sharing is included. Kanazawa Shogi is sold as a paid app, with editions that vary by platform and region. Its price buys an opponent; Shogiful's Premium buys unlimited review and training.
Is Shogiful's engine strong enough compared to Kanazawa Shogi?
Shogiful uses YaneuraOu, an open-source shogi engine, running on your device with its neural-network evaluation file. Its opponents run from gentle beginners to master level, and you can set the analysis depth in Settings. If your goal is to test yourself against the strongest dial-a-level opponent you can find, Kanazawa Shogi remains a respectable choice.
