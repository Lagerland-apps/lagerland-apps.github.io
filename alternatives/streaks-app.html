How do Taskful Day and Streaks compare?

Core mechanic
Taskful Day: Plan a realistic day and finish what you can. A missed day carries no penalty.

Streaks: Maintain an unbroken daily streak. Missing a day resets the counter.
Emotional tone
Taskful Day: Calm and minimal, with no gamification or guilt mechanics.

Streaks: Gamified progress, with flames, counts, and streaks for motivation.
Best for
Taskful Day: People who find streak pressure counterproductive, those with variable schedules, and anyone recovering from productivity burnout.

Streaks: People who respond to gamification and enjoy keeping a chain unbroken.

Taskful Day: No account, tracking, or ads. The core is free, and Pro is a subscription.

Questions

FAQ

Is there a habit app without streaks?
Yes. Taskful Day is a daily planner where you plan a realistic day; there is no streak to maintain. It has no flame counter, no red X for missed days, and no shame mechanics. If streak anxiety works against you, it is a calmer option than streak-based habit apps.
What is the best calm alternative to Streaks app?
Taskful Day is about finishing a realistic day, with no streak to keep alive. It has focus mode, smart insights, voice capture, and no-guilt rescheduling, and it suits people who bounce off gamified habit apps.
Does Taskful Day require an account?
No. Taskful Day has no account, no third-party tracking, and no advertising. Data stays on-device and syncs via your own iCloud account when enabled.
Is Taskful Day good for people with ADHD?
Many users with ADHD find streak-based apps counterproductive because a single missed day resets motivation. Taskful Day plans a realistic day and lets you reschedule without guilt, so it allows for days that go differently than planned.
Is Taskful Day available on Mac?
Yes. Taskful Day is a native Mac app as well as an iPhone and iPad app, and one Universal Purchase covers all three. iCloud sync keeps your day the same on every device.
How is Taskful Day's pricing different from Streaks?
Taskful Day is free with an optional Pro subscription from {P_MONTHLY@taskful-day}/month, and the core daily-plan flow stays free. Streaks is a single $4.99 one-time purchase. Taskful Day costs nothing for casual use; Streaks asks for one payment upfront.
Can I switch from Streaks to Taskful Day without losing my data?
There is no direct import, because Streaks and Taskful Day model habits very differently (chains vs. daily plans). The practical way to switch is to keep Streaks installed for a week, set up your day in Taskful Day alongside it, and see which one fits your life before deleting either.
