How does Xiangqiful compare to Xiangqi.com?

An online platform and a training app. Here's where each one fits.

Core purpose
Xiangqiful: Single-player training — play ten AI opponents, get every game reviewed, then train what the review found.

Xiangqi.com: Online play — real-time games against people, friend challenges, chat, tournaments and leaderboards, with bots, puzzles and lessons alongside.
Engine analysis
Xiangqiful: Pikafish runs on your device with its 53 MB NNUE network. Every move is graded on seven steps from best to blunder, with accuracy for both sides, and mistakes are named in xiangqi terms — a screenless cannon, a trapped horse, broken advisors, a missed Iron Bolt mate.

Xiangqi.com: In-app engine analysis for reviewing your games. Xiangqi.com credits Pikafish too, so the engine isn't the difference — what Xiangqiful adds is the naming and the training built from it.
Reading the pieces
Xiangqiful: Three piece sets — traditional characters, western symbols, and a hybrid that carries both. Traditional or simplified characters; WXF or Chinese notation; an optional 3D board.

Xiangqi.com: Traditional pieces and an International style with western symbols — a genuinely good on-ramp for players who don't read Chinese.
Puzzles & lessons
Xiangqiful: 902 mate problems, mate-in-1 to mate-in-7, most from eight classical manuals such as the Shiqing Yaqu (1570), each line re-checked by the app's own solver. 150+ drills in nine modules and 80 lessons in 8 tracks.

Xiangqi.com: Thousands of puzzles from beginner to expert, plus interactive lessons for beginners and improving players.
Offline & privacy
Xiangqiful: Fully offline — no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Xiangqi.com: An online service with accounts; a recent update added an offline mode for computer and local two-player games. Its privacy label lists data linked to you, including email, gameplay content and usage data.
Price & platforms
Xiangqiful: Free; Premium $1.99/month or $9.99/year (one-week free trial), or $19.99 once. iPhone, iPad and Mac with one purchase.

Xiangqi.com: Free to play, with an optional ad-free subscription. iPhone and iPad, plus Android and the web.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Xiangqi.com alternative for training?
Xiangqiful, if what you want is to improve rather than play online. Xiangqi.com is built around games against people, with bots, puzzles and lessons alongside. Xiangqiful is built around the improvement loop: you play ten AI opponents, Pikafish grades every move on your device and names each mistake in xiangqi terms, and your next drills and mate problems come from what you missed. It runs fully offline with no account on iPhone, iPad and Mac. For online play, keep Xiangqi.com.
Does Xiangqiful have online play like Xiangqi.com?
No, deliberately. Xiangqiful is a single-player training app with no accounts, no servers and no matchmaking. Its ten AI opponents run from strength 400 to 1900 on Xiangqiful's own scale (not Elo), games are untimed, and you unlock the next opponent by beating the current one twice. For games against people, Xiangqi.com is the better tool.
Xiangqi.com is free. Why would I pay for Xiangqiful?
You don't have to. Xiangqiful's free tier never expires: unlimited games against all ten opponents, all 80 lessons, the first drill set in every drill module, 2 full game analyses a day, 3 training puzzles a day and 2 coach hints a day, with no ads. Premium ($1.99/month or $9.99/year with a one-week free trial, or $19.99 once) removes the daily limits and unlocks every drill, deep multi-line analysis and the skill radar and trends. Xiangqi.com is free to play too, so on price alone it wins; Xiangqiful's case is offline, private coaching.
Do Xiangqi.com and Xiangqiful use the same engine?
Xiangqi.com's release notes credit Pikafish, and Xiangqiful runs Pikafish, the open-source engine derived from Stockfish that tops the computer-xiangqi rating lists. The difference is what happens around it: Xiangqiful runs the engine on your own device with no account, grades every move on seven steps from best to blunder, names each mistake in xiangqi terms and turns those mistakes into your next training session.
Can I play either app without reading Chinese characters?
Yes. Xiangqi.com offers an International piece style with western symbols. Xiangqiful has three piece sets — traditional characters, western symbols, and a hybrid that shows both so you learn the characters as you play — plus traditional or simplified characters and WXF or Chinese notation.
Can I use both Xiangqi.com and Xiangqiful?
Yes, and that is the pattern we'd suggest: play people on Xiangqi.com, then train offline in Xiangqiful. If you have a game's moves as WXF or ICCS movetext, you can import it into Xiangqiful and analyze it; XQF files are not supported in this version, and Xiangqiful does not sync with any online account.
