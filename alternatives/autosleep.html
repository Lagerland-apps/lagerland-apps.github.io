AutoSleep alternative?

AutoSleep is a reliable automatic sleep tracker for Apple Watch. If you want a direct replacement, Observa isn't one. It doesn't track sleep. It interprets what is already tracked and answers the "why" questions a tracker leaves open.

How does Observa compare to AutoSleep?

The two apps do different jobs, and each covers something the other doesn't.

What it does
Observa: Interprets Apple Health data: finds patterns and correlations and writes a plain-language weekly reflection.

AutoSleep: Automatically detects and scores sleep using Apple Watch sensors.
What you ask it
Observa: "What is consistently affecting my recovery?" "Why was this week different?" "What should I pay attention to?"

AutoSleep: "How long did I sleep?" "What was my sleep quality score?" "Did I hit my sleep target?"
Data handling
Observa: Fully on-device, no server, no account.

AutoSleep: Has a good privacy reputation; check its current policies.
Best used
Observa: Weekly, as a reflection layer on top of whatever's in Apple Health.

AutoSleep: Nightly, passively, in the background.

Can you use Observa and AutoSleep together?

The most useful setup is running both. AutoSleep captures sleep, and Observa correlates it with HRV, workouts, activity, and heart rate to explain patterns your tracker alone won't show. Sleep data flows through Apple Health, so neither app needs to talk to the other directly.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to AutoSleep?
Observa is a different kind of app. It does not track anything; it reads whatever Apple Health already collects (including AutoSleep's own data if you use both) and explains in plain language what keeps affecting your sleep, energy, and recovery. It works best alongside AutoSleep, as a companion more than a replacement.
Does Observa track sleep directly?
Observa reads sleep data from Apple Health, so it works with whichever tracker collects the data (Apple Watch, AutoSleep, third-party rings). It interprets the data and leaves the collecting to those trackers.
Is Observa private?
Yes. Observa runs entirely on-device. It has no server, no account, and no third-party analytics, and your health data never leaves your iPhone.
Can I use Observa and AutoSleep together?
Yes. AutoSleep captures sleep data, and Observa reads it together with everything else in Apple Health (HRV, ECG, activity, workouts) to show patterns over time.
