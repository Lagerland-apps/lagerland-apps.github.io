How does Observa compare to AutoSleep?

The two apps do different jobs, and each covers something the other doesn't.

What it does
Observa: Reads Apple Health and gives you a daily Readiness score from sleep, HRV (SDNN), resting heart rate, and training load, plus a Sleep Score, a stress read, and a Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest call.

AutoSleep: Automatically detects and scores sleep using Apple Watch sensors.
What you ask it
Observa: "Should I train today?" "Why is my Readiness low?" "How much sleep do I owe against my goal?"

AutoSleep: "How long did I sleep?" "What was my sleep quality score?" "Did I hit my sleep target?"
Data handling
Observa: No account and no analytics SDKs; the analysis runs on your device. Privacy Manifest.

AutoSleep: Has a good privacy reputation; check its current policies.
Best used
Observa: Each morning, in the app, on a widget, from a complication on your watch face, or by asking Siri.

AutoSleep: Nightly, passively, in the background.

Can you use Observa and AutoSleep together?

Running both is the most useful setup. AutoSleep tracks your nights and saves them to Apple Health. Observa reads them there with your HRV, resting heart rate, and workouts, scores how recovered you are, and names what moved the score. Neither app needs to talk to the other directly.

When to keep AutoSleep on its own

  • Sleep is the only thing you track, and AutoSleep's own scores answer your questions
  • You don't train regularly and have no use for a daily training call

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to AutoSleep?
If you want to keep AutoSleep's tracking and add an explanation of what your nights mean for today, use Observa next to it. It reads the sleep in Apple Health, including what AutoSleep saves there, with HRV, resting heart rate, and training load, and gives you Readiness, a Sleep Score, and sleep debt against your own goal. "Why this score?" names the signals behind each number.
Does Observa track sleep directly?
No. Observa reads sleep from Apple Health, whichever device or app saved it, such as your Apple Watch or AutoSleep. It scores the night, shows your sleep stages, and tracks sleep debt against the goal you set.
Is Observa private?
Yes. Observa has no account and no analytics SDKs, and the app makes no network calls of its own. Your health data is processed and stored on your device and never uploaded.
Can I use Observa and AutoSleep together?
Yes. AutoSleep saves your sleep to Apple Health, and Observa reads it there alongside HRV, resting heart rate, workouts, and activity. You get AutoSleep's tracking and Observa's Readiness score, training call, and patterns.
