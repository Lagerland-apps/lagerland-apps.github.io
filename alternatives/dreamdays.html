How does Soon. compare to Dreamdays?

Both apps are competent countdowns with themes. They differ in what they add around the number.

Event-type planning (Plan Mode)
Soon.: Templates for Trip, Wedding, Birthday, Pregnancy, Graduation, and Move. Each task has a T-minus offset (T-30, T-14, T-3) and is scheduled backward from the date.

Dreamdays: General countdowns and count-ups, without event-type templates.
Destination weather
Soon.: Apple WeatherKit forecast at the destination as the date approaches (Premium). Coarse location only.

Dreamdays: No weather integration.
Widgets
Soon.: Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy in every iOS size: small, medium, large, ring, minimal, and timeline.

Dreamdays: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets supported.
Memory cycle
Soon.: Past events turn into memories automatically and come back on the Home Screen on each anniversary.

Dreamdays: Count-up events display elapsed time but don't cycle as anniversaries.
Localisation
Soon.: 39 languages including right-to-left (Arabic, Hebrew) and CJK (Japanese, Korean, Chinese).

Dreamdays: Localised, scope varies by version.
Privacy
Soon.: No ads, third-party SDKs, or account. Events stay on-device.

Dreamdays: Standard freemium model with analytics typical for the category.

Who should pick Soon. vs Dreamdays?

Pick Soon. if…
  • Your countdowns come with real planning, like trips, weddings, pregnancies, or moves
  • You want a checklist that schedules itself backward from the date
  • You want destination weather on the widget for trips
  • You want StandBy widgets as well as Home Screen and Lock Screen ones
  • You want past events to return automatically as anniversary memories
  • You want an app with no tracking, ads, or account
Stick with Dreamdays if…
  • You're happy with simple count-down / count-up events without planning support
  • You've built a Home Screen layout around Dreamdays' widget styles
  • You don't need destination weather or event-type checklists

FAQ

What is the best Dreamdays alternative for iPhone?
Soon. is the closest privacy-first alternative to Dreamdays for iPhone. Both apps turn a date into a count-down (or count-up) with photo backgrounds and theme options; Soon. adds event-type Plan Mode checklists, Apple WeatherKit destination forecasts, StandBy widgets, and 39 languages, and has no ads or tracking SDKs on any tier.
Does Soon. count up from a past date like Dreamdays?
Yes. Once an event's date has passed, it turns into a memory automatically: Soon. counts the days, weeks, or years since it happened and brings it back on every anniversary. The widgets that showed the countdown switch to a count-up after the date.
Does Soon. have photo backgrounds and themes like Dreamdays?
Yes. Every event in Soon. can have a full-bleed photo background of your choosing. The free tier has three themes (Classic, Soft, Midnight) and Premium has ten. Soon. reads photos only through Apple's system picker, so it sees only the ones you select.
Is Soon. free like Dreamdays?
