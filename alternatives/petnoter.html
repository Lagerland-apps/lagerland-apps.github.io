On-device scanning · Medical records · No account

A PetNoter alternative
built around the medical record.

PetNoter is a friendly all-in-one pet-care tracker for walks, meals, tasks, and memories alongside vaccinations, weight, and documents. It is free on iPhone and Android with an optional lifetime pass. Pawza overlaps on records, daily care, and the vet PDF, but its center is the medical record. Pawza reads your vet documents on your device, and with Pawza Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills them in for you to check. Everything stays on your devices with no account. If a pet diary with tasks is what you want, PetNoter does it well. If the medical record and privacy come first, look at Pawza.

How does Pawza compare to PetNoter?

Both have a vet PDF, a care log, and vaccination tracking. After that they differ.

Main focus
Pawza: The medical record (vaccinations, medications, weight, symptoms, visits), with a one-tap care log for food, water, walks, toilet trips, and mood.

PetNoter: All-in-one daily care (walks, meals, tasks, memories) plus records.
Getting records in
Pawza: Scan a document and Pawza reads it on your device; with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills it in. A records PDF with years of visits can be imported and split by date.

PetNoter: Manual entry, with document storage.
Privacy & storage
Pawza: On your device with optional sync through your own private iCloud; no account.

PetNoter: Account-based sync across iPhone and Android.
Vet PDF export
Pawza: Yes, the Vet Visit PDF (Pro), with what changed since the last visit and your own questions.

PetNoter: Yes, a PDF health report export.
Platforms
Pawza: One universal app for iPhone and iPad, with 8 free widgets, a Control Center button (iOS 18), and Siri. Apple-only.

PetNoter: iPhone and Android.

PetNoter: Free, with an optional lifetime pass.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best PetNoter alternative for iPhone?
Pawza, if the medical record matters more to you than task lists. Both track vaccinations and weight, log daily care, and export a vet PDF. Pawza also reads vet documents on your device (Apple Intelligence auto-fill needs Pro, iOS 26, and an eligible device) and keeps everything on your devices with no account. PetNoter runs on Android too and is stronger on tasks and memories.
Does Pawza export a vet PDF like PetNoter?
Yes. Both export a vet-visit PDF. In Pawza, the Vet Visit PDF (Pro) covers the range you pick, with what changed since the last visit, medications and diet, vaccinations, weight, symptoms, recent care, and your own questions, on Letter or A4 paper.
Can Pawza scan vet documents to fill in records?
Yes, and it's the main thing PetNoter doesn't do. Photograph a certificate, lab result, or prescription and Pawza reads the text on your device. With Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the fields; otherwise you complete them from the recognized text. You review everything before saving, and documents are not uploaded.
Is Pawza more private than PetNoter?
Pawza keeps records on your device with optional sync through your own iCloud, needs no account, reads documents on the device, and has no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking. For on-device, account-free privacy, Pawza is the more private design.
How much does Pawza cost compared to PetNoter?
PetNoter is free on iOS and Android with an optional lifetime pass. Pawza is free for one pet. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@pawza}/month or {P_YEARLY@pawza}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@pawza}, or {P_LIFETIME@pawza} once, with Family Sharing on every Pro plan.
