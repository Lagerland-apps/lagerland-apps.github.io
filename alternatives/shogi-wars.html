FAQ

What is the best Shogi Wars alternative for training?
Shogiful is the closest alternative for the player who wants to improve more than compete. Shogi Wars centers on live online matchmaking with kyu/dan ranking. Shogiful centers on getting better: YaneuraOu analyzes every game on-device, mistakes are explained in plain language, and training comes from your own weaknesses. It works fully offline with no account on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
Can I play Shogiful without reading kanji?
Yes, and it is one of Shogiful's core features. The Western piece set uses symbolic icons with movement indicators so you can read the board from your first game, and the app introduces the kanji characters gradually as you train.
Does Shogiful have online play like Shogi Wars?
No, on purpose. Shogiful is a single-player training app with 10 AI opponents across four tiers. For ranked human play, Shogi Wars is still the right tool; Shogiful helps you get better between those games.
How much does Shogiful cost compared to Shogi Wars?
Shogiful is free with all 10 opponents, 2 analyses per day, and 3 puzzles per day. Premium is {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month, {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} once for lifetime. Shogi Wars is free for limited daily games with a subscription for unlimited play and paid AI-assist features.
Does Shogiful analyze my games like Shogi Wars' AI features?
Yes, every game. YaneuraOu reviews every move on-device, and each significant mistake is sorted into a shogi-specific category (missed drop tactics, wrong promotions, castle weaknesses, missed tsume) and explained in plain language.
Can I use both Shogi Wars and Shogiful?
Yes, and that's the most common pattern for improving players. Shogi Wars gives you human opposition and an official rank; Shogiful gives you the analysis and training loop that turns those games into improvement.
