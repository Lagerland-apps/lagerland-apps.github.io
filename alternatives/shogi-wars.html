Offline · No account · Training

A Shogi Wars alternative
for the practice between ranked games.

Shogi Wars is where you compete: an online app approved by the Japan Shogi Association, with live matchmaking and kyu/dan ranks. Shogiful is where you practice. There is no clock and no rank at stake, so you can take as long as you like over a move, and afterwards YaneuraOu reviews the whole game on your device. Each mistake gets a card with the better move and a coach's tip, and any game you have as a KIF file can be imported and reviewed the same way. You can play with pieces that need no kanji, and you need no account.

How does Shogiful compare to Shogi Wars?

They cover different parts of a shogi player's life.

Core purpose
Shogiful: Single-player training. You play 40 engine opponents, review every game, and train the weaknesses the review finds.

Shogi Wars: Online competition, with live matchmaking against humans with official kyu/dan ranking recognized by the Japan Shogi Association.
Readable without kanji
Shogiful: Three piece styles: Traditional kanji, International (kanji with movement arrows), and Chess (chess-style pieces, no kanji). Moves show in Western (P-7f) or Japanese notation.

Shogi Wars: Kanji pieces, or English-lettered pieces in the English version. Beginner pieces show where each piece can move.
Analysis & coaching
Shogiful: YaneuraOu reviews every move on your device, and each mistake gets a card in one of 12 shogi mistake types. The free version reviews 2 games a day; Premium has no limit and adds Deep Lines.

Shogi Wars: Paid Kishin AI features. Kishin can play moves for you during a game, Kishin analysis reviews a finished game, and the Kishin Learning plan turns bad moves from your games into quizzes. Premium adds automatic graph analysis of ranked games.
Offline & privacy
Shogiful: No account. Play, analysis, and training run on your device. Buying Premium and Game Center need a connection. No ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

Shogi Wars: Online play needs a connection; games against the computer work offline. Account required. Shows ads, and its App Store privacy label lists account data used for analytics.
Pricing
Shogiful: Free, with daily limits on analysis and training and none on play. Premium {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/mo or {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/yr, each with a {TRIAL@shogiful}, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} once.

Shogi Wars: Free for 3 games a day. Premium $5.49/mo or Super Premium $8.49/mo for unlimited play; Kishin Learning $7.99/mo; Kishin items and analysis tickets sold separately (US App Store).
Platforms
Shogiful: iPhone, iPad, and a native Mac app.

Shogi Wars: iPhone, iPad, and Android.

FAQ

What is the best Shogi Wars alternative for training?
Shogiful suits the player who wants to improve more than compete. Shogi Wars centers on live online matchmaking with kyu/dan ranking. Shogiful centers on getting better: YaneuraOu reviews every game on your device, each mistake is explained on a card, and Today's Training builds each session around your top weakness. Play, analysis, and training work offline with no account, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. The two work well together: compete on Shogi Wars, improve in Shogiful.
Can I play Shogiful without reading kanji?
Yes. Choose Chess-style pieces with no kanji, or International pieces that keep the kanji and add arrows showing how each piece moves; Traditional pieces are there when you want them. Shogi Wars has kanji pieces, plus English-lettered pieces in its English version and beginner pieces that show where each piece can move. If the characters stopped you before, Shogiful lets you start without them and switch when you're ready.
Does Shogiful have online play like Shogi Wars?
No. Shogiful is a single-player training app with 40 opponents in eight tiers, from gentle beginners to master level, unlocked as you win. Games are untimed and nothing is ranked. For ranked play against people, Shogi Wars is still the right tool, and Shogiful helps you get better between those games.
How much does Shogiful cost compared to Shogi Wars?
Shogiful is free to download, and play is never limited: you get unlimited games against all 40 opponents, which unlock as you win, plus 2 game analyses, 3 training puzzles, and 2 coach hints a day, with no ads. Premium removes those limits for {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month or {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, each with a {TRIAL@shogiful}, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} once, and Family Sharing is included. On the US App Store, Shogi Wars is free for 3 games a day, Premium ($5.49/month) or Super Premium ($8.49/month) unlocks unlimited play, and Kishin AI features cost extra.
Does Shogiful analyze my games like Shogi Wars' AI features?
Shogiful analyzes every game you play in the app and any game you import as KIF. YaneuraOu reviews each move on your device and labels it from Best move to Blunder, and each mistake gets a card in one of 12 shogi mistake types, such as Drop Mistake, Promotion Mistake, Castle Weakness, and Missed Tsume. The free version reviews 2 games a day; Premium has no limit and adds Deep Lines, the engine's top three lines for any position. Shogi Wars' Kishin features are paid: Kishin can play moves for you during a game, Kishin analysis reviews finished games, and the Kishin Learning plan turns bad moves from your games into quizzes.
Can I use both Shogi Wars and Shogiful?
Yes, and they fit together well. Shogi Wars gives you human opposition and an official rank; Shogiful gives you the review and training that turn those games into practice. Any game you can save as a KIF file can be imported into Shogiful and reviewed like your own.
