FAQ

What is the best Jefit alternative for iPhone?
For lifters who want a fast log without a social network, an account, or ads, GymLogger X is a strong candidate. It has 1,500 exercises, 44 ready-made programs (6 free), a free Smart Program Creator, and a free Apple Watch app, and Pro adds plateau and fatigue signals from your own logs. Jefit pairs a large exercise database with community features and cloud sync behind an account. GymLogger X syncs through your own iCloud and offers Pro as a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
Does GymLogger X have a community like Jefit?
No, on purpose. Jefit's community, shared routines, and social feed are a real draw for people who want them. GymLogger X is for the lifter who wants a quiet, fast log with no account and no feed. If the community is why you use Jefit, keep it; if it's noise to you, GymLogger X is the quieter option.
Does GymLogger X show ads like Jefit's free tier?
No. GymLogger X shows no ads and carries no advertising SDKs, on the free tier or with Pro. Jefit's free tier is ad-supported, and a Jefit Elite subscription removes ads and unlocks features. GymLogger X's Pro adds all 44 programs, your full history, and Insights, as a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
Does GymLogger X sync without a Jefit-style account?
Yes. GymLogger X syncs through your own iCloud when you're signed in to iCloud. There is nothing to sign up for, and the app has no server of its own and carries no third-party analytics.
How much does GymLogger X cost compared to Jefit?
Logging is free. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@gymlogger-x} a month or {P_YEARLY@gymlogger-x} a year, each starting with a {TRIAL@gymlogger-x}, or {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} once. Jefit is free with ads, with Jefit Elite typically around $6.99 a month or $39.99 a year to remove ads and unlock features.
Does GymLogger X log on Apple Watch?
Yes, and the Watch app is free. You start the workout on the iPhone, then log each set's weight and reps, run the rest timer, and see live heart rate on the wrist. Sets you log on the watch go through the phone, so the Watch app doesn't run a workout on its own.
