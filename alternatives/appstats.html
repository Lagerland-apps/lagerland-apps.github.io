App Store Connect on iPhone · Monitoring-first · Zero tracking

Looking for an
AppStats alternative?

AppStats is a well-built read-only App Store Connect dashboard for the same audience AppMeta Pulse serves — indie iOS developers and small studios who want revenue, subscription, and download numbers on their phone without opening a browser. Both use Apple's official App Store Connect API. Both keep credentials on-device. Both put your numbers in Home Screen widgets. If AppStats' iPad app, free tier, or alerts are what you want, keep it. If you want roughly a third the price and a privacy label that declares zero data collection rather than "usage data and diagnostics," AppMeta Pulse is built for that.

How does AppMeta Pulse compare to AppStats?

Honest framing: AppStats is a serious app with a clear point of view, and on a few specific features — iPad, a free tier, alerts — it is currently the stronger product. The two apps trade off differently. Here is where the differences matter in practice.

Price
AppMeta Pulse: $0.99/month · $1.99/year · $2.99 lifetime. Free download with a demo on sample data; 7-day free trial.

AppStats: $2.99/month · $19.99/year · $49.99 lifetime. Free tier for one app with 7 days of data.

AppMeta Pulse is roughly 67% cheaper monthly and 94% cheaper at the lifetime tier. AppStats is the one you can use for free with a single app.
Privacy label — what each app declares to Apple
AppMeta Pulse: Privacy label declares zero data collection. No usage data, no diagnostics, no third-party SDKs.

AppStats: Privacy label declares usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, purchases, and coarse location, not linked to identity, per the listing; its description says usage analytics are optional.

Both read from App Store Connect with your own API key; the difference is in what the apps themselves do after fetching the data.
Notifications and widgets
AppMeta Pulse: No notifications. 12 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for proceeds, sales, MRR, trends, subscriptions, and reviews, per app or across the portfolio.

AppStats: Eight widgets for downloads, revenue, products, the portfolio, subscription sales, and goals, plus alerts and summaries you can turn on.

If alerts are how you actually check your numbers, AppStats wins this category. For widgets, both have them; Pulse has more, including Lock Screen ones.
Vocabulary and reporting
AppMeta Pulse: Uses Apple's own terms throughout — proceeds (after commission and VAT), sales (gross), units, active subscriptions, trial conversions — plus MRR from the Subscription report. Direct mapping to ASC's Sales and Trends + Subscription reports. Converts every amount into the display currency you pick, using the European Central Bank's monthly rates.

AppStats: Similar coverage, with breakdowns by product, country, and device. Shows revenue in EUR or USD.
Platforms
AppMeta Pulse: iPhone. The App Store also offers it on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro, where it runs as the iPhone app.

AppStats: iPhone, iPad, iPod touch, and Apple silicon Mac.

AppStats has an iPad version; Pulse doesn't.
Authentication and credentials
AppMeta Pulse: App Store Connect API key (.p8 + key ID) in system Keychain. No build, pricing, or metadata controls; its only writes are review replies you send and analytics report requests.

AppStats: Same model — API key on-device, and its listing asks for a key with Sales and Reports access.

Both apps are credential-equivalent: the key stays on your device. Pulse asks for an Admin key, because it also reads App Analytics and sends review replies.

Who should pick AppMeta Pulse vs AppStats?

Pick AppMeta Pulse if…
  • You want the cheapest credible App Store Connect dashboard on iPhone
  • You want a privacy label that declares zero data collection — not "minimal" or "anonymous"
  • You check your numbers on your own schedule, or in widgets, rather than via notifications
  • You ship multiple apps and want a calm portfolio view without push interruptions
  • You want to reply to App Store reviews from the same app
  • You prefer Apple's official vocabulary (proceeds, units, sales) over a third-party rebrand
Stick with AppStats if…
  • Alerts and summaries are how you actually check your business
  • You want an iPad app
  • You only track one app and AppStats' free tier is enough
  • You have months of AppStats history you don't want to migrate

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to AppStats for iPhone?
AppMeta Pulse is a monitoring-first App Store Connect dashboard built for the same indie iOS developer audience. Both apps use Apple's official App Store Connect API and store credentials on-device. The differences are price (AppMeta Pulse is $0.99/month vs AppStats' $2.99/month, and $2.99 lifetime vs $49.99 lifetime) and data collection (AppMeta Pulse declares zero collection on its privacy label; AppStats' privacy label declares usage data and diagnostics).
How is AppMeta Pulse different from AppStats?
The two apps overlap heavily on core function — dashboards for App Store Connect on iPhone. The substantive differences: (1) AppMeta Pulse is roughly a third the monthly price and about 94% cheaper at the lifetime tier; (2) AppMeta Pulse's App Store privacy label declares zero data collection, while AppStats' declares usage data and diagnostics; (3) AppStats has an iPad version, a free tier for one app with 7 days of data, and alerts and summaries, which AppMeta Pulse does not match. Pick AppMeta Pulse for price and zero tracking; stay with AppStats if you need iPad, a free tier, or alerts.
Does AppMeta Pulse have notifications or widgets like AppStats?
Widgets, yes: AppMeta Pulse has 12 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for proceeds, sales, MRR, trends, subscriptions, and reviews; AppStats lists eight widgets. Notifications, no: AppMeta Pulse sends none, while AppStats offers alerts and summaries you can turn on. If you want to be told about your numbers rather than look at them, AppStats is the better fit.
Do both apps use the official App Store Connect API?
Yes. Both use Apple's official App Store Connect API with a user-generated .p8 API key. Both store the key in the system Keychain. Neither scrapes the App Store Connect web dashboard. The difference is in what the apps do with the data after fetching it — AppMeta Pulse sends nothing to any third party; AppStats' privacy label declares usage data and diagnostics collection.
Is AppMeta Pulse cheaper than AppStats?
Yes, materially. Monthly: $0.99 vs $2.99 (about 67% cheaper). Yearly: $1.99 vs $19.99 (about 90% cheaper). Lifetime: $2.99 vs $49.99 (about 94% cheaper). The free options differ: AppStats has a free tier for one app with 7 days of data; AppMeta Pulse has a demo on sample data and a 7-day free trial. Check each app's current App Store listing for exact terms.
