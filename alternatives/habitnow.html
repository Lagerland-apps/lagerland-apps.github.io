Questions

FAQ

Is there a HabitNow app for iPhone?
HabitNow is Android-first on Google Play, and there's no first-party iPhone version. On Apple devices the closest calm equivalent is Taskful Day, a daily planner without streaks that runs natively on iPhone, iPad, and Mac with iCloud sync.
What is the best HabitNow alternative on iPhone?
Taskful Day. It does the same daily-routine job without streaks, guilt, or an account, and it has a native Mac app. In exchange, it leaves out HabitNow's heavier gamification and statistics on purpose and keeps the focus on a single day.
Does Taskful Day handle habits, tasks, and routines like HabitNow?
It's built around planning and finishing a realistic day, which covers daily habits and tasks together. It has no streak counters or elaborate stats, since the point is getting through today at a sensible pace.
Does Taskful Day require an account?
No. There's no account, tracking, or advertising. Data stays on the device and syncs through your own iCloud, so setup is simple when you migrate from Android.
Can I move my HabitNow data into Taskful Day?
There's no direct import. The practical way to migrate is to recreate your few daily anchors by hand. Taskful Day plans one realistic day and keeps no long histories, so this usually takes a few minutes.
How does Taskful Day's pricing compare to HabitNow?
Taskful Day is free, with optional Pro from {P_MONTHLY@taskful-day}/month and a one-time lifetime option, and the core flow stays free. HabitNow is free on Android with a one-time premium unlock.
