What is the best Bobby alternative for iPhone?
AllPaid is the closest privacy-first alternative for people who want to track bills as well as subscriptions. Bobby is a very good on-device subscription tracker with no bank login. AllPaid keeps that model and extends it to one-off bills and due dates, tracked alongside subscriptions, with a calendar, receipt attachments, widgets, and Siri.
How is AllPaid different from Bobby?
Bobby shows recurring subscriptions as a grid of logo cards. AllPaid tracks subscriptions, one-time bills, and due dates in one place, on a calendar and in widgets, and keeps receipts and contracts attached to each bill. If you only track subscriptions, Bobby's narrow focus works in its favor. If you also have rent, insurance, and irregular bills to keep up with, AllPaid handles those too.
Is AllPaid as private as Bobby?
Yes. Neither uses bank aggregation: there is no Plaid, no bank login, and nothing reads your transactions. AllPaid has no account, no analytics, and no ad SDKs. Data stays on-device with optional iCloud sync.
Does AllPaid have renewal reminders like Bobby?
Yes. AllPaid sends due-date and renewal notifications before a payment is due. Upcoming items also show on a calendar and in home-screen and lock-screen widgets.
