The math before the payment · On-device OCR · No account

A Venmo Split alternative
for the math, not the payment.

Venmo handles payments well in the US. The hard part is deciding how much each person owes when one ordered the $14 salad and another ordered the $48 steak. RightSplit does that math first: scan the receipt, assign the items, and the tip is distributed fairly. Then everyone pays however they want, whether that's Venmo, Apple Pay, Wise, or cash, without being tied to one service.

How does RightSplit compare to Venmo's split feature?

They do different jobs. Venmo moves money; RightSplit decides how much each person owes before the money moves.

What it does
RightSplit: Calculates fair, item-level totals from a scanned receipt, including proportional tip.

Venmo Split: Divides one number equally between selected friends and sends payment requests.
Item-level fairness
RightSplit: Yes. Each person pays for what they ordered, including their share of any shared dishes.

Venmo Split: Equal-split only. The $14-salad eater pays the same as the $48-steak eater.
Geography
RightSplit: Works anywhere iPhones work; reads receipts in 16 languages on-device.

Venmo: US-only.
Does the group need accounts?
RightSplit: No. Share the total as plain text to anyone.

Venmo: Yes, every recipient needs a Venmo account.

Use them together?

Yes. For many US users the intended workflow is to scan the receipt with RightSplit, get the fair per-person totals, then send Venmo requests for those amounts. The payment habit stays the same and the numbers behind it are fairer.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Venmo Split?

Pick RightSplit (and still use Venmo) if…
  • Your group's bills aren't fair to split equally
  • You want documented OCR + proportional tip math
  • Some people at the table aren't on Venmo (outside US, or Apple Pay-only)
  • You want the math to stay private, without a shared payment app

  • You don't want a separate app for the math step
  • The amounts are small enough that fairness doesn't bother you

Questions

FAQ

Is RightSplit a Venmo alternative?
Not directly. Venmo is a payment app and RightSplit is a bill-splitting app, and the division is on purpose: one app does the math (item-level fairness, proportional tip, fair rounding) and another does the payment. After RightSplit calculates each person's total, you send Venmo requests, Apple Pay, Wise transfers, or whatever payment method your group uses.
Does RightSplit integrate with Venmo?
Only lightly, because RightSplit never moves money. It sends each person's total via iMessage, WhatsApp, or copy-paste as a short line like 'Hey Mike! Your share of the bill at Luigi's is $23.40.' If you save your own Venmo handle (or PayPal.me, Revolut or Wise), the pay button next to each person opens that service with their amount filled in where the service allows it. From there, each person pays through whichever app they prefer. RightSplit is not locked into a single payment provider.
Why not use Venmo's split feature directly?
Venmo's split assumes equal division, the same amount for everyone. RightSplit covers the case Venmo doesn't: when someone ordered a $14 salad and someone else ordered a $48 steak, an equal split is unfair. RightSplit does the item-level math first, and you can still pay via Venmo afterwards if you like.
