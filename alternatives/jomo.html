When should I pick Jomo instead?

Pick Jomo if any of these matter more to you than a lock tied to movement:

  • You want one blocker across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Jomo covers all three; EarnLock is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app in Premium.
  • You prefer session- and schedule-based blocking with a strict mode over a single daily goal.
  • You like Jomo's in-app unlock actions, such as meditation, and don't want movement as the cost.
  • Jomo's design and onboarding click with you. You'll see this app every day, so that matters.

EarnLock is for people who want to unlock their apps with a walk, a workout, or calories burned. Apple Health keeps score, halfway earns an hour, and the first week only counts.

What is the best alternative to Jomo?
If you want movement to be the key, EarnLock is the Jomo alternative built around it. It locks the apps you pick until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. Halfway opens them for an hour, and the full goal opens them until midnight. Locking one app with a step goal is free for good, there is no account, and Premium is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} purchase. If you need iPad and Mac, Jomo covers them.
How is EarnLock different from Jomo?
Jomo unlocks through sessions, schedules, a strict mode, and in-app actions. EarnLock unlocks when Apple Health shows you've moved enough that day, and a locked app shows what's left, such as "2,026 steps until unlock". EarnLock starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked. Jomo runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac; EarnLock is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app in Premium.
How much does EarnLock cost compared with Jomo?
Locking one app or category with a step goal is free for good, with partial and full unlocks, Shadow Week, streaks with a weekly rest day, the Insights Day view, every widget, and the emergency unlock. Premium adds unlimited apps, active minutes and active calories, the Apple Watch app, a Live Activity, Smart Schedules, and Week and Month insights for {P_MONTHLY@earnlock}/month or {P_YEARLY@earnlock}/year, each with a {TRIAL@earnlock}, or {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} once. Does Jomo work on Mac and iPad?
Yes. Jomo runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, which is a real advantage if you want one blocker everywhere. EarnLock is an iPhone app, with an Apple Watch app in Premium, and has no iPad or Mac version.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, makes no network connections of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Apple Health is read on your iPhone and never written to or sent anywhere, and Apple's Screen Time picker gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. EarnLock is built for limiting your own iPhone and has no parent controls.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Jomo can?
On iPhone, for apps, yes. EarnLock locks any app or whole category you can choose in Apple's Screen Time picker: one on the free tier, as many as you like with Premium. They differ in how each app decides when to unlock.
