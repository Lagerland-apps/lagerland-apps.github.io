When should I pick Jomo instead?

Pick Jomo if any of these matter more to you than a movement-gated block:

  • You want one blocker across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Jomo spans all three; EarnLock is iOS + Apple Watch only.
  • You prefer session- and schedule-based blocking with a strict mode over a single daily activity gate.
  • You like Jomo's in-app unlock actions (meditation and similar) and don't want movement as the cost.
  • Jomo's design and onboarding click with you. It is a polished app, and that matters for a tool you'll see daily.

EarnLock is for people who want to unlock their apps with a walk, a workout, or calories burned. Apple's Family Controls enforces the block, Apple Health measures the activity, the count shows on the wrist, and the lifetime price is a fraction of Jomo's.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Jomo?
EarnLock is a movement-based, privacy-first alternative to Jomo. Jomo blocks apps with scheduled sessions, a strict mode, and active actions to unlock. EarnLock shields your chosen apps through Apple's Family Controls until you hit a real daily activity goal (steps, active minutes, or active energy) measured by Apple Health, with a live progress ring on every blocked app. It costs {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime (one-time), against Jomo's roughly $99.99 lifetime, and has no account, analytics SDK, or server.
How is EarnLock different from Jomo?
Jomo works with sessions and schedules, a strict mode, and unlock actions like meditation. EarnLock unlocks on movement: the app stays shielded until you hit a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health, and the shield shows a live progress ring counting down to unlock. EarnLock has a full Apple Watch app. Jomo covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only. EarnLock requires no account and has no tracking SDKs.
Is EarnLock cheaper than Jomo?
Yes. EarnLock is {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} lifetime (one-time). Jomo is typically a subscription, around $5.99/month or $29.99/year, with a one-time option near $99.99. EarnLock's lifetime tier costs roughly a fifth of Jomo's lifetime price, and both apps have ways to start without paying. Check current prices before switching.
Does Jomo work on Mac and iPad?
Yes. Jomo runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, which helps if you want one blocker across all your Apple devices. EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only, because it depends on Apple's Family Controls and HealthKit on the iPhone and Watch. If cross-device coverage matters more to you than a movement gate, Jomo is the better fit.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, server, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It reads HealthKit data on-device only and never sends it off the device. Apple's App Privacy nutrition label shows Data Not Collected for every release. EarnLock is for restricting yourself and is not a parental control app.
Can EarnLock block the same apps Jomo can?
Yes. Both apps build on Apple's Family Controls framework, so you pick the apps to block in the same iOS picker. Any app or category Jomo can block, EarnLock can shield. EarnLock keeps it locked until you move and shows the count on your Apple Watch.
