How does Chessful compare to Chessis?

Both apps run Stockfish on-device, explain moves in plain language, and sell a one-time Pro purchase. They differ in which platforms come first and how much they do beyond analysis.

Analysis layer
Chessful: Stockfish on-device, plus motif detectors and an on-device language model that write plain-language sentences sorted into 30+ mistake categories.

Chessis: Stockfish on-device, plus a game report naming blunders, missed wins, and best moves. The core is equivalent, and Chessis's version is mature.
Adaptive training
Chessful: Yes. A spaced-repetition training queue is generated automatically from the motifs found in your own losses, with trend graphs for each skill.

Chessis: Not in current scope. The app focuses on the analysis flow.
In-app opponents
Chessful: 40 distinct AI opponents, each with its own opening repertoire and structural preferences.

Chessis: Made for analysing games played elsewhere (PGN, Chess.com and Lichess imports). There is no play mode.
Platform priority
Chessful: iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Universal Purchase). No Android.

Chessis: Android first, limited iOS.
PGN / Chess.com / Lichess import
Chessful: No PGN, Chess.com, or Lichess import.

Chessis: A core feature today.

FAQ

What is the best Chessis alternative for iPhone?
Chessful is the closest match to Chessis on Apple devices. Both run Stockfish on-device, turn engine output into plain-language explanations of your mistakes, and have a one-time-purchase Premium option. Chessful currently runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Universal Purchase), while Chessis is currently Android-primary. Chessful also adds an adaptive training queue and 40 AI opponents.
How is Chessful's analysis different from Chessis?
The analysis itself is very similar. Both apps run Stockfish locally and produce a game report that names blunders and missed wins in plain language. The bigger practical difference is what Chessful does next: a per-motif skill-bucket system feeds an adaptive training queue (with spaced repetition), so the same mistake pattern keeps coming back in drills until you stop making it.
Does Chessful support PGN import like Chessis?
No. Chessis is built around PGN import as a core feature; Chessful doesn't import PGN files. Play your games inside Chessful (against the 40 AI opponents) and the on-device analyser processes them automatically.
Why use Chessful instead of Chessis on iPhone?
The strongest reason is platform: Chessful is made for Apple devices first, with iPhone, iPad, and Mac in one Universal Purchase. It also adds an adaptive training queue with spaced repetition on the patterns from your own games, and it has 40 distinct AI opponents to play against.
