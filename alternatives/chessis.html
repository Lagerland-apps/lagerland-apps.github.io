A Chessis alternative

How does Chessful compare to Chessis?

Both run Stockfish on the device, explain moves in plain language, import PGN, and sell a one-time purchase. They differ in which platforms come first and how much they do beyond analysis.

Analysis
Chessful: On-device Stockfish rates every move from Brilliant to Blunder. Each mistake shows what you played, what was best, why, and the refutation, tagged with one of 10 mistake types.

Chessis: On-device Stockfish with a game report naming blunders, missed wins, and best moves. The core is equivalent, and Chessis's version is mature.
Training from your games
Chessful: Today's Training builds a session from your mistakes, and Review past mistakes brings misses back with spaced repetition.

Chessis: Not in current scope. The app focuses on the analysis flow.
Play and learn
Chessful: 40 opponents from 400 to 2400 Elo (first 10 free), 156 lessons, 15,000 puzzles, and 96 endgames.

Chessis: Made for analysing games played elsewhere. There is no play mode.
Platform priority
Chessful: iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Apple silicon), one Universal Purchase. No Android.

Chessis: Android first, limited iOS.
PGN import
Chessful: PGN files or pasted text, up to 500 games per import, including exports from Chess.com and Lichess.

Chessis: A core feature, with Chess.com and Lichess imports.
Pricing
Chessful: Free with daily limits; Premium {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/mo, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} lifetime.

Chessis: Free tier; one-time Pro purchase.

FAQ

What is the best Chessis alternative for iPhone?
Chessful is the closest match to Chessis on Apple devices. Both run Stockfish on the device, explain your mistakes in plain language, and import PGN files. Chessful runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac with one Universal Purchase, while Chessis puts Android first. Chessful also builds daily training from your mistakes and includes 156 lessons, 15,000 puzzles, 96 endgames, and 40 opponents to play.
How is Chessful's analysis different from Chessis?
The analysis is similar: both run Stockfish locally and report your mistakes in sentences. Chessful's mistake card shows what you played, what was best, why, and the line that punishes your move, worded for your coaching level and tagged with one of 10 mistake types. The bigger difference is what comes next. Chessful turns those mistakes into the puzzles in your daily training, and spaced repetition brings back the ones you miss.
Does Chessful support PGN import like Chessis?
Yes. Open a PGN file from Files, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop, or paste PGN text. One import takes up to 500 games, flags duplicates, and lets you pick which side you played. That covers games exported from Chess.com or Lichess. Chessful doesn't fetch games by username.
Why use Chessful instead of Chessis on iPhone?
Chessful is made for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with one Universal Purchase for all three, while Chessis prioritises Android. It adds daily training built from your own mistakes, 156 free lessons for learning the game, 15,000 puzzles in 53 themes, 96 endgames against Stockfish, and 40 opponents from 400 to 2400 Elo to play in the app.
Are both apps a one-time purchase?
Both offer one. Chessful has a {P_LIFETIME@chessful} lifetime option alongside {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/month and {P_YEARLY@chessful}/year subscriptions, each with a {TRIAL@chessful}, and a free version with daily limits. Chessis sells a one-time Pro purchase (the price varies by region).
