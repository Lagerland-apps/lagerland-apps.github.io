How does Soon. compare to Wedding Happy?

Soon. and Wedding Happy handle different parts of wedding planning, so one doesn't fully replace the other.

The countdown experience
Soon.: The countdown is the centrepiece, with a full-bleed photo of the venue, live destination weather as the date approaches, and Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy widgets in every size.

Wedding Happy: The countdown is a header above the planning checklist.
Plan Mode template (90-day wedding)
Soon.: A built-in 12-week template: T-90 vendor confirmation, T-60 save-the-dates, T-45 final dress / suit fitting, T-30 wedding band rehearsal, T-21 confirm headcount, T-14 pack ceremony kit, T-7 venue walkthrough, T-1 day-of timeline.

Wedding Happy: Generic checklist categories you populate yourself.
Guest list, RSVPs, seating
Soon.: Not covered. Soon. is a countdown and checklist and doesn't manage guest lists.

Wedding Happy: Built-in support for guests, RSVPs, and table assignments, and it does this well.
Ads & account
Soon.: No ads on any tier, and no account required.

Wedding Happy: Free with banners on the free tier; account required for cloud sync.
Notification rhythm
Soon.: One notification per Plan Mode task on its scheduled day (Premium), never a daily nag.

Wedding Happy: Reminders triggered from checklist items you enter by hand.
Long-term use
Soon.: After the date, Soon. automatically turns the wedding into an anniversary countdown every year.

Wedding Happy: Wedding-day focused; you typically uninstall after the day.

Which one to pick, or whether to run both

Pick Soon. if you want…
  • The countdown as the centrepiece of your Home Screen for 12 weeks
  • A Plan Mode template that gives you a ready-made 90-day prep schedule
  • Destination weather, especially for outdoor or destination weddings
  • No banner ads between checklist items
  • An app that keeps the wedding alive as an anniversary countdown every year
Stick with Wedding Happy if you need…
  • Guest list management, RSVPs, and seating charts (Soon. doesn't do these)
  • A two-person planning workflow with explicit shared access (Soon. is on-device only)
  • Wedding-specific vendor contracts and budget tracking in one place

Many couples run both, using Wedding Happy for the guest list and Soon. for the countdown widget and the planning schedule.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Wedding Happy alternative for iPhone?
Soon. is a privacy-first iPhone alternative to Wedding Happy. It ships a 12-week Wedding Plan Mode template anchored backward from the wedding date (T-90 vendor confirmation through T-1 day-of timeline), and pairs it with a full-bleed photo background of the venue, destination weather, and Home Screen / Lock Screen / StandBy widgets. It has no ads, no account, and no server-side guest list.
Does Soon. handle wedding guest lists and RSVPs?
No. Soon. is a wedding countdown with a built-in vendor and prep checklist, and it doesn't manage RSVPs, guest lists, seating charts, or vendor contracts. If you need those, keep Wedding Happy (or a dedicated guest-list app) for them, and run Soon. alongside it for the countdown and the week-by-week planning schedule.
Why use Soon. for a wedding instead of Wedding Happy?
The countdown is the centrepiece, with a full-bleed photo of the venue on your Home Screen for 90 days, which a list-driven app doesn't do. Plan Mode tasks are scheduled backward from the date, and each one fires its own notification on its scheduled day instead of arriving in a weekly digest. And there's no account and no ads: wedding planning is emotional enough without a banner ad between 'final dress fitting' and 'walk-through with photographer.'
Can two people share a Soon. wedding countdown?
Not directly, because Soon. stores events only on the device. For couples, the workable approach is for each partner to install Soon. and create the wedding event, and the Plan Mode template gives both the same set of tasks. Shared events through opt-in iCloud are on the long-term roadmap.
