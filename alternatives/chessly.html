How does Chessful compare to Chessly?

They cover different parts of getting better at chess.

Training source
Chessful: Training queue generated from the mistakes in your own games, classified into about thirty motifs, drilled with spaced repetition.

Chessly: Structured opening-repertoire courses by Hikaru Nakamura and other named coaches, taught as a curriculum.
Celebrity content
Chessful: None. It has no named Grandmaster content.

Chessly: Nakamura's name leads the marketing, and strong named coaches present the video courses.
Scope
Chessful: Tactics, defense, positional play, openings, and endgames, all five skill dimensions drawn from your games.

Chessly: Openings primarily, with some middlegame and endgame coverage in specific courses.
Pricing
Chessful: Free tier; Premium {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/mo, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} lifetime (7-day free trial).

Chessly: Subscription course bundles, typically $99 to $199/year. Individual courses are also for sale.
Offline support
Chessful: Fully offline. Stockfish, the motif detectors, and the training engine all run on the device.

Chessly: Video courses stream, or download for offline use. It is account-based.
Account requirement
Chessful: No account at any point.

Chessly: Account required for course progress and subscription management.

Which one to pick

  • Training built on the patterns you actually miss

FAQ

What is the best Chessly alternative for iPhone?
Chessful is the closest alternative to Chessly for a player who wants training more than ranked play. Chessly sells opening-repertoire video courses by Hikaru Nakamura and other named coaches. Chessful builds an adaptive training queue from the specific mistake patterns in your own losses. They work well together: Chessly for the opening curriculum, Chessful for the pattern-recognition work once the openings are learned.
Does Chessful have opening courses by Hikaru Nakamura?
No. Chessful has no celebrity content. Its training engine detects the specific mistake patterns in your own games and drills the patterns you're actually missing.
How much does Chessful cost compared to Chessly?
Chessful's free tier has no time limit or ads. Premium is {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/month, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} once for lifetime access, with a 7-day free trial. Chessly sells subscription course bundles, typically $99 to $199/year.
Can I use both Chessly and Chessful?
Yes, and many improving players do. Chessly is an opening curriculum; Chessful covers what happens after move 12. Players who use both typically open Chessly for dedicated opening study and Chessful for the play-and-train cycle in between.
Does Chessful detect repertoire drift like Chessly's opening focus?
Yes. "Repertoire drift" is one of the thirty mistake motifs Chessful's classifier detects, and it fires when you leave your usual opening setup. Chessful tracks the repertoire you actually play from your games, while Chessly teaches you a recommended repertoire from scratch.
