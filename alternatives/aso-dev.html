App Store Connect + ASO · Subscription vs pay once

An ASO.dev alternative
if you mostly edit and rarely track.

ASO.dev is a big toolkit: an App Store Connect client, keyword rank tracking, competitor research, product page tests, Google Play support, and apps for Mac, iPhone, iPad, Windows, Linux and Android, priced per seat by subscription. AppMeta is smaller and Mac-only. It edits listings, in-app purchases, Game Center, TestFlight and reviews, has keyword difficulty checks but no rank tracking, and costs $17.99 a year or $29.99 once.

How does AppMeta compare to ASO.dev?

They overlap on the App Store Connect editing work. ASO.dev adds market data and team features; AppMeta adds Game Center and a one-time price.

Pricing
AppMeta: $17.99/year or $29.99 lifetime, restored on every Mac signed in with the same Apple ID.

ASO.dev: Per seat, excluding VAT. Console (no keyword tools) is $15/month billed yearly ($180/year). Indie, which adds keyword rankings, is $29/month billed yearly ($348/year). Monthly billing costs more. 3-day trial, no free tier.
Keyword data
AppMeta: On-device checks for wasted characters, duplicate stems and locale gaps. Live difficulty and opportunity estimates from App Store search results and Apple's search suggestions, plus popularity if you connect your own Apple Ads account. No ranking history.

ASO.dev: On Indie and up: rankings checked every 4 hours with 30 or 60 days of history, competitor keywords and a keyword explorer. Apple Ads insights are on the Marketing tier.
Platforms
AppMeta: Mac only, macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

ASO.dev: Mac (App Store or DMG), iPhone and iPad, Windows, Linux and Android. There's deliberately no web app.
App Store Connect coverage
AppMeta: 50 locales side by side, In-App Purchases and subscriptions, Game Center, TestFlight, reviews and submission.

ASO.dev: Metadata and bulk editors for 50 locales, In-App Purchases and subscriptions with purchasing-power pricing, TestFlight, reviews, certificates and profiles, product page tests and Google Play. Game Center isn't listed.
AI and translation
AppMeta: Your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, a local model through Ollama or LM Studio, or Apple Intelligence on-device on macOS 26.

ASO.dev: Bulk translation through Google Translate by default, or DeepL, ChatGPT or Claude with your own keys. AI review replies, and a local MCP server for AI assistants.
Teams
AppMeta: Built for one developer. No seats or permissions.

ASO.dev: Shared workspaces with read and write access set per section.

Who should pick AppMeta and who should pick ASO.dev?

Pick AppMeta if…

Questions

FAQ

What is a cheaper alternative to ASO.dev?
AppMeta costs $17.99/year or $29.99 lifetime for one person, against ASO.dev's per-seat subscriptions starting at $180/year for the Console tier. You get App Store Connect editing across 50 locales, In-App Purchases, Game Center, TestFlight and reviews. You don't get ASO.dev's keyword rank tracking, team seats or Google Play support.
How much does ASO.dev cost?
On 25 October 2026 ASO.dev's pricing page listed, per seat and excluding VAT: Console $15/month billed yearly ($180/year), Indie $29/month billed yearly ($348/year), Pro $69/month billed yearly ($828/year) and Marketing $149/month billed yearly. Paying month to month costs more. There's a 3-day trial and no free tier, so check current pricing before you buy.
Does AppMeta track keyword rankings like ASO.dev?
No. AppMeta checks your own metadata on-device (wasted characters, duplicate stems, locale gaps) and estimates difficulty and opportunity from live App Store search results and Apple's search suggestions. It doesn't store ranking history or track competitors' keywords. If you need rank tracking, ASO.dev's Indie tier or a dedicated ASO platform fits better.
Is ASO.dev available on Windows or iPhone?
Yes. ASO.dev offers a Mac app (App Store or direct download), an iPhone and iPad app, Windows and Linux builds and an Android app. It has no web version by design. AppMeta runs only on macOS 14 or later.
Where do ASO.dev and AppMeta keep my App Store Connect key?
Both keep it on your device. ASO.dev says keys are stored locally and requests go straight from your device to Apple, with optional encrypted key sync for teams. AppMeta stores the key in the Mac Keychain as a this-device-only item that never syncs, and there's no Lagerland server in between.
Does ASO.dev support Game Center?
Game Center isn't listed on ASO.dev's pricing or feature pages as of October 2026. AppMeta creates and edits Game Center achievements, leaderboards and leaderboard sets with the same per-locale workflow it uses for your listing.
