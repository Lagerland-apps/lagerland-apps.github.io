How does RightSplit compare to Tab?

The workflow is the same. RightSplit documents more of its math.

OCR transparency
RightSplit: Apple's on-device Vision framework (VNRecognizeTextRequest) in 16 receipt languages. The image never leaves the phone.

Tab: Photo-tap workflow; OCR engine not publicly documented.
Tip math
RightSplit: Tip is split in proportion to each person's share of the subtotal, so the $14-salad eater pays less tip than the $48-steak eater. The rounding rules are published.

Tab: Standard tax + tip calculation; per-person tip distribution not publicly documented.

Tab: Free app on the App Store.
Privacy

Tab: Standard category analytics likely.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Tab?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • You want to know exactly which OCR engine reads your receipt
