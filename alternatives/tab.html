How does RightSplit compare to Tab?

Both apps turn a photo of the receipt into items you tap onto people. The differences are in what happens after the split, and in what you pay.

Reading the receipt
RightSplit: Apple Intelligence reads it on an eligible iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later); every other iPhone uses an on-device scanner. It reads receipts in 16 languages, the photo stays on the phone, and every line is editable.

Tab: Photo-and-tap workflow; how the receipt is read is not publicly documented.
Getting paid back
RightSplit: One message per person with their amount and a tap-to-pay link for Venmo, PayPal.me, Revolut, Wise, IBAN, or Apple Pay. With Pro+, a Live Activity tracks who has paid, and a reminder comes a day later if someone hasn't.

Tab: Built around working out each person's total at the table.
Pricing
RightSplit: Equal splits and your first scan are free. Pro+ is {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/yr or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once, and adds unlimited scans and item splits.

Tab: Free app on the App Store, item splitting included.
Privacy
RightSplit: No account, no ads, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. Receipts, amounts, and names stay on your iPhone. See the transparency entry.

Tab: See its App Store privacy label.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Tab?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • You end up chasing people for their share after the dinner
  • Your friends pay with different services, from Venmo and PayPal to Revolut, Wise, or a bank transfer
  • You want the receipt read on your iPhone, with every line editable before you assign it
  • You travel and split in other currencies: 58 of them, with receipts read in 16 languages
  • You want a split on your Home Screen: Pocket Split is a free widget that does the math without opening the app
Stick with Tab if…
  • You want item splitting free, with no paid tier at all
  • You only need the totals and collect the money yourself
  • You're used to Tab's tap-to-claim screens and they work for your group
