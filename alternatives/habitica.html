How do Taskful Day and Habitica compare?

Core mechanic
Taskful Day: Plan the day and finish what you can. Unfinished tasks carry forward to today the next time you open the app, and a recurring task that slips keeps its original rhythm.

Habitica: An RPG layer with XP, gold, quests, and avatar damage for missed dailies.

Taskful Day: Analytics shows completion rate, trends, categories, and a current and best streak, with the footer "This is just a pattern, not a judgment." The Journal records how each day felt.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Habitica alternative without gamification?
Taskful Day suits people who want to plan the day without a game around it. Habitica gives your avatar damage when you miss a daily and XP for completions. Taskful Day has no avatars, XP, or parties: you plan the day, finish what you can, and unfinished tasks move to today the next time you open the app. It needs no account and runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.
Why would I switch from Habitica to Taskful Day?
For the people it works for, Habitica's game is the whole point. For others the RPG layer turns into a second job: managing a party, avoiding damage, and keeping the game alive while the habit slips. Taskful Day keeps the day itself in front of you, with type-a-sentence capture, recurring tasks, and reminders you can snooze. If the game stopped motivating you, that is the reason to switch.
Does Taskful Day have streaks or penalties like Habitica?
It has streaks, but no penalties. Analytics shows a current and best streak as one stat beside your completion rate and trends, and Pro adds a Journal Streak complication for Apple Watch. The streak never appears on the Today list, and a missed task simply moves to today the next time you open the app, with no overdue badge. There is no avatar damage or other penalty for a missed day.
Does Taskful Day require an account like Habitica?
No. Habitica is an account-based social platform, and your character and party live on its servers. Taskful Day works without an account and carries no ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs. Your tasks stay on the device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, a Pro feature that uses your own private iCloud.
Is Taskful Day available on Mac like Habitica?
Yes. Taskful Day runs on Mac with macOS 14 or later, as well as on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, and it is free to download on each. With Pro, iCloud Sync keeps your devices on the same plan. Habitica is cross-platform through the web and mobile apps tied to its account.
How does Taskful Day's pricing compare to Habitica?
Taskful Day is free to download and lets you create up to 50 tasks in total for free. Pro costs {P_MONTHLY@taskful-day} a month, {P_YEARLY@taskful-day} a year, or {P_LIFETIME@taskful-day} once, and adds unlimited tasks, iCloud Sync, the visual day views, and 21 Home Screen widgets. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro free with no card. Habitica is free with an optional subscription of around $5 a month that supports the service.
