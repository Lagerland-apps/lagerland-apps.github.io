How does LiftLog compare to Heavyset?

The approach is similar. Pricing and program depth differ.

Pricing
LiftLog: Free 7-day trial (no card), then a one-time, pay-what-you-can price from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}.

Heavyset: Subscription for full features.
Programs & progression
LiftLog: 40 ready programs (StrongLifts, Starting Strength, PPL, Upper/Lower, 5/3/1 with Training Max) and per-exercise progression (double, linear, % of Training Max or RPE), with a deload after repeated misses.

Heavyset: Routine builder, minimal program templates.
Analytics
LiftLog: Estimated 1RM (Adaptive Brzycki/Epley, or either alone), plus actual best 1/3/5/10-rep PRs, best volume, best set, 26-week heatmap, 30-day deltas.

Heavyset: Volume and rep tracking.
Privacy / account
LiftLog: No account, no third-party tracking, data stays on the device with JSON and CSV export.

Heavyset: Account / sync model varies by version.

Who should pick LiftLog vs Heavyset?

Pick LiftLog if…
  • You like Heavyset's minimal-numbers philosophy but don't want to subscribe
  • You want ready program templates whose weights progress and deload on their own
  • You want a published 1RM formula (Brzycki/Epley) and Rep PRs at 1/3/5/10 reps on top of rep counts
  • You want a haptic confirmation for every set you log
  • You'd rather pay once and keep your training history for good
Stick with Heavyset if…
  • You have years of Heavyset history you don't want to migrate
  • You're already happy with the subscription
  • You specifically prefer Heavyset's UI conventions
  • You don't need automatic progression and a clean routine builder is enough

FAQ

What is the best Heavyset alternative for iPhone?
LiftLog is a design-led iPhone alternative to Heavyset for lifters who want a minimal strength log without a recurring subscription. It ships 40 ready programs (StrongLifts 5×5, PPL, Upper/Lower, 5/3/1 and more) whose weights progress automatically, with a deload after repeated misses, tracks estimated 1RM and Rep PRs at 1/3/5/10 reps. After a free 7-day trial it is a one-time, pay-what-you-can purchase from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}, with no card and no account needed.
Is LiftLog a one-time purchase?
Yes. After a free 7-day trial with no card required, you pick a one-time price between {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} and {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}: the Fair, Good, Generous, or Patron tier. There is no recurring charge and no Pro tier locked behind a subscription.
Does LiftLog support periodization and deloads?
Progression and deloads, yes; periodization, only partly. Each exercise follows double progression, linear, percent of Training Max (including the four-week 5/3/1 wave with its deload week) or RPE, and drops to 90% after three missed sessions in a row. LiftLog does not schedule undulating or block periodization for you. The 40 bundled programs include StrongLifts 5×5, Starting Strength, PPL, Upper/Lower, full-body and body-part splits, and 5/3/1 with Training Max.
