How does LiftLog compare to Heavyset?

Both keep logging simple. Pricing and program depth differ.

Pricing
LiftLog: Free for 7 days with no card, then one payment at the tier you choose, from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}. Every tier unlocks the same app, and Family Sharing is on.

Heavyset: Subscription for full features.
Programs and progression
LiftLog: 40 ready-made programs (20 for strength, 17 for hypertrophy, 3 general) based on routines such as StrongLifts 5×5, Starting Strength, Push/Pull/Legs, Upper / Lower, and 5/3/1. Program exercises can follow Double progression, Linear, % of training max, or RPE / RIR, and a Progression review follows each workout.

Heavyset: Routine builder, minimal program templates.
Analytics
LiftLog: Estimated 1RM with Adaptive, Brzycki, or Epley; Personal Bests (Est. 1RM, Best Set, Best Volume) and Rep PRs; Estimated 1RM, Volume, and Avg RPE charts per exercise; an activity heatmap, Muscle Coverage, and the last 30 days against the 30 before.

Heavyset: Volume and rep tracking.
Privacy and account
LiftLog: No account, analytics, or ads. Workouts stay on your iPhone, every feature works offline, and you can export as JSON or CSV. See the Privacy Manifest.

Heavyset: Account / sync model varies by version.

Who should pick LiftLog vs Heavyset?

  • You like Heavyset's minimal approach but don't want to subscribe

FAQ

What is the best Heavyset alternative for iPhone?
If you like Heavyset's minimal logging but not the recurring subscription, LiftLog keeps that focus and adds ready-made programs. Its 40 programs are based on well-known routines such as StrongLifts 5×5, Starting Strength, Push/Pull/Legs, Upper / Lower, and 5/3/1, and they can suggest the weight for your next set. It tracks estimated 1RM with the Adaptive, Brzycki, or Epley formula, plus Personal Bests and Rep PRs for every exercise. It is free for 7 days with no card, then a one-time purchase from {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}, with no account.
Is LiftLog a one-time purchase?
Yes. LiftLog is free for 7 days with no card required. After that you buy it once at the tier you choose: {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} (Fair), {P_IAP:Suggested@liftlog} (Good), {P_IAP:Supporter@liftlog} (Generous), or {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog} (Patron). Every tier unlocks the same app, nothing renews, and Family Sharing is on.
Does LiftLog support progression and deloads?
Yes, through progression schemes on program exercises: Double progression, Linear, % of training max, or RPE / RIR. LiftLog fills each pending set with the weight the scheme prescribes. After the workout, a Progression review marks each exercise as Progressed, Hold, Missed, or Deload. RPE is added by editing a set after the workout, so the RPE scheme only works when you enter it.
