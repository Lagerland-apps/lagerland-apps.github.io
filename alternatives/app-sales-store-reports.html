App Store Connect on iPhone · Privacy label matters

Looking for an
App Sales alternative?

App Sales - Store Reports is a capable iPhone dashboard for App Store Connect, with a 4.9 average rating across 41 reviews. Multi-account API support, country-level breakdowns, and daily and monthly charts all work as advertised. Indie developers who look for an alternative usually do so because of the privacy label: App Sales declares that the app collects purchases, identifiers, and usage data, and shares some of it with third-party advertising networks. If that is acceptable for your use, stay with App Sales. If you want a dashboard that declares zero data collection, which you can check on the App Store listing, that is what AppMeta Pulse does.

How does AppMeta Pulse compare to App Sales - Store Reports?

App Sales is the better-rated app today and runs on iPad, which AppMeta Pulse does not. Most people decide on something else: the App Store privacy label, the field on each app's listing that says, in Apple's own categories, what data the app collects and how it is used.

What each app declares on its App Store privacy label
AppMeta Pulse: Privacy label declares zero data collection. It lists nothing under "Data Used to Track You," "Data Linked to You," or "Data Not Linked to You."

App Sales - Store Reports: Privacy label declares "Data Used to Track You" (purchases, identifiers, usage data), with some of that data shared for third-party advertising, analytics, and app functionality.

Both apps read from App Store Connect with your own API key. They differ in what each app does with the data once it has fetched it.
App Store ratings
AppMeta Pulse: Newer release, without enough ratings to show an average.

App Sales: Approximately 41 ratings averaging 4.9, from a user base that has been around longer.

App Sales leads here on volume today, and AppMeta Pulse does not claim to match it.
Feature coverage
AppMeta Pulse: Proceeds or sales, MRR, active subscriptions, trial conversions, downloads, top territories per app, per-app portfolio view, period-over-period trends, 12 widgets, and review replies, in Apple's native vocabulary (proceeds vs sales, units).

App Sales: Revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions, with advertised country-level insights and multi-account API key support. Daily and monthly charts, with Pro features split across tiers.
Pricing structure
AppMeta Pulse: One tier: {P_MONTHLY@appmeta-pulse}/month, {P_YEARLY@appmeta-pulse}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@appmeta-pulse} lifetime.

App Sales: Five tiers: Starter ($2.99), Growth ($4.99), Scale ($7.99), Enterprise ($12.99), Pro ($39.99).

AppMeta Pulse is cheaper for the simple case, and there is no tier to pick. App Sales' ladder may sell the one feature you want at a price you prefer.
Authentication and credentials
AppMeta Pulse: App Store Connect API key (.p8 + key ID) stored in system Keychain. No build, pricing, or metadata controls.

App Sales: Same model: API key on-device, read-only against ASC. Multi-account API key support is explicitly advertised.

Both let you add several App Store Connect accounts and switch between them in the app.
Platforms
AppMeta Pulse: iPhone. The App Store also offers it on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro, where it runs as the iPhone app.

App Sales: iPhone, iPad, Mac (M1+), Apple Vision.

App Sales has an iPad version; Pulse doesn't.

Who should pick AppMeta Pulse vs App Sales?

  • You want a privacy label that declares zero data collection, as opposed to "anonymous" or "minimal" collection
  • You won't use App Store Connect dashboards that share data with third-party advertising networks
  • You want MRR, trials, and trial conversions alongside revenue, plus widgets and review replies
  • You prefer one simple price ({P_MONTHLY@appmeta-pulse}/mo or {P_LIFETIME@appmeta-pulse} lifetime) over a five-tier subscription ladder
  • You want Apple's official vocabulary (proceeds, sales, units, trials) in the UI, not a third party's own terms
Stick with App Sales - Store Reports if…
  • You want an iPad app
  • What the privacy label declares is acceptable to you
  • The higher rating count (4.9 / 41) matters to you
  • You have months of App Sales history you don't want to walk away from

What is the best alternative to App Sales - Store Reports?
AppMeta Pulse is an App Store Connect dashboard for the same people App Sales serves: indie iOS developers who check revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions across multiple apps from an iPhone. The two differ on privacy. AppMeta Pulse's App Store privacy label declares zero data collection. App Sales' privacy label declares that the app collects purchases, identifiers, and usage data, and shares some of it for third-party advertising. Both apps read from App Store Connect with your own API key, so the difference is what each app does with the data once it has fetched it.
Does App Sales - Store Reports track me?
According to its App Store privacy label, App Sales - Store Reports collects data used to track you across other apps and websites, including purchases, identifiers, and usage data, and shares some of that data with third-party advertising networks. App Store privacy labels call this "Data Used to Track You." If that is acceptable for your use, App Sales is a capable app with a 4.9 average rating. If you want a dashboard that declares no advertising tracking at all, AppMeta Pulse is the closest equivalent.
Does App Sales have a higher rating than AppMeta Pulse?
Yes, on volume. App Sales has roughly 41 ratings averaging 4.9, while AppMeta Pulse is a newer release without enough ratings to show an average. More ratings is a real advantage. It mostly reflects how long the app has been on the market and how many users left a rating, and says little about privacy or features.
What does App Sales have that AppMeta Pulse does not?
An iPad version, a higher rating count thanks to a longer time in market, and a tiered plan ladder. Country breakdowns and multiple App Store Connect accounts are in both apps: AppMeta Pulse shows each app's top territories and lets you add and switch between accounts.
