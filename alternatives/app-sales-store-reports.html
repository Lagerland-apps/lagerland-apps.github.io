Looking for an
App Sales alternative?

App Sales - Store Reports is a capable iPhone dashboard for App Store Connect, with a 4.9 average rating across 41 reviews. Multi-account API support, country-level breakdowns, and daily and monthly charts all work as advertised. Developers who look for an alternative usually do so because of the privacy label, which lists purchases, identifiers, and usage data under Data Used to Track You. If that is acceptable for your use, stay with App Sales. AppMeta Pulse has no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics, keeps your sales history on your iPhone after Apple's one-year window, and lets you answer reviews from the same app.

How does AppMeta Pulse compare to App Sales - Store Reports?

App Sales is the better-rated app today and runs on iPad, which AppMeta Pulse does not. The differences that matter most are what each app does with your data, how long your history lasts, and whether you can answer reviews.

Privacy and tracking
AppMeta Pulse: No ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics or SDKs. There is no AppMeta Pulse server: requests go from your iPhone to Apple, and exchange rates come from the European Central Bank.

App Sales - Store Reports: Privacy label declares "Data Used to Track You" (purchases, identifiers, usage data), with some of that data shared for third-party advertising, analytics, and app functionality.

Both apps read from App Store Connect with your own API key. They differ in what each app does around that data.
Sales history
AppMeta Pulse: App Store Connect serves about a year of daily sales. Pulse keeps every day it fetches on your iPhone until you delete it, and once a full year is stored, Trends compares this year with last year.

App Sales: Daily and monthly charts of the data it fetches from App Store Connect.
Feature coverage
AppMeta Pulse: Proceeds or sales, units, MRR, subscribers, trials, and churn for every app, the top five territories per app, an App Store funnel (impressions, page views, downloads), and 12 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, in Apple's own vocabulary (proceeds, sales, units).

App Sales: Revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions, with advertised country-level insights and multi-account API key support. Pro features are split across tiers.
Reviews
AppMeta Pulse: Every app's reviews in one list. Translate a review on the device, reply one by one or to up to 25 at once, and on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence on a supported iPhone, start from two English drafts written on the device.

App Sales: Built around sales and downloads; check its App Store listing for review features.
Pricing structure
AppMeta Pulse: One tier, Pro: {P_MONTHLY@appmeta-pulse}/month or {P_YEARLY@appmeta-pulse}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@appmeta-pulse}, or {P_LIFETIME@appmeta-pulse} once for Lifetime. The free download opens a demo on sample data.

App Sales: Five tiers: Starter ($2.99), Growth ($4.99), Scale ($7.99), Enterprise ($12.99), Pro ($39.99).

AppMeta Pulse is simpler for the plain case, with no tier to pick. App Sales' ladder may sell the one feature you want at a price you prefer.
Credentials and platforms
AppMeta Pulse: An App Store Connect API key with the Admin role, stored in this iPhone's Keychain, where it never syncs. It can't change your prices, builds, or metadata; it writes only the review replies you send and analytics report requests. iPhone only, iOS 17 or later.

App Sales: API key on the device, with multi-account support explicitly advertised. iPhone, iPad, Mac (M1+), and Apple Vision.

Both let you add several App Store Connect accounts and switch between them.

Who should pick AppMeta Pulse vs App Sales?

Pick AppMeta Pulse if…
  • You won't use App Store Connect dashboards that track you or share data with advertising networks
  • You want your sales history kept after Apple's one-year window, and a fair year-over-year comparison
  • You answer App Store reviews and want to do it from your phone, in bulk when they pile up
  • You want MRR, subscribers, trials, and churn next to revenue, plus 12 widgets
  • You prefer one paid tier over a five-tier ladder
Stick with App Sales - Store Reports if…
  • You want an iPad or Mac app
  • What the privacy label declares is acceptable to you
  • The higher rating count (4.9 / 41) matters to you
  • You'd rather choose from App Sales' five tiers, $2.99 to $39.99, than buy a single Pro tier

Questions

FAQ

What is the best alternative to App Sales - Store Reports?
AppMeta Pulse is an App Store Connect app for the same people App Sales serves: indie iOS developers who check revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions across several apps from an iPhone. The two differ on privacy. App Sales' App Store privacy label lists purchases, identifiers, and usage data as data used to track you. AppMeta Pulse has no ads, no tracking, and no third-party analytics, and requests go from your iPhone straight to Apple. Pulse also keeps your sales history on the iPhone after Apple's one-year window and lets you answer reviews, up to 25 at once.
Does App Sales - Store Reports track me?
According to its App Store privacy label, App Sales - Store Reports collects data used to track you across other apps and websites, including purchases, identifiers, and usage data, and shares some of that data with third-party advertising networks. App Store privacy labels call this "Data Used to Track You." If that is acceptable for your use, App Sales is a capable app with a 4.9 average rating. If you want a dashboard with no advertising tracking at all, AppMeta Pulse is the closest equivalent.
Does App Sales have a higher rating than AppMeta Pulse?
Yes, on volume. App Sales has roughly 41 ratings averaging 4.9, while AppMeta Pulse is a newer release without enough ratings to show an average. More ratings is a real advantage. It mostly reflects how long the app has been on the market and how many users left a rating, and says little about privacy or features.
What does App Sales have that AppMeta Pulse does not?
iPad and Mac versions, a higher rating count thanks to a longer time on the market, and a tiered plan ladder. AppMeta Pulse runs on iPhone only. Country breakdowns and multiple App Store Connect accounts are in both apps: AppMeta Pulse shows each app's top five territories and lets you add and switch between accounts.
Does AppMeta Pulse cost more or less than App Sales?

App Sales has a tiered structure starting at $2.99 and ranging up to $39.99 for the Pro tier. At the entry tier, AppMeta Pulse costs less per month.
