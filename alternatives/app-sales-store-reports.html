App Store Connect on iPhone · Privacy label matters

Looking for an
App Sales alternative?

App Sales — Store Reports is a feature-rich iPhone dashboard for App Store Connect with a strong 4.9 average rating across 41 reviews. It does what it advertises well — multi-account API support, country-level breakdowns, daily and monthly charts. The reason indie developers come looking for an alternative is usually one specific thing: App Sales' App Store privacy label declares that the app collects purchases, identifiers, and usage data, and that some of this data is shared with third-party advertising networks. If that posture is acceptable for your use case, stay with App Sales. If you want a dashboard that declares zero data collection — verifiable on the App Store listing — AppMeta Pulse is built for that.

How does AppMeta Pulse compare to App Sales — Store Reports?

App Sales is the better-rated app today and runs on iPad, which AppMeta Pulse does not. The decision rarely turns on those features alone; it usually turns on the App Store privacy label — which is the field on each app's listing that says, in Apple's own categories, what data the app collects and how it is used.

App Store privacy label — what each app declares
AppMeta Pulse: Privacy label declares zero data collection. No "Data Used to Track You," no "Data Linked to You," no "Data Not Linked to You."

App Sales — Store Reports: Privacy label declares "Data Used to Track You" (purchases, identifiers, usage data), with some of that data shared for third-party advertising, analytics, and app functionality.

Both apps read from App Store Connect with your own API key. The difference is what each app does with the data after fetching it.
Social proof — App Store ratings
AppMeta Pulse: Newer release; insufficient ratings to display an aggregate.

App Sales: Approximately 41 ratings averaging 4.9 — a meaningful, longer-tenured user base.

App Sales wins this dimension today on volume. AppMeta Pulse will catch up over time but does not claim parity here.
Feature coverage
AppMeta Pulse: Proceeds or sales, MRR, active subscriptions, trial conversions, downloads, top territories per app, per-app portfolio view, period-over-period trends, 12 widgets, and review replies, in Apple's native vocabulary (proceeds vs sales, units).

App Sales: Revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions, with advertised country-level insights and multi-account API key support. Daily and monthly charts; tiered Pro features.
Pricing structure
AppMeta Pulse: Single tier — {P_MONTHLY@appmeta-pulse}/month, {P_YEARLY@appmeta-pulse}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@appmeta-pulse} lifetime.

App Sales: Multi-tier ladder — Starter ($2.99), Growth ($4.99), Scale ($7.99), Enterprise ($12.99), Pro ($39.99).

AppMeta Pulse is cheaper for the simple case and removes the tier-shopping problem. App Sales' ladder may give individual features at a price you prefer.
Authentication and credentials
AppMeta Pulse: App Store Connect API key (.p8 + key ID) stored in system Keychain. No build, pricing, or metadata controls.

App Sales: Same model — API key on-device, read-only against ASC. Multi-account API key support is explicitly advertised.

Both let you add several App Store Connect accounts and switch between them in the app.
Platforms
AppMeta Pulse: iPhone. The App Store also offers it on Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro, where it runs as the iPhone app.

App Sales: iPhone, iPad, Mac (M1+), Apple Vision.

App Sales has an iPad version; Pulse doesn't.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to App Sales — Store Reports?
AppMeta Pulse is an App Store Connect dashboard built for the same audience and use case as App Sales — indie iOS developers checking revenue, downloads, IAPs, and subscriptions across multiple apps from iPhone. The substantive difference is privacy: AppMeta Pulse's App Store privacy label declares zero data collection. App Sales' privacy label declares that the app collects purchases, identifiers, and usage data, with some of that data shared for third-party advertising. Both apps read from App Store Connect with your own API key; the difference is what each app does with the data after fetching it.
Does App Sales — Store Reports track me?
According to its App Store privacy label, App Sales — Store Reports collects data used to track you across other apps and websites — including purchases, identifiers, and usage data — and shares some of that data with third-party advertising networks. This is what App Store privacy labels call "Data Used to Track You." If that posture is acceptable for your use case, App Sales is a feature-rich app with a strong 4.9 average rating. If you want a dashboard that does not declare advertising tracking at all, AppMeta Pulse is the closest equivalent.
Does App Sales have a higher rating than AppMeta Pulse?
Yes, on volume. App Sales has roughly 41 ratings averaging 4.9, while AppMeta Pulse is a newer release with insufficient ratings to display an aggregate. Higher rating volume is a legitimate social-proof advantage, but it reflects how long the app has been in market and how many users left a rating, not necessarily privacy posture or feature parity.
What does App Sales have that AppMeta Pulse does not?
An iPad version, a higher rating count thanks to a longer time in market, and a tiered plan ladder. Country breakdowns and multiple App Store Connect accounts are in both apps: AppMeta Pulse shows each app's top territories and lets you add and switch between accounts.
