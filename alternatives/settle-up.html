How does RightSplit compare to Settle Up?

Settle Up is a shared group ledger. RightSplit settles one bill and closes it.

Scope
RightSplit: One bill, settled now and closed out. Pro+ Spaces also keep a trip's or shared home's balances on your phone.

Settle Up: Ongoing group ledger across many expenses, shared by the whole group.
Receipt OCR
RightSplit: Apple's Vision framework reads every line item on the device, with no upload and no server.

Settle Up: Manual expense entry; no receipt OCR as a core feature.
Tip math
RightSplit: Proportional to each person's share of the subtotal, with the method documented openly and fair rounding.

Settle Up: Equal-split convention; tip handled as a line item.
Pricing
RightSplit: Free + {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/yr or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} lifetime (one-time).

Settle Up: Free + Pro tier for advanced features.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Settle Up?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • Your splits are mostly single bills, not running tabs
  • You'd rather scan the receipt than type each item
  • You want the OCR and tip math documented openly
  • You prefer a lifetime price ({P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once) to a subscription
  • You don't want a group ledger sitting open in your phone
Stick with Settle Up if…
  • You're tracking shared expenses across weeks or months and everyone needs to add to them
  • You and your group rely on the running-balance view
  • You need a multi-currency ledger the whole group shares long-term
  • Your group is already onboarded onto Settle Up

FAQ

What is the best Settle Up alternative for one-off bills?
RightSplit is a privacy-first iPhone alternative to Settle Up for when the bill in front of you is the whole transaction and doesn't belong in a running group ledger. Receipt OCR runs on-device with Apple's Vision framework, the split goes item by item with a proportional tip, and you share each total via iMessage or WhatsApp. It is free for equal splits and your first scan, with Pro+ at {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/year or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} lifetime.
Does RightSplit track group balances over time?
Only on your own phone. RightSplit settles one bill at a time and closes it out. With Pro+, a Space (for a trip, a shared flat, or split subscriptions) collects receipts and expenses, keeps each person's balance, and works out a settle-up plan, with reminders for weekly or monthly bills. There are no accounts and no sync, so the rest of the group can't add expenses or see the balance. If you want a running group balance that everyone shares across many transactions and months, Settle Up's ledger model is the right fit. RightSplit is the app you open at the table when the bill comes.
Does RightSplit need an account like Settle Up?
No. Nobody needs an account. You enter names locally and share each person's total through the iOS share sheet, to Messages, WhatsApp, or any other app.
