Receipt splitting · One bill at a time · No account

A Settle Up alternative
for bills that don't need a ledger.

Settle Up keeps a ledger of who owes whom across a group's expenses, and members record their payments in it. For one dinner, the real work is collecting the money. RightSplit reads the receipt on your iPhone, you tap who had each item, and each person gets a message with their amount and a link to pay you. With Pro+, a Live Activity on your Lock Screen shows who has paid, and you get a reminder a day later if someone hasn't.

How does RightSplit compare to Settle Up?

Settle Up is a shared group ledger. RightSplit settles one bill and closes it.

Scope
RightSplit: One bill, settled now and closed out. Pro+ Spaces also keep a trip's or shared home's balances on your phone.

Settle Up: Ongoing group ledger across many expenses, shared by the whole group.
Reading the receipt
RightSplit: Apple Intelligence reads it on an eligible iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later); every other iPhone uses an on-device scanner. Nothing is uploaded, and every line is editable.

Settle Up: Manual expense entry; no receipt OCR as a core feature.
Getting paid back
RightSplit: One message per person with their amount and a tap-to-pay link for Venmo, PayPal.me, Revolut, Wise, IBAN, or Apple Pay. With Pro+, a Live Activity on your Lock Screen counts people off as they pay.

Settle Up: Group members record payments in the shared ledger.
Pricing
RightSplit: Equal splits and your first scan are free. Pro+ is {P_YEARLY@rightsplit}/yr or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once.

Settle Up: Free + Pro tier for advanced features.

Who should pick RightSplit vs Settle Up?

Pick RightSplit if…
  • Your splits are mostly single bills, not running tabs
  • You'd rather scan the receipt than type each item
  • You want the people at the table to receive a message and a pay link, with nothing to install
  • You prefer a one-time purchase ({P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once) to a subscription
  • You want to see who has paid you back without asking around (Pro+ Live Activity)
Stick with Settle Up if…
  • You're tracking shared expenses across weeks or months and everyone needs to add to them
  • You want each member to record their own payments in the shared ledger
  • You need a multi-currency ledger the whole group shares long-term
  • Your group is already onboarded onto Settle Up

FAQ

What is the best Settle Up alternative for one-off bills?
RightSplit is an iPhone alternative to Settle Up for when the bill in front of you is the whole transaction and doesn't belong in a running group ledger. The receipt is read on your iPhone, you tap who had each item, and each person gets their amount with a tap-to-pay link by Messages or WhatsApp. Equal splits with tip and your first scan are free; Pro+ is {P_YEARLY@rightsplit} a year or {P_LIFETIME@rightsplit} once.
Does RightSplit track group balances over time?
Only on your own phone. RightSplit settles one bill at a time. With Pro+, a Space (for a trip, a shared flat, or split subscriptions) collects receipts and expenses, keeps each person's balance, and works out the fewest transfers to settle up, and recurring bills remind you to log them. There are no accounts and no sync, so the rest of the group can't add expenses or see the balance. If you want a running balance the whole group shares across months, Settle Up's ledger is the right fit.
Does RightSplit need an account like Settle Up?
No. Settle Up works best when every member joins the group in its app. With RightSplit only you need the app: you type the names of the people at the table, and each person gets their amount as a message by Messages or WhatsApp, or you copy it into any other app.
