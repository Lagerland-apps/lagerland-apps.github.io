How does Soon. compare to Days Matter?

Both count down days. They differ in what they add around the number and in what the free tier includes.

Event-type-aware checklists (Plan Mode)
Soon.: Built-in templates for Trip, Wedding, Birthday, Pregnancy, Graduation, Move, and Concert. Each task is anchored to a T-minus offset (T-30, T-14, T-3) and scheduled backward from the date.

Days Matter: Generic countdown events without event-type templates.
Destination weather
Soon.: Apple WeatherKit forecast at the destination as the date approaches (Premium). Only the coarse destination is shared with WeatherKit.

Days Matter: No weather integration.
Widgets
Soon.: Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy in every iOS size: small, medium, large, ring, minimal, timeline.

Days Matter: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; the current listing does not mention StandBy.
Ads & account
Soon.: No ads on any tier, no account at any point, and no third-party SDKs.

Days Matter: Banner ads on the free tier and an optional account for cross-device sync.
Data storage
Soon.: On-device only. Events are included in your standard iPhone iCloud Backup, so a phone swap restores everything.

Days Matter: Optional account-based cloud sync, which puts your countdowns on a server tied to a login.
Price
Soon.: Free with 5 events. Premium {P_MONTHLY@soon}/mo or {P_YEARLY@soon}/yr or {P_LIFETIME@soon} lifetime (7-day trial).

Days Matter: Free with ads. Premium subscription removes ads and unlocks features (typical pricing in the category).

Who should pick Soon. vs Days Matter?

Pick Soon. if…
  • Your countdowns involve real planning: trips, weddings, pregnancies, moves
  • You want a checklist next to the countdown, tailored to the event type
  • You want destination weather on the widget as the date approaches
  • You want StandBy widgets as well as Lock Screen ones
  • You don't want ads in apps you use daily
  • You don't want a countdown account tied to a server
Stick with Days Matter if…
  • You need explicit cross-device sync via an account (iOS + Android, multiple iPhones without backup restore)
  • You're already happy with Days Matter's templates and visual themes
  • You'd rather see banner ads than pay anything (the free tier of either app works, but they make different trade-offs)

FAQ

What is the best Days Matter alternative for iPhone without ads?
Soon. is the closest planner-grade alternative to Days Matter for iPhone. It has no ads on any tier, no third-party trackers, and no account requirement, where Days Matter's free tier carries banner ads. Soon. also adds event-type Plan Mode checklists, Apple WeatherKit destination forecasts, and StandBy widgets that Days Matter doesn't ship.
Does Soon. import countdowns from Days Matter?
Not directly. There is no automated Days Matter import. Soon. can import any event from the iOS Calendar with one tap, and Days Matter can optionally sync events to iCloud Calendar, so one way over is to export your Days Matter events to iOS Calendar and then import each into Soon. You only do this once per countdown.
Does Soon. sync between iPhones the way Days Matter does?
Soon. is on-device only, so events live on your iPhone and not on a server. The countdowns are part of your standard iPhone iCloud Backup, and setting up a new phone from that backup restores everything. Days Matter has explicit cross-device cloud sync through an account. Soon. gives that up so you never need an account.
Is Soon. free like Days Matter?
Soon.'s free tier stays free and includes 5 events, one pinned widget, three themes, iOS Calendar import, memories, and reflection mode, with no ads and no upsell screens. Premium unlocks unlimited events, weather intelligence, daily notes, recurring events, and notifications: {P_MONTHLY@soon}/month, {P_YEARLY@soon}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@soon} once for lifetime access with a 7-day free trial. Days Matter's free tier shows banner ads; Soon.'s does not.
Does Soon. have Lock Screen and StandBy widgets like Days Matter?
Soon. supports Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy widgets in every iOS size: small, medium, large, ring, minimal, and timeline. Days Matter has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; its current App Store listing does not mention StandBy support.
