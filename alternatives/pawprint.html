On-device scanning · No account · Works with any vet

A Pawprint alternative
that reads the document instead of phoning your vet.

Pawprint, now Great Pet Care, built its reputation on fetching vaccination records from participating U.S. clinics into a cloud account. Pawza takes the other route. You scan the vet documents you already have and Pawza reads them on your device; with Pawza Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the record for you to check. There is no clinic integration, account, or server of ours, so it works with any vet in any country, and your records stay on your devices unless you sync them through your own iCloud. If you want records pulled automatically from a U.S. vet, Great Pet Care still does that.

How does Pawza compare to Pawprint / Great Pet Care?

Both aim for a complete record. They get there differently, with different privacy and reach.

How records get in
Pawza: Scan a document and Pawza reads it on your device; with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills it in. Years of visits can come in as one records PDF, split by date.

Pawprint / GPC: Historically fetched records from participating U.S. clinics into the cloud.

Questions

FAQ

Is Pawprint still available?
Pawprint has been rebranded as Great Pet Care and is still available and updated. It is still an account- and cloud-based app, historically known for fetching vaccination records from participating U.S. clinics. For a private replacement on your own devices, Pawza is the closest alternative.
What is the best Pawprint alternative for iPhone?
Pawza, if you want a private record on your own devices. Pawza does not request records from your vet's system. You scan the documents you have, Pawza reads them on your device, and with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the fields you confirm. It works with any vet, needs no account, and stores everything on your device with optional iCloud sync.
Can Pawza fetch records from my vet like Pawprint did?
No, and this is the main difference. Pawprint's retrieval depended on integrations with participating U.S. clinics. Pawza reads the documents you already have on your device and can import a multi-visit records PDF, so it works with any vet anywhere and keeps your data private, but you do the scanning yourself.
Is Pawza more private than Pawprint / Great Pet Care?
Yes. Pawza has no account and no server of its own; records live on your device with optional sync through your own iCloud, and document scanning runs on the device. Great Pet Care is a cloud-account service. Pawza also has no ads or third-party tracking.
