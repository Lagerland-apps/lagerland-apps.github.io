What is the best PiyoShogi alternative for learning shogi?
Shogiful, if you want coaching on top of graded play. YaneuraOu analyzes every game on-device and mistakes are explained in plain-language sentences. It tracks weaknesses across five skills, builds spaced-repetition training from your own games, and has Western piece sets with progressive kanji learning. It runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
Is PiyoShogi or Shogiful better for beginners?
Both are good. PiyoShogi's 40 levels mean you can always find a winnable game, and it's free. Shogiful's advantage for Western beginners is readability and explanation: Western pieces you can play before you can read kanji, and coaching that tells you why you lost. Many beginners would do well with both.
Does Shogiful have adjustable difficulty like PiyoShogi's 40 levels?
It has 10 named opponents across four tiers (~500 to 1800 strength) with distinct playing styles, where PiyoShogi has a 40-step dial. PiyoShogi's ladder is more finely graded; Shogiful's roster feels like playing different people, with progression built in.
Does PiyoShogi explain mistakes like Shogiful?
PiyoShogi's analysis function gives you engine evaluation. That's generous for a free app, but you interpret it yourself. Shogiful classifies each significant mistake into shogi-specific categories and explains it in sentences, then drills those patterns with spaced repetition.
