Apple Health insights · Private · On-device

Looking for a
Bevel alternative?

Bevel is a connected health coach that combines wearables, bloodwork, and AI guidance and serves them from the cloud. Observa works from the Apple Health data you already have and runs every calculation on your own device. Each morning you get a Readiness score, the reason behind it, and a straight answer to "Should I train today?". It covers less ground than Bevel and needs no account.

How does Observa compare to Bevel?

Bevel covers more data sources through the cloud; Observa covers Apple Health only and keeps it on your device.

Scope
Observa: Apple Health only: sleep, HRV (SDNN), resting heart rate, workouts, activity, breathing, and body data, with ECG recordings in Pro.

Bevel: Wearables, bloodwork integrations, health records, and lifestyle journaling, a much broader set of data routed through their AI coach.
Where compute happens
Observa: On your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch. The app makes no network calls of its own. Privacy Manifest.

Bevel: Cloud-based, which the AI coach and the merging of data from many sources depend on.
What you get back
Observa: Readiness, Sleep Score, and Stress, each with "Why this score?"; a Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest call; and up to four ranked Patterns with confidence labels. Pro adds Functional Age, a norm-based fitness estimate that is not clinical.

Bevel: AI-coach guidance, biological age scoring, integrated dashboards, conversational Q&A about your data.
Account & pricing
Observa: No account. The daily scores are free; Pro costs {P_MONTHLY@observa}/month, {P_YEARLY@observa}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@observa} once, with Family Sharing on every paid plan.

Bevel: Account required (cloud product). Multi-tier subscription.

When to pick Observa over Bevel

  • You don't want your health data on someone else's servers, whatever security they promise
  • Apple Health is your main signal, and you don't have lab data to feed a coach
  • You want a short, specific reason behind each score instead of coach paragraphs
  • You want the answer on your wrist or from Siri, with an Apple Watch app and complications
  • You'd rather pay {P_LIFETIME@observa} once than subscribe to a coach indefinitely

When to keep Bevel instead

  • You want a connected coach that integrates bloodwork, lab data, and wearables
  • You prefer conversational AI Q&A over fixed explanations
  • You use the biological-age and integrated lifestyle dashboards Bevel pioneered

What is the best Bevel alternative?
If you want your Apple Health data explained without a cloud AI coach, Observa is a close match. It gives you a daily Readiness score from sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and training load, measured against your own baseline, with "Why this score?" and a Push, Maintain, Recover, or Rest call. Is Observa a connected health coach like Bevel?
No. Bevel pairs wearables, bloodwork, and lifestyle data with an AI coach in the cloud. Observa works from Apple Health only, and its notes and explanations are rule-based and computed on your device.
