How does Observa compare to Bevel?

Bevel covers more data sources through the cloud; Observa covers Apple Health only and keeps it on your device.

Scope
Observa: Apple Health and Apple Watch data only: HRV, ECG, sleep stages, RHR, workouts, and active energy.

Bevel: Wearables, bloodwork integrations, health records, and lifestyle journaling, a much broader set of data routed through their AI coach.
Where compute happens
Observa: Entirely on-device. App Privacy nutrition label: zero data collected.

Bevel: Cloud-based, which the AI coach and the merging of data from many sources depend on.
What you get back
Observa: Plain-English insight cards explaining named correlations against your 60-day baseline, with sample size and confidence.

Bevel: AI-coach guidance, biological age scoring, integrated dashboards, conversational Q&A about your data.
Account & pricing
Observa: No account at all. Free, with Pro from {P_MONTHLY@observa}/mo or {P_LIFETIME@observa} lifetime.

Bevel: Account required (cloud product). Multi-tier subscription.

When to pick Observa over Bevel

  • You don't want your health data on someone else's servers, whatever security they promise
  • You don't have bloodwork or extensive lab data to feed a coach, and Apple Health is your primary signal
  • You want short sentences that explain specific correlations, and no AI-coach paragraphs
  • You'd rather pay {P_LIFETIME@observa} once than subscribe to a coach indefinitely
  • You like that there is no account to create

When to keep Bevel instead

  • You want a true connected coach that integrates bloodwork, lab data, and wearables
  • You prefer conversational AI Q&A over plain-English insight cards
  • You use the biological-age and integrated lifestyle dashboards Bevel pioneered

FAQ

What is the best Bevel alternative?
If you want plain-English Apple Health interpretation without a cloud coach, Observa is the closest match. It reads Apple Health and Apple Watch data and names the correlations it finds against a 60-day personal baseline. Everything stays on-device, with no account and no servers.
Is Observa a connected health coach like Bevel?
No, and that is on purpose. Bevel pairs wearables, bloodwork, and lifestyle data with an AI coach in the cloud. Observa translates the Apple Health data you already have, and every computation stays on your device.

Why pick on-device over a cloud-based health coach?
Cloud coaches have to upload your health data to work. You give up privacy and get breadth, since they can also see your bloodwork, lab records, and medical history. Observa covers less (Apple Health only), and Apple's App Privacy nutrition label lists it as collecting zero data.
