When should I pick Unpluq instead?

Pick Unpluq if any of these matter more to you than skipping hardware and paying once:

  • You want a physical tag as the unlock ritual, and you find the act of tapping (or deliberately not having the tag) more effective than any software gate.
  • You want the choice between a physical tag and several digital challenges to unlock.
  • You need more than one platform. Unpluq supports iOS and Android via NFC; EarnLock is iOS and Apple Watch only.
  • You don't want activity goals tied to movement.

EarnLock is for someone who doesn't want a tag to carry or a yearly bill, and would sooner earn the unlock with a walk or a workout. Apple's Family Controls does the blocking and Apple Health does the counting, for {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} once.
