Looking for an
Unpluq alternative?

Unpluq unlocks when you pass a digital challenge, such as shaking your phone, or tap its physical NFC tag. The free version covers 2 apps and 1 schedule, and the tag works with Premium, a subscription. Having to find the tag does slow you down, but it is also a gadget to carry and a plan to renew. EarnLock keeps the apps you pick locked until Apple Health shows you've moved, and halfway to your goal they open for an hour. Locking one app is free for good, and Premium comes monthly, yearly, or as a one-time {P_LIFETIME@earnlock} purchase.

When should I pick Unpluq instead?

Pick Unpluq if any of these matter more to you than skipping hardware:

  • You want a physical tag as the unlock ritual, and tapping it (or deliberately not having it) works better for you than any software gate.
  • You want a choice between the tag and several digital challenges.
  • You need more than one platform. Unpluq supports iPhone and Android; EarnLock is an iPhone app.
  • You don't want your screen time tied to movement.

EarnLock is for someone who doesn't want a tag to carry and would rather earn the unlock with a walk or a workout. Your iPhone already counts the steps, so there is no tag to keep track of.
