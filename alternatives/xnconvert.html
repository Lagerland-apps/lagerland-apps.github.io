FAQ

What is a good XnConvert alternative for iPhone and Mac?
MediaKit is an iPhone and Mac app that covers more kinds of file than XnConvert. XnConvert is a powerful free cross-platform batch image processor that supports hundreds of formats. MediaKit converts, resizes, and compresses images on your device and adds video, audio, PDF, archive, and ebook tools, more than 150 in one app.
Does MediaKit do batch image conversion like XnConvert?
Yes, with Pro. The Queue works through a batch of images one after another, and a Recipe can save a chain of steps, such as resize then convert then strip metadata, to run again on new files. On Mac, Folder Automation runs a Recipe on every file that lands in a watched folder. MediaKit converts to HEIC, JPG, PNG, TIFF, GIF, and WebP where your system can write it, and reads camera RAW, AVIF, PSD, and EXR. XnConvert goes deeper on pure image processing, with more filters, actions, and formats.
XnConvert is free, so why pick MediaKit?
XnConvert is free and excellent for dedicated image-batch work, especially if you want its filter and action chains. MediaKit keeps Image Resize and Image Compress free for good, one file at a time. Pro earns its place when image batches are one job among video, audio, PDF, and archive tasks, and when you want the same tools on your iPhone as well as your Mac.
Is MediaKit on-device like XnConvert?
Yes. Both process files locally with no upload. MediaKit makes no network requests of its own and needs no account; you can read its Privacy Manifest on the transparency page.
Does MediaKit run on iPhone, unlike desktop-only XnConvert?
Yes. XnConvert is a desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) with no mobile version. MediaKit runs on iPhone and Mac, and one purchase covers both; there is no iPad version. On iPhone you can bring photos in from the Photos library or the share sheet and save results back to Photos. If you need Windows or Linux, pick XnConvert.
How much does MediaKit cost?
Five MediaKit tools stay free for good, one file at a time, including Image Resize and Image Compress. Your first 3 days unlock every other tool for single-file runs with no payment.
