How does Chessful compare to Magnus Trainer?

Both use the same engine and aim at improving club players. They differ in how they teach and what they cost.

Training source
Chessful: A training queue built from the mistakes in your own games, sorted into about thirty motifs and drilled with spaced repetition.

Magnus Trainer: 250+ pre-authored lessons by Magnus Carlsen and a team of Grandmasters, in a structured curriculum from foundations through endgames.
Celebrity content
Chessful: None, from any named Grandmaster.

Magnus Trainer: Carlsen's name is the main draw. The World Champion presents bite-sized games with three-star scoring.
Account requirement
Chessful: No account at any point.

Magnus Trainer: Chess.com account required (Magnus Trainer was acquired by Chess.com / Play Magnus Group in 2022).
Offline support
Chessful: Works fully offline. Stockfish, the motif detectors, and the training engine all run on the device.

Magnus Trainer: Cloud-backed, with an account login required.

Chessful: Free tier, or Premium at {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/mo, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/yr, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} lifetime (7-day free trial).

Magnus Trainer: Free with IAPs. Premium costs $4.49 to $74.99/yr, or $299.99 lifetime.
Platforms
Chessful: iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Universal Purchase).

Magnus Trainer: iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision.

FAQ

What is the best Magnus Trainer alternative for iPhone?
Chessful is the closest functional alternative to Magnus Trainer for a player who wants training more than ranked play. Magnus Trainer ships 250+ pre-authored lessons by Magnus Carlsen and Grandmasters. Chessful builds an adaptive training queue from the specific mistake patterns in your own losses and drills it with spaced repetition.
Does Chessful have lessons by Magnus Carlsen?
No. Chessful has no celebrity content and no lessons from Carlsen, Caruana, or any other named Grandmaster. Its training engine takes the mistake patterns from your own games and builds focused spaced-repetition sessions around them. The idea is that drilling your real losses teaches more than watching a Grandmaster explain things you haven't run into yet.
How much does Chessful cost compared to Magnus Trainer?
Chessful's free tier has no ads and no time limit. Premium is {P_MONTHLY@chessful}/month, {P_YEARLY@chessful}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@chessful} once for lifetime access with a 7-day free trial. Magnus Trainer is free with in-app purchases: Premium subscriptions from $4.49 to $74.99 a year and a $299.99 lifetime option.
Does Chessful work without an account like Magnus Trainer?
Chessful requires no account, no sign-up, and no personal information. Magnus Trainer requires a Chess.com account (Chess.com / Play Magnus Group has owned it since 2022).
Can I use both Magnus Trainer and Chessful?
Yes, and many improving players do. Magnus Trainer is a structured curriculum, and Chessful drills the mistakes from your own games. Use Magnus Trainer to learn new material and Chessful to close the gaps in your real games.
