What is the best Subby alternative for iPhone?

How is AllPaid different from Subby?
Subby centers on recurring subscriptions and what they cost. AllPaid tracks subscriptions together with recurring and one-time bills such as rent, insurance, or an annual membership, reminds you at 9:00 before each due date, and reminds you before a contract ends.
