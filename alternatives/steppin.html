When should I pick Steppin instead?

Pick Steppin if any of these matter more to you than Apple Watch, the lifetime tier, and the daily goal model:

  • You prefer continuous micro-rewards to a single daily commitment. Walking a little to earn a little is Steppin's design, and it suits some people's habits better than a daily goal.
  • You're already comfortable with Steppin's per-step exchange rate and don't want to switch unlock mental models.
  • You don't wear an Apple Watch and only need step tracking on the iPhone, where Steppin's iPhone-only app may feel simpler.

EarnLock is for the person who tried Steppin's per-step model and found it became a constant negotiation: earn a few minutes, scroll, earn a few more, scroll. The daily goal asks for one decision in the morning and then stays out of the way.

EarnLock unlocks at a daily goal: you set the threshold in the morning, and crossing it unlocks the rest of your calendar day. Steppin gives me minutes per step. Why does EarnLock use a daily goal instead?
The two apps follow different ideas about behavior change. Steppin's per-step earning model gives continuous micro-rewards: walk a bit, earn a bit, scroll a bit. EarnLock's daily-goal model is a single commitment per day: you pick the threshold in the morning, and either you hit it or you don't. Hitting it unlocks the apps for the rest of the calendar day. Falling short earns at most a timed partial window once you pass 50% (an hour at halfway), plus the one math-gated emergency unlock. The per-step model can turn into a loop of small unlocks; the daily goal asks for one decision and then stays out of the way. Either can work, so pick the one that fits how you want your phone use to feel.
Which is more private, EarnLock or Steppin?
They are tied at the strictest tier. Both apps have Apple App Privacy labels reading Data Not Collected, meaning no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no data leaving the device. Both read HealthKit on-device only, and neither tracks you across other apps. The differences are elsewhere: the unlock mechanic, Apple Watch support, lifetime pricing, and locale coverage.
