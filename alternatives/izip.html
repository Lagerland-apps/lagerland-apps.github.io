FAQ

What is a good iZip alternative for iPhone and Mac?
MediaKit suits people who want more than zip and unzip. iZip is a popular free iOS utility for extracting ZIP, RAR, and 7Z and creating password-protected ZIPs. MediaKit creates ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, GZIP, XZ, and BZIP2 archives and extracts those plus RAR4 and RAR5, and it has more than 150 tools for video, audio, image, PDF, and ebook files. It shows no ads, and one purchase covers iPhone and Mac.
Does MediaKit handle zip and unzip like iZip?
For the common archive jobs, yes. MediaKit creates ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, GZIP, XZ, and BZIP2 archives and extracts those plus RAR4 and RAR5. You see what's inside before extracting, with limits against path traversal and zip bombs. MediaKit cannot create RAR archives and refuses encrypted RAR files. iZip extracts a longer list of formats (7Z, ZIPX, ISO, CAB, DEB, RPM, and more) and creates password-protected ZIPs, so for those iZip is the more complete tool.
Is MediaKit on-device like iZip?
Yes. Every MediaKit tool runs on your device, with no upload and no account, and the app makes no network requests of its own. You can read its Privacy Manifest on the transparency page. iZip also processes archives on the device, though its free tier shows ads and offers optional cloud-drive integrations.
How does MediaKit's pricing compare to iZip?
iZip is free with ads, with a Premium in-app purchase (around $7.99, verified against the App Store on 2026-05-31) that removes ads and unlocks features like AES encryption and HEIC conversion. MediaKit shows no ads. Why pick MediaKit over iZip?
Pick it if archives are one job among many. MediaKit creates and extracts archives, and the same app can compress a video, convert a HEIC photo, normalize audio, or compress a PDF, with more than 150 tools in all. Everything runs on your device without ads, and one purchase covers iPhone and Mac. iZip is a focused zip utility; MediaKit is a wider toolkit.
Does MediaKit run on both iPhone and Mac?
Yes. MediaKit runs on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Mac (macOS 14.6 or later), and one purchase covers both. There is no iPad version. iZip is primarily an iOS and iPadOS app from ComcSoft and also runs on Apple Silicon Macs and Apple Vision.
