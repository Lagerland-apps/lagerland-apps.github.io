for lifters who follow a program.

How does GymLogger X compare to Fitbod?

In Fitbod the algorithm plans each session. In GymLogger X the program does, and you can see every week of it.

Who decides the workout
GymLogger X: Your program: one of 44 ready-made programs, your own, or one the Smart Program Creator builds around your equipment, goals, injuries, and schedule. You can preview and edit every week.

Fitbod: An algorithm generates each session from recovery data.
Apple Watch
GymLogger X: A free Watch app. Start the workout on the iPhone, then log weight and reps, run the rest timer, and see live heart rate on the wrist.

Fitbod: An Apple Watch app that must connect to the iPhone.
Privacy model
GymLogger X: No account, and no ads or third-party analytics. Workouts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Fitbod: Account-based; processes training data to recommend.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Fitbod alternative without a subscription?
If you want to follow a program instead of an algorithm, GymLogger X is worth a look. Logging, the Apple Watch app, the Smart Program Creator, and 6 programs are free, and Pro is available as a {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} one-time purchase, so no subscription is required. Fitbod builds each workout for you from recovery data and needs a recurring subscription after a few free workouts.
Does GymLogger X generate workouts with AI like Fitbod?
Not session by session. The Smart Program Creator builds a whole program up front from your equipment, goals, injuries, schedule, and preferences, using fixed rules on your iPhone, and you can preview it, swap exercises, or regenerate it before you start. After that, the program sets each session. With Pro, the Next Move card suggests whether to rest, deal with a plateau, work on a weak point, or train as planned, and any change to the program is yours to make. There is no chat bot or language model. If you want an app to decide each workout for you, Fitbod does that.
Does GymLogger X work on Apple Watch like Fitbod?
Yes, and the Watch app is free. You start the workout on the iPhone, then log each set's weight and reps, run the rest timer, and see live heart rate and calories on the wrist; sets you log on the watch go through the phone. With heart rate available, the rest timer can tell you when your heart rate has recovered. Fitbod also has an Apple Watch app that connects to the iPhone, tied to its account and subscription.
Is GymLogger X private compared to Fitbod?
GymLogger X needs no account, carries no ads or third-party analytics, and has no server of its own. Workouts are stored on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud when you're signed in to iCloud. Fitbod is an account-based service that processes your training data to generate recommendations.
How much does GymLogger X cost compared to Fitbod?
GymLogger X is free to download and log with. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@gymlogger-x} a month or {P_YEARLY@gymlogger-x} a year, each starting with a {TRIAL@gymlogger-x}, or {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} once. Fitbod is subscription-only after a few free workouts, at around $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US; check the App Store for current prices.
Can I use GymLogger X with a coach's program?
Yes. A coach can share a program as a spreadsheet (week, day, exercise, sets, and reps), and Import Program turns it into a program you follow day by day; importing is a Pro feature. You can also enter the program yourself in the program editor and log it like any other.
