Questions

FAQ

What is the best Fitbod alternative without a subscription?
GymLogger X. Fitbod's AI-generated workouts need a recurring subscription. GymLogger X is a fast native logger you run yourself, with 1,500 exercises, smart programs, plateau detection, and Apple Watch logging, plus a free tier and a one-time lifetime Pro option.
Does GymLogger X generate workouts with AI like Fitbod?
No, on purpose. Fitbod's main feature is generating workouts from muscle recovery. GymLogger X assumes you or your program decide the training, and it logs that training fast and adds plateau detection. If you want an app to tell you what to do, Fitbod does that. If you write your own programming, pick GymLogger X.
Does GymLogger X work on Apple Watch like Fitbod?
Yes. You start the session on the iPhone, then log each set's reps and weight, run the rest timer and see live heart rate from the wrist, so the phone can stay in your bag between sets. The watch app needs the iPhone running the workout. Fitbod also has Apple Watch support tied to its account and subscription.
Is GymLogger X private compared to Fitbod?
Yes. GymLogger X requires no account and has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Workout data stays on-device and syncs through your own iCloud when iCloud is on. Fitbod is an account-based service that processes your training data to generate recommendations.
How much does GymLogger X cost compared to Fitbod?
GymLogger X is free at base; Pro is {P_MONTHLY@gymlogger-x}/month, {P_YEARLY@gymlogger-x}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} lifetime. Fitbod is subscription-only after a few free workouts, at around $12.99/month or $79.99/year. The lifetime tier ends the recurring cost.
Can I use GymLogger X with a coach's program?
Yes. GymLogger X doesn't add AI programming of its own, so it works well for logging a program your coach writes or one you run from a spreadsheet, with smart programs and plateau detection on top. Importing a spreadsheet plan is a Pro feature.
