How does MediaKit compare to PDF Expert?

The two apps do different jobs, and for some of them PDF Expert is the stronger pick.

Scope
MediaKit: More than 150 tools across video, audio, image, PDF, archive, and ebook files, so PDF is one category of six.

PDF Expert: A dedicated, far deeper PDF editor with annotation, advanced editing, and sync. The better pick for heavy PDF work.
What survives an edit
MediaKit: Watermarks, page numbers, and headers go on as overlays, so text stays searchable, vectors stay sharp, and bookmarks and links survive. Flatten and Redact rasterize on purpose.

PDF Expert: Edits text and pages directly in the document.
Privacy
MediaKit: Runs on your device, with no uploads and no account. See the Privacy Manifest.

PDF Expert: Edits locally, with optional cloud sync and AI features that may use the network.
Platforms
MediaKit: iPhone and Mac, one purchase for both. No iPad version.

PDF Expert: iPhone, iPad, and Mac through its Premium subscription.

FAQ

Is MediaKit a good PDF Expert alternative?
It depends on what you need. PDF Expert is a deep, dedicated PDF editor, and for serious annotation, text and page editing, form filling, and cross-device sync it is the stronger tool and the better pick. MediaKit handles PDF utility jobs on your device (compress, merge, split, reorder, sign, redact, encrypt, and OCR) among more than 150 tools for other kinds of file. If your PDF work is occasional utility tasks and you also convert video, audio, and images, MediaKit is a practical alternative with a one-time lifetime option.
Can MediaKit edit PDFs like PDF Expert?
Not to the same depth. PDF Expert is built for rich PDF editing and annotation, and MediaKit doesn't edit the text of a PDF. MediaKit's PDF tools merge, split, extract and reorder pages, compress with Screen, Ebook, Printer, or Prepress presets, encrypt and unlock, redact, sign, add watermarks, page numbers, and headers, compare two PDFs, and OCR scans into searchable text in the language you choose. Watermarks and page numbers go on as overlays, so text stays searchable and bookmarks and links survive; Flatten and Redact rasterize on purpose.
Does MediaKit have a subscription like PDF Expert?
MediaKit offers a subscription and a one-time option: Pro is {P_MONTHLY@mediakit}/month or {P_YEARLY@mediakit}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@mediakit}, or {P_LIFETIME@mediakit} once with no renewal. PDF Compress is one of five tools that stay free for good. PDF Expert moved to a subscription model (around $79.99/year, with a one-time lifetime option around $139.99) and a limited free tier.
Does MediaKit upload my PDFs like a cloud service?
No. Every MediaKit tool runs on your iPhone or Mac, and the app makes no network requests of its own, so a contract or a scan never leaves the device. There is no account, and the app has no analytics SDK. PDF Expert also edits locally, with optional cloud sync and AI features that may use the network.
Does MediaKit work on iPhone and Mac like PDF Expert?
Yes. MediaKit runs on iPhone and Mac, and one purchase covers both. There is no iPad version. PDF Expert covers iPhone, iPad, and Mac through its subscription, so if you work on PDFs on an iPad, PDF Expert is the one to pick.
