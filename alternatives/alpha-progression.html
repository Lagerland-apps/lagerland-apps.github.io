Questions

FAQ

Does Alpha Progression have an Apple Watch app?
Not a dedicated one. Alpha Progression's own comparison table lists no dedicated Watch app, and its App Store listing covers iPhone (plus Apple Vision), with the rest timer shown as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. GymLogger X has a Watch app: you start the session on the iPhone, then log each set's reps and weight, run the rest timer and see live heart rate from the wrist.
Is there an Alpha Progression alternative with a lifetime price?
GymLogger X offers one. Its core logging is free, and Pro is $1.99 a month, $17.99 a year, or $39.99 once for lifetime access. Alpha Progression has a permanent free version, and its Pro plan is $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, with a 14-day trial on the yearly plan and no lifetime option listed. Check current pricing in your App Store.
Do both apps build periodized programs with deloads?
Yes. Alpha Progression's Pro plan generator includes periodization with mesocycles, RIR targets and deloads. GymLogger X's Smart Program Creator builds Full Body, Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs, Push/Pull, Arnold and Bro Split programs with linear, undulating, block or wave periodization, reps-in-reserve targets and a deload week every 4 to 6 weeks depending on your level. One program is free; Pro removes the limit.
Can I log reps in reserve for each set in GymLogger X?
No. GymLogger X writes reps-in-reserve targets into the programs it builds, but a logged set is weight and reps, and its analytics work from those. Alpha Progression lets you record RIR as you log sets on its Pro plan and uses your performance to recommend weight and reps before each set. If per-set effort ratings matter to you, Alpha Progression is the better fit.
Which app is more private?
GymLogger X's App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and it needs no account. Alpha Progression can also be used without an account, with guest data kept on the device, but its App Store label lists purchases and identifiers as data used to track you, and fitness, contact, usage and other data as linked to you.
Does GymLogger X recommend my next weight?
No. It shows what you lifted last time, flags plateaus with a published rule on Pro, and leaves the next increase to you. Alpha Progression's Pro plan gives a weight and rep recommendation before each set based on your previous workouts and today's sets, which is a real advantage if you want the app to steer progression set by set.
