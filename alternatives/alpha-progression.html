How does GymLogger X compare to Alpha Progression?

The programming ideas are similar. Alpha Progression coaches each set. GymLogger X puts logging and heart rate on your wrist and offers a one-time lifetime option.

Apple Watch
GymLogger X: A free Watch app. Start the workout on the iPhone, then log weight and reps, run the rest timer, and see live heart rate on the wrist.

Alpha Progression: No dedicated Watch app; the rest timer runs as a Live Activity on the iPhone.
Effort and recommendations
GymLogger X: Logs weight and reps. Each set row shows last time's numbers and the target, and you choose the next weight. Pro adds a plateau status for each lift and a Next Move card.

Alpha Progression: Optional per-set RIR and a weight and rep recommendation before each set (Pro).
Privacy model
GymLogger X: No account, and no ads or third-party analytics. Workouts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Alpha Progression: Guest mode without an account; its App Store label lists purchases and identifiers as used to track you.

Questions

FAQ

Does Alpha Progression have an Apple Watch app?
Not a dedicated one. Alpha Progression's own comparison table lists no dedicated Watch app, and its App Store listing covers iPhone (plus Apple Vision), with the rest timer shown as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. GymLogger X has a free Apple Watch app: you start the workout on the iPhone, then log each set's weight and reps, run the rest timer, and see live heart rate on the wrist. With heart rate available, the rest timer can tell you when your heart rate has recovered.
Is there an Alpha Progression alternative with a lifetime price?
GymLogger X offers one. Logging is free, and Pro is {P_MONTHLY@gymlogger-x} a month or {P_YEARLY@gymlogger-x} a year, each starting with a {TRIAL@gymlogger-x}, or {P_LIFETIME@gymlogger-x} once. Alpha Progression has a permanent free version, and its Pro plan is $12.99 a month or $79.99 a year in the US, with a 14-day trial on the yearly plan and no lifetime option listed. Check current pricing in your App Store.
Do both apps build periodized programs with deloads?
Yes. Alpha Progression's Pro plan generator includes periodization with mesocycles, RIR targets, and deloads. GymLogger X's Smart Program Creator builds full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs, push/pull, Arnold, and bro split programs around your equipment, goals, injuries, and schedule, with linear, undulating, block, or wave periodization, and its preview shows the deload weeks. The Creator is free; free users keep one program at a time, and Pro removes the limit.
Can I log reps in reserve for each set in GymLogger X?
No. A logged set in GymLogger X is weight and reps, plus whether it was a warm-up, and its analytics work from those. Alpha Progression lets you record RIR as you log sets on its Pro plan and uses your performance to recommend weight and reps before each set. If per-set effort ratings matter to you, Alpha Progression is the better fit.
Which app is more private?
GymLogger X needs no account and has no server of its own: workouts stay on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud, and the app carries no ads or third-party analytics. Alpha Progression can also be used without an account, with guest data kept on the device, but its App Store label lists purchases and identifiers as data used to track you, and fitness, contact, usage, and other data as linked to you.
Does GymLogger X recommend my next weight?
No. Each set row shows what you lifted last time and the target, and the next increase is yours to choose. With Pro, each lift gets a plateau status, and the Next Move card says whether to rest, deal with a plateau, work on a weak point, or train as planned. Alpha Progression's Pro plan gives a weight and rep recommendation before each set based on your previous workouts and today's sets, which is a real advantage if you want the app to steer progression set by set.
