FAQ

What is the best TrackMySubs alternative for iPhone?
If you don't want a web account, AllPaid is the closest native iPhone alternative. TrackMySubs is a browser-based service with a login. AllPaid keeps your bills on your iPhone with no account, web login, or bank connection, and it covers bills and contracts as well as subscriptions.
How is AllPaid different from TrackMySubs?
TrackMySubs is a web service with access from any device and folders, and your data sits on its servers. AllPaid is an iPhone app that keeps your bills on the phone, needs no account, and adds one-time bills, contract end dates with a reminder before renewal, documents attached to each bill, ten widgets, and Siri. TrackMySubs reaches you from any browser; AllPaid only runs on iPhone.
Does AllPaid store my data in the cloud like TrackMySubs?
No. AllPaid stores your bills on your iPhone, and there is no AllPaid server or account. If you turn on iCloud Sync (Pro), a copy goes to your own private iCloud, which we can't access. There are no third-party analytics. TrackMySubs, as a web service, keeps your data on its servers behind a login.
Does AllPaid send renewal reminders like TrackMySubs?
Yes. AllPaid reminds you at 9:00 before each due date, and you can mark the bill paid or snooze it from the notification. For a contract with an end date, a reminder comes at 10:00 before it ends. Free sends one reminder per bill; Pro lets you pick several days plus the due day.
How much does AllPaid cost compared to TrackMySubs?
AllPaid is free for up to 3 bills. Pro adds unlimited bills, several reminders per bill, Spending Analytics, payment history, and iCloud Sync for {P_MONTHLY@allpaid}/month, {P_YEARLY@allpaid}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@allpaid} once, and every Pro plan covers up to five family members through Family Sharing. TrackMySubs has a free tier for a limited number of subscriptions and paid plans above it.
Can I use AllPaid without an internet connection?
Yes. Bills, reminders, widgets, and attachments all live on your iPhone, so AllPaid works offline. It needs a connection only for purchases and iCloud Sync. TrackMySubs, being web-based, needs a connection and a login to reach your data.
