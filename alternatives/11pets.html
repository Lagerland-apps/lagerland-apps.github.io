How does Pawza compare to 11pets?

Both keep your pets' health records in one place. They differ in six areas.

Getting records in
Pawza: Scan a document and Pawza reads it on your device. With Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in dates, vet, vaccines, medications, and diagnoses for you to confirm. One records PDF with years of visits can be split by date; your first import is free.

11pets: Manual entry across its forms, with photo attachments for documents.
Where data lives
Pawza: On your device, with optional sync through your own private iCloud. No account, and Lagerland Apps has no server.

11pets: In an 11pets cloud account, synced across devices and the web.
Pricing
Pawza: Free for one pet. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@pawza}/mo or {P_YEARLY@pawza}/yr, each starting with a {TRIAL@pawza}, or {P_LIFETIME@pawza} once. Family Sharing on every Pro plan.

11pets: Free tier; premium subscription (commonly ~$4.99/mo). No one-time lifetime option.
Platforms
Pawza: One universal app for iPhone and iPad. No Android, Mac, or web version.

11pets: iPhone, Android, and a web app, which suits a household that isn't all-Apple.
Scope
Pawza: The owner's record: medications with Given buttons and refill reminders, vaccines, weight, symptoms, a daily care log, and a free Emergency Card and Lost Pet Kit.

11pets: A broad 50+ feature platform, including provider collaboration and tools for shelters and breeders.
Sharing
Pawza: The Vet Visit PDF and the Sitter Pack (both Pro) you hand over or send. Sync covers your own devices only.

11pets: Granular, time-limited sharing with veterinary providers and other professionals.

Which one to pick

Pick Pawza if you want…
  • To scan vet papers instead of typing them, with the text read on your device
  • Records on your own devices with no account and no third-party server
  • A one-time Pro purchase instead of a monthly subscription
  • Care logged in one tap from a widget, Control Center, or Siri
  • A Vet Visit PDF that shows what changed since the last appointment (Pro)
Stick with 11pets if you want…
  • An Android app or a web app as well as Apple devices
  • Its 50+ feature platform
  • Collaboration and provider tools for shelters, breeders, or vet professionals
  • Granular, time-limited sharing of records with care providers

The two suit different owners. Pawza puts a private record and on-device scanning first; 11pets puts breadth and cross-platform collaboration first.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best 11pets alternative for iPhone?
For a private record on iPhone and iPad, Pawza is the closest fit. Both track vaccinations, medications, weight, and vet visits for several pets. Pawza reads vet documents on your device, and with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the fields for you to check. Records stay on your devices with no account. 11pets keeps data in a cloud account and also runs on Android and the web.
Does Pawza require an account like 11pets?
No. Pawza has no account and no server of its own. Records stay on your device, and optional sync runs through your own private iCloud between your iPhone and iPad. 11pets uses an account to sync across iPhone, Android, and the web and to share with providers.
How much does Pawza cost compared to 11pets?
Pawza is free for one pet. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@pawza}/month or {P_YEARLY@pawza}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@pawza}, or {P_LIFETIME@pawza} once, and every Pro plan includes Family Sharing. 11pets has a free tier and a premium subscription (commonly around $4.99/month) with no one-time option.
Can Pawza scan vet documents the way I'd otherwise type into 11pets?
Yes. Photograph a certificate, lab result, or prescription and Pawza reads the text on your device. With Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the fields; otherwise you fill them in from the recognized text. You check everything before it is saved, and documents are not uploaded. In 11pets these records are entered by hand.
When should I choose 11pets over Pawza?
Choose 11pets if you need an Android or web app, if you want to share records directly with providers, or if you are a breeder, shelter, groomer, or vet professional who needs its collaboration tools. Pawza is for the individual owner who wants a private record on iPhone and iPad.
