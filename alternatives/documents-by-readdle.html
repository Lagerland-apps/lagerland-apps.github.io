FAQ

Is MediaKit a replacement for Documents by Readdle?
Not exactly. They do different jobs, and many people use both. Documents by Readdle is an excellent free file manager and reader for iPhone and iPad: it organizes files, reads PDFs, downloads, and syncs across clouds. MediaKit has more than 150 tools that compress and convert video, audio, images, PDFs, archives, and ebooks on your device. You use Documents to file and read, and MediaKit to convert and compress.
Does MediaKit manage and organize files like Documents by Readdle?
No. Documents by Readdle is a dedicated file manager with folders, a built-in browser, a media player, and deep cloud integration (iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive), and MediaKit doesn't try to replace it. MediaKit changes files: it compresses a video, converts a HEIC photo, merges or compresses a PDF, extracts audio, or creates a ZIP. On iPhone, finished results stay in its Library tab until you clear them. If you need to organize and read files, Documents is the better pick.
Does MediaKit run on iPhone like Documents by Readdle?
Yes. MediaKit runs on iPhone and Mac, and one purchase covers both. It has no iPad version, while Documents by Readdle runs on iPhone and iPad. On iPhone you can send a file to MediaKit from the share sheet, including from Documents, and save the result to Photos or share it.
Do MediaKit and Documents both keep files on-device?
MediaKit runs every tool on your iPhone or Mac, and the app makes no network requests of its own, so files are never uploaded while you convert or compress them. Documents by Readdle stores and syncs files across cloud services, since cloud sync is a core feature of a file manager. If you want a file to stay on your device while you change it, that is the relevant difference.
How does MediaKit's pricing compare to Documents by Readdle?
Documents by Readdle is free for its core file-manager features, with optional Documents Plus and VPN subscriptions for advanced PDF tools and browsing privacy. Five MediaKit tools stay free for good, one file at a time: Video Compress, Image Compress, Image Resize, Audio Convert, and PDF Compress. Should I use MediaKit or Documents by Readdle?
Use Documents by Readdle to organize, read, download, and sync files across your clouds. Use MediaKit when you need to change a file: compress a video for sharing, convert an image, shrink a PDF, extract audio, or build an archive, all on your device. The two apps complement each other.
