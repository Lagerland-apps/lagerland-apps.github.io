Does MediaKit convert HEIC to JPG and PNG like iMazing?
Yes. MediaKit's image Convert tool turns HEIC into JPG, PNG, TIFF, GIF, or WebP where your system can write it, on your device. It also reads camera RAW, AVIF, PSD, and EXR. Is MediaKit on-device like iMazing HEIC Converter?
Yes. Both convert locally with no upload. MediaKit makes no network requests of its own, needs no account, and has no analytics SDK. You can read its Privacy Manifest on the transparency page.
Does MediaKit run on iPhone as well as Mac?
