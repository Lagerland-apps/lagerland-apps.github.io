How does LiftLog compare to Caliber?

LiftLog is an app you coach yourself with; Caliber is a coaching service.

Coaching model
LiftLog: Self-coached, with 40 ready programs. Each exercise progresses by double progression, linear, % of Training Max or RPE, with a deload after repeated misses.

Caliber: 1:1 remote human coach with custom programming and weekly check-ins.
Pricing
LiftLog: Free 7-day trial (no card), then a one-time, pay-what-you-can {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog}.

Caliber: Monthly coaching subscription (varies by coach tier).
Privacy
LiftLog: No account or third-party tracking. Data stays on the device, with JSON and CSV export.

Caliber: Account required; data shared with assigned coach.
Best for
LiftLog: Lifters who already know the program they want to run and want a well-made app they own.

Caliber: Lifters who want accountability and human eyes on every program decision.

Who should pick LiftLog vs Caliber?

Pick LiftLog if…
  • You already know which program you want to run (5/3/1, StrongLifts, PPL, Upper/Lower, your own template)
  • You're tired of recurring fitness subscriptions and want to pay once
  • You'd rather have a clean log than a coaching DM thread
  • You don't need accountability, just a tool that stays out of the way during a working set
  • You want your training history on your own device instead of a coach's server
Stick with Caliber if…
  • You actively want a remote human coach reviewing your training
  • You want someone to tell you what to do this week, more than you want a template
  • You value weekly check-ins and form-video feedback
  • You're a new lifter who'd benefit from outside structure

LiftLog is a design-led iPhone alternative to Caliber for lifters who don't want a remote coach on subscription. It ships 40 ready programs (StrongLifts 5×5, PPL, Upper/Lower, 5/3/1 and more) whose weights progress automatically, with a deload after repeated misses, tracks estimated 1RM and Rep PRs at 1/3/5/10 reps, and costs a one-time, pay-what-you-can {P_IAP:Minimum@liftlog} to {P_IAP:Patron@liftlog} after a free 7-day trial.
Do I need a coach to use LiftLog?
No. LiftLog ships 40 self-coached programs, including StrongLifts 5×5, Starting Strength, Push/Pull/Legs, Upper/Lower, full-body splits, and 5/3/1 with Training Max, and you can build your own. Each exercise progresses on its own rule (double progression, linear, percent of Training Max, or RPE) and drops to 90% after three missed sessions in a row.
