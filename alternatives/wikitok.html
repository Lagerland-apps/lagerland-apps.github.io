Wikipedia · Swipe feed · Native iPhone & iPad

A WikiTok alternative
you can install from the App Store.

WikiTok proved a lot of people would rather scroll Wikipedia than another video feed. It is a free, open-source web page with one job: show a random article, then another. WanderWiki does the same thing as a native iPhone and iPad app, and adds the parts a random feed leaves out: topic feeds, Today in History, saves that open offline, and widgets.

How does WanderWiki compare to WikiTok?

Both start from a random Wikipedia article you swipe past. WanderWiki builds more of an app around it.

What it is
WanderWiki: A native app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 18.2 or later), installed from the App Store.

WikiTok: A web app at wikitok.io that runs in any browser. You can add it to your Home Screen from Safari.
The feed
WanderWiki: Random, Browse for one of 16 topics, For You built from the topics you pick, and Today in History. Where next under each article offers three related ones.

WikiTok: Random articles only, with an image, title, and extract on each card.
Saving and offline
WanderWiki: Saved articles download in full, open without a connection, and sort into folders. The reader remembers where you stopped.

WikiTok: Likes live in your browser's storage and can be exported as JSON. Reading needs a connection.
Languages
WanderWiki: 32 Wikipedia editions.

WikiTok: 41 Wikipedia editions. WikiTok covers more.
Price
WanderWiki: Free for 20 articles a day. Premium is {P_MONTHLY@wanderwiki}/month, {P_YEARLY@wanderwiki}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@wanderwiki} once.

WikiTok: Free, and open source under the MIT license.
Privacy
WanderWiki: No account and no ads. The app carries no analytics and does no tracking, and your reading stays on your device. See the Privacy Manifest.

WikiTok: No account, no ads, no backend. The site loads Vercel's page analytics.

Who should pick WanderWiki vs WikiTok?

Pick WanderWiki if…
  • You want an App Store app with widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • You want topic feeds and Today in History next to the random deck
  • You save articles for a flight or a commute and need them to open offline
  • You like having an article read aloud while you walk

Questions

FAQ

Is there a WikiTok app for iPhone?
The original WikiTok, built by Isaac Gemal in February 2025, is a web app at wikitok.io with no App Store version. In Safari you can add it to your Home Screen, where it opens like an app. Apps named WikiTok on the App Store come from other developers, and the project's README says it is not affiliated with them. If you want the swipe-through-Wikipedia idea as a native app, WanderWiki runs on iPhone and iPad.
What is a good WikiTok alternative for iPhone?
WanderWiki is the closest native alternative. It keeps WikiTok's core, a feed of random Wikipedia articles you move through with a swipe, and adds a For You mode built from topics you pick, Today in History from Wikipedia's On This Day feed, saved articles that open offline and sort into folders, read-aloud, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. It has no ads and no account.
Is WikiTok free? Is WanderWiki free?
WikiTok is free and its code is open source under the MIT license. WanderWiki is free for 20 articles a day, covering Random and topic browsing, with saving and search included. Premium unlocks For You, Today in History, and unlimited swiping for {P_MONTHLY@wanderwiki} a month, {P_YEARLY@wanderwiki} a year, or {P_LIFETIME@wanderwiki} once.
Which Wikipedia languages do WikiTok and WanderWiki support?
WikiTok's language picker covers 41 Wikipedia editions, or 48 options if you count script and regional variants such as the Chinese ones. WanderWiki carries 32 editions. WikiTok covers more, including Bengali, Persian, Urdu, Esperanto, and Basque, which WanderWiki does not have.
Can I read WikiTok articles offline?
No. WikiTok loads articles live from Wikipedia. Liked articles are kept in your browser's local storage as a title, short extract, and link, and you can export them as a JSON file. WanderWiki downloads the article's full text when you save it, so it opens without a connection, and you can sort saves into folders.
Do WikiTok and WanderWiki use a recommendation algorithm?
WikiTok shows random articles, and its creator has said he turned down requests to add an algorithm. WanderWiki's Random mode is random too. Its For You mode starts from the topics you choose and adjusts when you press and hold a card to see less of a topic or hide an article. Those choices stay on your device, and neither app keeps a reading profile on a server.
