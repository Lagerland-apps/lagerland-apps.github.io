A WikiScroll alternative
without the ads.

WikiScroll and WanderWiki started from the same idea: if you are going to scroll, scroll Wikipedia. The feeds look alike. Random articles, a this-day-in-history mode, topics, saves, widgets. What differs is how each app pays for itself. WikiScroll is free with ads. WanderWiki has a free daily allowance, then a small subscription or one payment, and no ads or tracking at any point.

How does WanderWiki compare to WikiScroll?

Two swipe-through-Wikipedia apps with similar feeds and different business models.

How it's paid for
WanderWiki: Free for 20 articles a day. Plus is $1.99/month, $8.99/year, or $24.99 once. No ads.

WikiScroll: Free with ads, plus premium in-app purchases.
Privacy label
WanderWiki: Data Not Collected. See the Privacy Manifest.

WikiScroll: Data Used to Track You (identifiers, usage data), with data linked to you for advertising and analytics.
The feed
WanderWiki: Random, For You from 16 interest topics, topic browsing, and Today in History.

WikiScroll: Random articles, a personalised feed from topics you choose, and Timeline Mode for this day in history.
Saving and reading
WanderWiki: Saved articles open offline and sort into folders. Articles can be read aloud.

WikiScroll: Like and save articles to come back to.
Languages
WanderWiki: 32 Wikipedia editions, listed in the app.

WikiScroll: Lets you choose a reading language; its listing doesn't say how many Wikipedia editions. The interface comes in 8 languages.
Device support
WanderWiki: iPhone and iPad, iOS 18.2 or later.

WikiScroll: iPhone, iOS 15 or later, so it reaches older phones.

Who should pick WanderWiki vs WikiScroll?

Pick WanderWiki if…
  • You want a Wikipedia feed with no ads and no tracking
  • You save articles for a flight or a commute and want them to open offline
  • You like folders for your reading list and having articles read aloud
  • You read in a language other than English and want to know it's supported before you pay
Stick with WikiScroll if…
  • Your iPhone can't run iOS 18.2
  • You'd rather see ads than pay anything
  • You already have it set up the way you like

Questions

FAQ

What is a WikiScroll alternative without ads?
WanderWiki covers the same ground as WikiScroll, a swipe feed of Wikipedia articles with a this-day-in-history mode, topics and widgets, with no ads on any tier. It is free for 20 articles a day. WanderWiki Plus, which unlocks For You, Today in History and unlimited swiping, costs $1.99 a month, $8.99 a year, or $24.99 once.
How are WikiScroll and WanderWiki different?
The core feed is similar. WikiScroll is ad-supported and sells premium options in the app. WanderWiki has no ads and collects no data. WanderWiki also stores saved articles on the device so they open offline, sorts them into folders, reads articles aloud, and lists exactly which 32 Wikipedia editions it supports. WikiScroll supports older iPhones, from iOS 15, while WanderWiki needs iOS 18.2.
How much does WikiScroll cost compared with WanderWiki?
WikiScroll is free to download and shows ads. On 25 October 2026 its App Store listing showed in-app purchases of $4.99 for a weekly subscription, $24.99 for a yearly subscription, $39.99 for lifetime premium, and a $24.99 lifetime offer. Check the App Store for current pricing and what premium includes. WanderWiki is free for 20 articles a day, and WanderWiki Plus is $1.99 a month, $8.99 a year, or $24.99 once.
Does WikiScroll track you?
WikiScroll's App Store privacy label says identifiers and usage data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, and it lists data linked to you for third-party advertising and analytics. That is common for an ad-supported app. WanderWiki's App Store label says Data Not Collected, and its privacy manifest declares no tracking domains.
Does WikiScroll have Today in History?
Yes. WikiScroll's Timeline Mode shows historical events from this day in the past. WanderWiki's Today in History reads Wikipedia's own On This Day feed in five categories: Selected, Events, Births, Deaths and Holidays. In WanderWiki it is part of WanderWiki Plus.
Which one works on an older iPhone?
WikiScroll. It requires iOS 15 or later, so it installs on iPhones that can't run newer versions of iOS. WanderWiki requires iOS 18.2 or later on iPhone and iPad. If your phone is stuck below iOS 18.2, WikiScroll is the one of the two you can use.
