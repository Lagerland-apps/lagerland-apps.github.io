App Store Connect on iPhone · Companion, not a replacement

Already using the
App Store Connect app?

Apple's App Store Connect app is free, made by the company that runs the store, and rated 4.7 from about 15,000 ratings. It is where you check review status, move testers and builds around in TestFlight, reply to reviews and get notified when something changes. It is a management tool, and a good one. AppMeta Pulse does a narrower job: the daily look at money and subscriptions across every app you ship, in Apple's own report terms, from an app that has no code for builds, pricing or metadata. You will probably keep Apple's app installed too.

How does AppMeta Pulse compare to the App Store Connect app?

They overlap on two things, sales figures and review replies. Everything else splits cleanly: Apple's app manages releases, Pulse reads reports.

What each app is for
AppMeta Pulse: A dashboard. It reads Apple's daily Sales and Trends reports, the subscription reports, App Store impressions and page views, and customer reviews. Its only writes are review replies you send and the analytics report requests Apple needs before it prepares impressions data.

App Store Connect app: A management app. Its App Store listing covers app status and App Store submissions, TestFlight tester groups, builds and feedback, sales and trends, ratings and reviews, marketing assets for launches and featuring, and notifications.

If you need to act on a release from your phone, Apple's app is the one that can.
Price and sign-in
AppMeta Pulse: Free trial, then from $0.99 a month. You connect an App Store Connect API key with the Admin role, its Issuer ID and Key ID, and your Vendor Number. The key stays in the iOS Keychain.

App Store Connect app: Free. You sign in with your Apple Account, so there is no key to create, and team members can use it with their own App Store Connect access.

Apple's app is cheaper and quicker to set up. That is a real advantage.
Money and subscription numbers
AppMeta Pulse: Proceeds by default, or Sales with one setting, per app and across the portfolio. MRR, active subscriptions and trials from Apple's Subscription report, downloads net of refunds, and period-over-period trends, with amounts converted into one display currency.

App Store Connect app: Sales and trends information, according to its listing. The listing doesn't describe subscription metrics such as MRR; on the web, App Store Connect's Analytics has a subscriptions dashboard.

Both read the same Apple data. The difference is how much of it each puts on one screen.
Notifications and widgets
AppMeta Pulse: No push notifications. 12 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for proceeds, sales, MRR, trends, subscriptions and reviews, refreshed from Apple's daily reports.

App Store Connect app: Notifications for app status changes, new reviews, tester feedback and featuring. Its listing doesn't mention widgets.

If you want to hear the moment App Review approves a build, Apple's app wins this category.
Review replies
AppMeta Pulse: Read and reply to customer reviews, with optional reply drafts written on-device by Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it.

App Store Connect app: View and respond to your latest ratings and reviews.
Platforms and privacy label
AppMeta Pulse: iPhone app, iOS 17 or later; the App Store also lists it for Apple silicon Macs and Apple Vision Pro. Privacy label: Data Not Collected.

App Store Connect app: iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Its privacy label lists data linked to you, such as contact info and identifiers, and data not linked to you, such as usage data and diagnostics.

Apple already runs your App Store Connect account, so its label sits inside a relationship you have anyway. Pulse's label matters because it is a third party reading your sales.

Who should pick AppMeta Pulse, and who should stick with Apple's app?

Pick AppMeta Pulse if…
  • You open App Store Connect mostly to see how yesterday sold, not to ship something
  • You sell subscriptions and want MRR, active subscriptions and trials on one screen
  • You ship several apps and want proceeds per app and for the whole portfolio
  • You want widgets on the Home Screen or Lock Screen instead of opening an app
  • You want an app that cannot touch builds, pricing or metadata, even by accident
Stick with the App Store Connect app if…
  • You need TestFlight, submissions and app status from your phone
  • Push notifications for App Review and new reviews are how you keep up
  • You don't want to create an API key or pay for a second app
  • You need an iPad layout
  • Apple's sales and trends view already tells you what you need to know

Questions

FAQ

Is AppMeta Pulse a replacement for the App Store Connect app?
For most developers, no. Apple's App Store Connect app manages the business: app status and submissions, TestFlight, review replies, marketing assets and notifications. AppMeta Pulse is a dashboard for the money side, with proceeds, sales, MRR, subscriptions, trials and downloads across your apps, and no controls for builds, pricing or metadata. Many people keep both.
Does the App Store Connect app show proceeds and subscriptions?
Its App Store listing says it shows sales and trends information. It doesn't describe subscription metrics such as MRR. On the web, App Store Connect's Analytics has a subscriptions dashboard. AppMeta Pulse reads Apple's daily Sales and Subscription reports and shows proceeds or sales, MRR, active subscriptions and trials for each app.
Why does AppMeta Pulse need an API key when Apple's app doesn't?
Apple's app signs in with your Apple Account because Apple runs App Store Connect. Third-party apps reach the same data through the official App Store Connect API, which uses a key you create. AppMeta Pulse asks for a key with the Admin role plus your Vendor Number, keeps the key in the iOS Keychain, and calls Apple's servers directly.
Can AppMeta Pulse submit builds or manage TestFlight?
No. AppMeta Pulse has no code for builds, submissions, TestFlight, pricing or metadata. The only things it sends to App Store Connect are review replies you write and the analytics report requests Apple needs before preparing impressions data. For releases, use Apple's App Store Connect app or the website.
Which app has notifications and widgets?
Apple's App Store Connect app sends notifications for app status changes, new reviews, tester feedback and featuring; its listing doesn't mention widgets. AppMeta Pulse has no push notifications and offers 12 Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for proceeds, sales, MRR, trends, subscriptions and reviews.
How much do they cost?
Apple's App Store Connect app is free. AppMeta Pulse has a free trial, then costs from $0.99 a month; check the App Store listing for the current plans in your currency.
