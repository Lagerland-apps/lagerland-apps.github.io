When should I pick one sec instead?

Pick one sec if any of these matter more to you than a lock that only movement lifts:

  • The breathing pause works for you. A moment of friction is often all it takes to close the app, and it is a gentler approach than a lock.
  • You want a range of interruptions (breathe, reflect, wait) rather than one activity requirement.
  • You need Android as well as iPhone. one sec is on both; EarnLock is an iPhone app.
  • You don't want your screen time tied to movement. Even EarnLock's free tier needs step data from Apple Health.

EarnLock is for someone who has tapped through every pause and wants unlocking to cost a walk. If halfway is all you manage, you still get an hour.

What is the best alternative to one sec?
If the pause has stopped working for you, EarnLock is a one sec alternative with a lock instead of a delay. The apps you pick stay locked until you reach a daily activity goal measured by Apple Health. Halfway opens them for an hour, the full goal opens them until midnight, and a math-gated emergency unlock covers real needs once a day. Locking one app is free for good, and there is no account. If a pause is enough, one sec is the lighter tool.
How is EarnLock different from one sec?
one sec adds a breath before an app opens and then lets you continue. EarnLock keeps the app locked until Apple Health shows you've moved enough that day, and a locked app shows exactly what's left, such as "2,026 steps until unlock". EarnLock also starts with a Shadow Week that blocks nothing and counts how often you would have been blocked, so you see your real pattern before the first lock. one sec runs on iPhone and Android; EarnLock is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app in Premium.

Does one sec block apps or just pause them?
one sec pauses them. Its breathing screen asks you to reconsider, and you can continue if you still want to. That is the design, and for many people it works. EarnLock keeps the app locked until your daily activity goal is met, with one emergency unlock per day behind a two-digit math problem.
Is EarnLock private?
Yes. EarnLock has no account, makes no network connections of its own, and includes no analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs. Apple Health is read on your iPhone and never written to or sent anywhere, and Apple's Screen Time picker gives EarnLock opaque tokens instead of the names of your apps. EarnLock is built for limiting your own iPhone and has no parent controls.
Can EarnLock block the same apps one sec can?
For apps, yes. EarnLock locks any app or whole category you can choose in Apple's Screen Time picker: one on the free tier, as many as you like with Premium. The difference is what happens when you open one.
