What is a good Image Size alternative for iPhone and Mac?
MediaKit suits people who want more than resizing. MediaKit resizes and compresses photos for free with no ads, and with Pro it converts to HEIC, JPG, PNG, TIFF, GIF, or WebP, strips metadata, runs OCR, and removes backgrounds with Apple Vision. Can MediaKit resize images to exact dimensions like Image Size?
Yes, in pixels. MediaKit's Image Resize is one of the five tools that stay free for good, one file at a time. Image Compress, also free, shrinks a photo to a quality level or a target file size. Image Size does resizing very well, with multitouch crop and print units (mm, cm, in), which MediaKit doesn't offer. MediaKit's strength is what comes after: compress, convert, or clean up the result in the same app.

No to both. MediaKit has no ads, and every tool runs on your iPhone or Mac, so your photos never leave the device. The app makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics SDK. Image Size also processes on the device, but its free tier is ad-supported.
Why pick MediaKit over Image Size?
Pick it if resizing is only the first step. If you only ever resize photos, Image Size is a clean, capable single-purpose tool.
