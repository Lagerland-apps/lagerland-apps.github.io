How does Mockly compare to Picsew?

They belong to different categories. Picsew stitches screenshots on the phone, and Mockly builds App Store screenshots on the Mac.

Platform
Mockly: A Mac app for macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Picsew: iPhone and iPad, designed for touch.
Device frames
Mockly: Six 3D models (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad Pro 11-inch and 12.9-inch, MacBook Pro 16-inch, Apple Watch Ultra, and Apple Watch Series 11), with rotation on three axes, three render styles, and adjustable light.

Picsew: Flat device frames overlaid on screenshots.
Localization
Mockly: All 50 App Store languages in one project, with AI Translate on your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google key, or a CSV for a translator.

Picsew: Not built for this. You'd edit text for each language by hand.
App Store Connect upload
Mockly: Upload from the app with your App Store Connect API key, for the languages you pick.

Picsew: Export to Files or Photos and upload separately.

Who should pick Mockly vs Picsew?

Pick Mockly if…
  • You're making App Store screenshots for a real release
  • You ship for more than one device, such as iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Watch
  • You localize your listing into more than one language
  • You want to upload the finished set to App Store Connect from the same app
  • You want to pay once with no subscription
Stick with Picsew if…
  • You stitch long screenshots on iPhone for social media or chat
  • You don't need 3D devices or App Store-sized output
  • You work entirely on the phone with no Mac in the loop
  • Picsew already covers your screenshot needs

FAQ

What is the best Mac alternative to Picsew for App Store screenshots?
Mockly is a Mac app built for App Store screenshots. It puts your screenshots on six 3D device models, keeps all 50 App Store languages in one project with AI translation through your own key, exports PNG or HEIC at exact artboard sizes, and uploads to App Store Connect. That workflow is awkward in a phone app made for stitching.
Does Mockly render real 3D device models?
Yes. Mockly has six 3D models: iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad Pro 11-inch and 12.9-inch, MacBook Pro 16-inch, Apple Watch Ultra, and Apple Watch Series 11. You set each one's rotation on X, Y, and Z, its scale, and its offset, render it Realistic, Clay, or UI Only, and switch between perspective and orthographic projection.
Can Mockly translate screenshot text for App Store localisations?
Yes. AI Translate translates the text blocks into the languages you pick with your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google key, and you can export a CSV for a translator and import it back. Each of the 50 App Store languages can also carry its own screenshots and layout. AI output is machine translation, so read it before you ship.
Is Mockly a subscription?
No. Mockly is a one-time {P_APP@mockly} purchase with no subscription, and every editor feature comes with it.
