FAQ

What is the best Permute alternative for Mac?
MediaKit is the closest native iPhone and Mac alternative, with a wider toolset. Permute is a polished converter for audio, video, and images; MediaKit also works on-device and has 152 tools that cover PDF and archive work too, in one app.
Does MediaKit run on iPhone too, unlike Permute?
Yes. MediaKit is a Universal Purchase: buy it once and use it natively on both iPhone and Mac. Permute is a Mac-only desktop app, so MediaKit's iPhone version helps if you also convert files on your phone.
Does MediaKit do everything Permute does?
For common cases, yes: drag-and-drop video (H.264, HEVC, AV1), audio, and image conversion, on-device. Permute is mature and focused; MediaKit's advantage is breadth, with PDF and archive tools added.
Is MediaKit on-device like Permute?
Yes. Both run entirely on your device with no upload, and MediaKit was built so you never upload files to a conversion website.
How does MediaKit's pricing compare to Permute?
MediaKit is free to start; Pro is {P_MONTHLY@mediakit}/month, {P_YEARLY@mediakit}/year, or {P_LIFETIME@mediakit} lifetime. Permute is typically a one-time purchase (~$14.99) or via Setapp. If you only convert audio, video, and images, Permute's price is hard to beat. If you want PDF and archives too, MediaKit covers more.
Why pick MediaKit over Permute?
It covers more in one app: you can transcode a clip, compress a PDF, convert a HEIC, extract audio, and unzip an archive without switching tools. Permute is very good but sticks to audio, video, and images.
Is MediaKit a modern native app?
Yes. It is a native iPhone and Mac app with drag-and-drop and a unified inspector, made for current iOS and macOS. Permute is also Mac-native and well regarded for its clean design.
