A ShogiQuest alternative
for when you're tired of losing the same way.

ShogiQuest is fast and light, with a human opponent a tap away. When five games in a row slip away in the same way, though, working out why is left to you. Shogiful plays you against 40 engine opponents and reviews every game on your device. Each mistake gets a card that names its type, such as a hanging piece, a drop mistake, or a missed tsume, and the next day's training starts from your top weakness. It runs on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with no account and no ads.

How does Shogiful compare to ShogiQuest?

ShogiQuest is for playing a lot of games; Shogiful is for understanding them.

What it is
Shogiful: A single-player training app: 40 engine opponents, a review of every game, and training aimed at your weaknesses.

ShogiQuest: Lightweight online quick-play with instant rated matches against humans.
After the game ends
Shogiful: A move-by-move review with a card for each mistake, and Train These Weaknesses to practice the ones you repeat.

ShogiQuest: The next game.
Offline support
Shogiful: Play, analysis, and training run on your device. Buying Premium and Game Center need a connection.

ShogiQuest: Online only; needs a connection and an opponent.
Ads & account
Shogiful: No account. No ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

ShogiQuest: Free with ads and optional in-app purchases; account-based rating.
Kanji barrier
Shogiful: Three piece styles: Traditional kanji, International (kanji with movement arrows), and Chess (chess-style pieces, no kanji). Moves show in Western (P-7f) or Japanese notation.

ShogiQuest: Simple piece graphics; no structured help for learning the pieces.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best ShogiQuest alternative for improving?
Shogiful is made for improving. YaneuraOu reviews every game on your device, each mistake gets a card in one of 12 shogi mistake types, and Today's Training builds each session around your top weakness. Play, analysis, and training work offline with no account, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
How is Shogiful different from ShogiQuest?
ShogiQuest is a quick-play arena: tap, get matched, play, repeat, and it does that well. It doesn't answer the question "why do I keep losing?" Shogiful does. After every game you get a move-by-move review with a card for each mistake (the drop fork you missed, the promotion you shouldn't have taken, the edge pawn that weakened your castle), and Train These Weaknesses opens practice on the patterns you repeat.
Does Shogiful work offline, unlike ShogiQuest?
Yes. The YaneuraOu engine, game analysis, mistake cards, tsume problems, and all 40 opponents run on your device. Only buying Premium and Game Center need a connection. ShogiQuest needs a connection to find you an opponent, so on a flight or in the subway Shogiful is the one that works.
Can I play shogi without knowing kanji in Shogiful?
Yes. The Chess piece style uses chess-style pieces with no kanji, and the International style keeps the kanji and adds arrows that show how each piece moves. Switch to Traditional pieces in Settings whenever you like. Shogiful is made for the player who got curious about shogi and bounced off the characters.
How much does Shogiful cost compared to ShogiQuest?
Shogiful is free to download, and play is never limited: you get unlimited games against all 40 opponents, which unlock as you win, plus 2 game analyses, 3 training puzzles, and 2 coach hints a day, with no ads. Premium removes those limits for {P_MONTHLY@shogiful}/month or {P_YEARLY@shogiful}/year, each with a {TRIAL@shogiful}, or {P_LIFETIME@shogiful} once, and Family Sharing is included. ShogiQuest is free to play with ads and optional in-app purchases. If all you want is free online games, ShogiQuest does that; Shogiful is the one you pay for when you want to improve.
Can I use both ShogiQuest and Shogiful?
Yes, they pair well. Get your fast human games on ShogiQuest, then use Shogiful to understand the recurring mistakes and train them. Shogiful's ladder of 40 opponents is a quiet place to test a fix before you take it back online.
