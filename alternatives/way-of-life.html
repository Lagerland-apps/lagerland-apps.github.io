Looking for a
Way of Life alternative?

Way of Life turns habits into color-coded chains you try to keep unbroken: green for done, red for missed. That works for people who are motivated by chains. If a broken chain feels like a small failure, Taskful Day works the other way. You plan the day, finish what you can, and whatever is left moves to today the next time you open the app. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

How do Taskful Day and Way of Life compare?

Core mechanic
Taskful Day: Plan the day and finish what you can. Unfinished tasks carry forward to today the next time you open the app, and a recurring task that slips keeps its original rhythm.

Way of Life: Yes/no/skip chains; missing breaks the chain.
How progress is shown
Taskful Day: Analytics shows completion rate, trends, categories, and a current and best streak, with the footer "This is just a pattern, not a judgment." The Journal records how each day felt.

Way of Life: Color-coded chains and trends as motivation.
Free tier
Taskful Day: Up to 50 tasks in total (deleting tasks doesn't reset the count), one workspace, the Journal, and weekly Analytics. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro free with no card.

Way of Life: Free tier limits the number of habits.
Privacy and platforms
Taskful Day: No account, ads, or analytics SDKs. Tasks stay on the device unless you turn on iCloud Sync (Pro), which uses your own private iCloud. Runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch.

Way of Life: iPhone only, though the iPhone app also runs on Macs with Apple silicon. Standard app disclosures.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Way of Life alternative without chains?
If chains work against you, Taskful Day plans the day instead. You finish what you can, unfinished tasks carry forward to today the next time you open the app, and recurring tasks keep their rhythm when they carry over. There is no chain of green and red marks to keep, though Analytics does show a current and best streak as one stat. It needs no account.
Does Taskful Day use chains or trends like Way of Life?
No chains. Analytics shows trends: your completion rate, a completion trend, categories, and a current and best streak, with monthly, quarterly, and yearly ranges in Pro. Nothing resets your plan when a day slips. A missed task moves to today, and the Today list shows no overdue badge.
Why switch from Way of Life to Taskful Day?
Way of Life's color chains motivate people who respond to visible streaks. If a broken chain demoralizes you, or you want to plan your day as well as track habits, Taskful Day fits better. It also has iPad and Apple Watch apps and a native Mac app, with no account or analytics SDKs.
Does Taskful Day limit habits on the free tier like Way of Life?
It limits tasks rather than habits. The free version lets you create up to 50 tasks in total, and deleting tasks doesn't reset the count. Pro removes the limit and adds iCloud Sync, the visual day views, the focus timer, and 21 Home Screen widgets. Every new install gets 7 days of Pro free with no card.
Does Taskful Day require an account?
No. Taskful Day works without an account and carries no ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs. Your tasks stay on the device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, a Pro feature that uses your own private iCloud.
Is Taskful Day available on Mac?
Yes. Taskful Day runs on Mac with macOS 14 or later, with a toolbar, menus, keyboard shortcuts, and desktop widgets, and it is free to download there as on iPhone and iPad. With Pro, iCloud Sync keeps your Mac and your other devices on the same plan. It also has an Apple Watch app, which needs the iPhone app. Way of Life is an iPhone app that also runs on Macs with Apple silicon, with no iPad or Apple Watch version (checked 29 October 2026).
