Looking for a
Way of Life alternative?

Way of Life turns habits into color-coded chains you try to keep unbroken: green for done, red for missed. That works for people who are motivated by chains. If a broken chain feels like a small failure to you, Taskful Day works the other way. You plan a realistic day, finish what you can, and a missed day doesn't count against you. It is native on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best Way of Life alternative without chains?
Taskful Day. Way of Life tracks habits as color-coded yes/no/skip chains. Taskful Day has no chain: you plan a realistic day, finish what you can, and a missed day counts for nothing either way. It needs no account.
Does Taskful Day use chains or trends like Way of Life?
No. There are no chains to keep and no penalty for breaking one. It's built around finishing a realistic day, and when life interrupts you reschedule without guilt.
Why switch from Way of Life to Taskful Day?
If a broken chain demoralises you, or you want to plan your day as well as chart habits, Taskful Day suits you better, and it adds a native Mac app and zero tracking.
Does Taskful Day limit habits on the free tier like Way of Life?
Taskful Day's core flow is permanently free with no chain cap. Way of Life's free tier limits the number of habits before upgrading.
Does Taskful Day require an account?
No. It has no account, tracking, or ads. Data stays on-device and syncs via your own iCloud when enabled.
Is Taskful Day available on Mac?
Yes. It is native on iPhone, iPad, and Mac under one Universal Purchase with iCloud sync. Way of Life is an iPhone and iPad app.
