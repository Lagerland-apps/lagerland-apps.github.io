On-device AI · No account · Works offline

A VetKeep alternative
that reads your vet documents without the cloud.

VetKeep and Pawza both let you photograph a vet document and fill in the record from it, so the difference is where the work happens. VetKeep is a cloud service, with an account, your records on its servers, and a conversational AI chat that answers questions about your pet's history. Pawza reads the text on your device, and with Pawza Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the fields on the device too. Records stay on your devices with no account and no server of ours, Pawza works offline, and you can pay once for Pro instead of subscribing.

How does Pawza compare to VetKeep?

The headline feature is the same; where the data goes is not.

Where the AI runs
Pawza: On your device. Apple Vision reads the text on every supported device; Apple Intelligence fills in the fields with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device (the app names iPhone 15 Pro or newer). Documents are not uploaded.

VetKeep: In the cloud, under an account, which powers cross-device access and chat.
Account & storage
Pawza: No account and no server of ours. Data stays on the device, with optional sync through your own private iCloud.

VetKeep: Account required; records stored in its encrypted cloud.
Conversational record chat
Pawza: Not offered. Records feed reminders, the weight chart, the Since last vet visit card, and the Vet Visit PDF (Pro).

VetKeep: Its signature feature: ask questions and get answers from your pet's history.
Offline use
Pawza: Logging, records, scanning, reminders, and PDFs work with no connection; only iCloud sync and purchases need one.

VetKeep: Cloud AI depends on a connection.
Pricing
Pawza: Free for one pet. Pro is {P_MONTHLY@pawza}/mo or {P_YEARLY@pawza}/yr, each starting with a {TRIAL@pawza}, or {P_LIFETIME@pawza} once. Family Sharing on every Pro plan.

VetKeep: Subscription (Pro). No one-time lifetime option.
Platforms
Pawza: One universal app for iPhone and iPad.

VetKeep: iPhone; other platforms unconfirmed.

Questions

FAQ

What is the best VetKeep alternative for iPhone?
Pawza, for owners who want AI document scanning without the cloud. Pawza reads the text on your device, and with Pro on iOS 26 and an eligible device, Apple Intelligence fills in the record on the device too. Records stay on your devices with no account, Pawza works offline, and it runs on iPhone and iPad. VetKeep is a cloud service with an account and a conversational AI chat over your records.
Does Pawza send my vet documents to the cloud like VetKeep?
No. Pawza's text recognition and Apple Intelligence extraction run on your device, and documents are not uploaded for processing. Records stay on the device with optional sync through your own private iCloud. VetKeep is cloud-based, which makes its cross-device chat possible and means your data leaves the device.
Does Pawza have a chat that answers questions about my pet's records?
No. This is the main thing VetKeep has that Pawza doesn't. VetKeep's conversational AI chat works because records live in its cloud. Pawza puts records to use in reminders, the weight chart, the Since last vet visit card, and the Vet Visit PDF (Pro). If a cloud chat is the feature you want most, VetKeep offers it.
Is Pawza cheaper than VetKeep?
Pawza offers a one-time option VetKeep doesn't. Pawza is free for one pet; Pro is {P_MONTHLY@pawza}/month or {P_YEARLY@pawza}/year, each starting with a {TRIAL@pawza}, or {P_LIFETIME@pawza} once, with Family Sharing on every Pro plan. VetKeep is subscription-based.
Does Pawza work offline like a local app?
Yes. Logging, records, scanning, reminders, widgets, and PDFs all work offline. Pawza needs a connection only for iCloud sync and purchases. VetKeep's cloud AI depends on a connection.
