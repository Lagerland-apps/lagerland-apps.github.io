## distinctness

Reading all 63 styles through one lens: first viewports are mostly distinct worlds, but resources and structure converge. The structural sameness (one section order relabelled per surface, and one dominant order across the board) is the largest failure; fix it before the owner sees the cards. Every cross-surface collision here is conditional (it only bites if both styles win), and the fixes are chosen so that any combination the owner picks stays distinct. KEEP-MAP, one owner per contested resource. Worlds: commentary big board → shogiful-b; multilingual leaflet → appmeta-c; prepress proof with sign-off box → appmeta-b; wayfinding route + Signika → earnlock-b; metro line maps → mediakit-b. ## feasibility

Method. Every face was checked against the npm registry: npm view for existence, then npm pack --dry-run for the actual latin woff2 files, weights, italics and sizes. Glyph coverage for DSEG7, DSEG14, Doto, Tiny5, B612 and Handjet was checked with fontTools. About 260 text/ground, accent and focus pairs were computed with a WCAG 2.x script.

Fonts: every one of the roughly 105 named faces exists on Fontsource with the weights and italics named. Every variable file named exists, with sizes within about 3 KB of the cards' claims (Recursive casl 106.2 KB, Saira full 96.6, Literata opsz 107.5, Hubot wdth 90.9, Archivo wdth 88.0, Pathway standard 95.2, Bricolage opsz 75.1, Handjet full 18.1). The only glyph-coverage failure is DSEG7 Classic: no comma, '$', '·' or '%'.

Payload: every latin stack is under 160 KB. The heaviest are wanderwiki-a at about 142 KB with its script slices, liftlog-a at about 130, chessful-a at about 129 and pawza-b at about 128. The CJK and complex-script styles (all xiangqiful, wanderwiki-c, and the kanji in shogiful a/c) only fit with committed subsets or latin-only @font-face rules.

Contrast: the cards' claims were mostly accurate to within about 0.5. The real failures are listed as issues: gymlogger-x-b red 3.88, taskful-day-c Orbit links 1.70, the observa-c yarn steps, and focus rings on paper in pawza-a and chessful-c.

Public Sans: observa-b and soon-c keep, gymlogger-x-a changes. Sofia family: mediakit-a keeps, pawza-a and soon-b change. Doto: chessful-a and soon-c keep, earnlock-a changes. After the fixes, no face appears on more than two surfaces. Every replacement face named here (Mohave, Asap, Anton, Kumbh Sans, Teko, Ubuntu Sans, Reddit Sans and Reddit Mono, Roboto Flex, Smooch Sans, Rethink Sans, Hind, DSEG14 Classic, Gantari, Commissioner, Murecho, Petrona, Wix Madefor Text, Comic Neue) was verified on Fontsource and is unused elsewhere in the catalogue.

Worlds and palettes. Leaflet: appmeta-c keeps it, mockly-c changes. Press proof: appmeta-b keeps the sign-off box, mockly-b changes its conversion device. The cobalt drench appears four times (chessful-c, earnlock-c, millrace-b, pawza-a): pawza-a keeps it, and the other three move to violet #3B2A8F, icon black #17191A and walnut #3B2616.

Cross-cutting rules every build should adopt:
(1) On mobile the badge's bottom edge sits at y560 or above in the 390x844 plan.
(2) Committed image derivatives: no 1024px icon or 1206x2622 PNG is ever served raw.
(3) scroll-padding sized to the sticky bars, and one sticky element per edge.
(4) Latin-only @font-face plus metric-matched fallbacks for the giant H1s.
(5) No unbounded looping motion. Every loop stops within 5s, or has a pause.

## truth-contract

Truth lens, checked against _apps/*.md, _data/transparency.yml, _data/testimonials.yml, _data/app_enrichment.yml, the journal and the alternatives pages. The catalogue is well grounded overall. Most numbers the cards cite trace to front matter, screenshots or journal posts, e.g. Millrace's 1.31% / 280 strategies / chapter budgets, EarnLock's 2,026 and 3,412 steps, AllPaid's sample bills, AppMeta Pulse's 992,55 € portfolio, Chessful's 32 languages, Pawza's 43 languages and ZIPFoundation, and MediaKit's Unrar.swift. No style shows star ratings. Every testimonial used is real (_data/testimonials.yml) and shown without stars. Prices match price.value and plans everywhere except two invented plan labels (EarnLock b's 'most walkers', Soon b's 'Best value'). Several styles caught real content errors and should keep those fixes: the GymLogger X styles flag the Strong page's false 'Mac-and-iPhone' line; the WanderWiki styles flag the faulty On This Day cards and the $0.61/$0.76 per-language mismatch; the Taskful Day styles flag wrong screenshot alts. Recurring risks to fix at build: (1) the ai-canonical block recast as a short spec table (Chessful, MediaKit, Mockly, Shogiful); (2) hand-typed FAQ and feature counts that are wrong (AppMeta Pulse 14, AppMeta 15, Millrace 11, EarnLock features 10), so always loop the data; (3) hero trust lines with no /transparency/ link; (4) existing front-matter contradictions the new layouts would set side by side (the GymLogger X quick_answer vs its plateau rule, AppMeta Pulse 'half the price of AppStats', the RightSplit cent); (5) Tare modelled as a live listing. Front-matter popularity labels such as LiftLog's 'Most chosen', RightSplit's 'Most chosen' and GymLogger X's '~1 in 3 pick this' are allowed because they are in the source. Several styles enlarge them (pills, circles, stamps), so the owner should confirm them against App Store Connect before they are amplified. Typeface and world collisions from the face census, such as the Atkinson Hyperlegible Next text-face monoculture across 7 surfaces and display-face pairs like Dela Gothic One (earnlock a / shogiful b), are outside this lens and left to the distinctness critic.
