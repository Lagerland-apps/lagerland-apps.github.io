The character question, answered honestly

Every xiangqi piece is a flat disc with a Chinese character on it, and both sides use different characters for most of the same pieces — the red general is 帥, the black one 將; red's elephant is 相, black's 象; red's soldier 兵, black's 卒. For a newcomer that can be as many as fourteen characters to learn before the first move. The good news, and it's better news than in shogi, is that the major xiangqi apps have already solved it: Xiangqi.com has an International piece style, PyChess has internationalized pieces, and Best XiangQi has western symbols. Xiangqiful (ours) adds a hybrid set that shows each symbol with its character, plus lessons that introduce the characters one piece at a time, so you can move to the traditional set when you're ready. Whichever app you choose, learn the characters eventually: books, diagrams and every wooden set in a park use them.

A sensible path from zero to online games

1. Learn the moves against a patient computer
Start offline and untimed. The rules that catch chess players out — the cannon's jump, the blocked horse, the palace, the generals that may not face each other, stalemate as a loss — are easier to absorb when nobody is waiting on you.
2. Build a stock of mating patterns
Xiangqi's native puzzle is the consecutive-check problem, where every move must give check. A few classical shapes finish most games. Xiangqiful has 902 of them; Xiangqi.com and PyChess have puzzle sections too.
3. Understand your losses
The step most players skip. An engine line tells you the better move; it doesn't tell you that you keep handing an enemy cannon its screen. Plain-language review does. (This is the job Xiangqiful was built for — we own this opinion.)
4. Then go online
Xiangqi.com for the English-speaking community, PyChess for free open-source play, Club Xiangqi for a bilingual club, PlayOK for a quick game as a guest. Timed games against people are the deep end — far more fun once you stop losing your chariot to a cannon fork.

FAQ

What is the best xiangqi app for iPhone?
It depends on the job. Xiangqi.com is the best free all-rounder in English; Xiangqiful (ours) is the best for learning and improving, with every game reviewed by Pikafish on your device; PyChess is the best free web platform. Most improving players end up with two apps: one to play people, one to train.
What is the best free xiangqi app?
Xiangqi.com — free online games, bots, puzzles and lessons. In a browser, PyChess, Club Xiangqi and PlayOK are free. For a free offline opponent, Chinese Chess - Best XiangQi. Xiangqiful's free tier covers all ten opponents, all 80 lessons, and 2 analyses, 3 puzzles and 2 coach hints a day, with no ads.
Can I play xiangqi without reading Chinese characters?
Yes. Xiangqi.com, PyChess and Best XiangQi all offer western-style pieces. Xiangqiful has western, traditional and hybrid sets, with lessons that introduce the characters one piece at a time.
Can I play xiangqi offline on iPhone?
Yes — Xiangqiful (fully, including analysis and training), Chinese Chess - Best XiangQi, and Xiangqi.com's offline mode against the computer. PyChess, Club Xiangqi and PlayOK need a connection.
Which xiangqi app do Chinese and Vietnamese players use?
In mainland China, Tencent's 天天象棋 (Tiantian Xiangqi), a Chinese-language app on the China App Store. Vietnamese players call the game cờ tướng; Club Xiangqi runs in English and Vietnamese, and Xiangqi.com and Xiangqiful both offer Vietnamese.
Should I learn xiangqi if I already play chess?
Yes — it's chess's closest cousin, but the cannon's jump, the blockable horse, the river, the palace and stalemate-as-a-loss will all mislead a chess instinct. Learn them against a patient computer first. If you like the idea, our Chessful and Shogiful are built on the same play-review-train loop.
