Why is subscription-only workout pricing a real problem?

Workout logging suits a one-time purchase: it is simple in scope, infrequently updated, and used in bursts of 45 to 90 minutes. Saving a set of numbers does not require a recurring server bill.

Even so, the biggest workout loggers have all moved to subscription-only pricing:

Strong
Freemium with a Pro subscription. Multiple routines, progress tracking, and cloud sync are behind the paywall. Many lifters treat it as the baseline strength logger, and many also find it overpriced for what it does.
Hevy
The free tier covers a lot at first, but advanced features and some export functions need Hevy Pro, a recurring cost.
Fitbod
AI-generated workouts at ~$10/month. It works, and it is the most expensive option in the category.
Caliber
A coaching-style app with personalized programs. Its subscription price pays for human-coach overhead more than for the app.
