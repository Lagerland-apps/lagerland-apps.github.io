"One right answer" — and who actually checks it

The difference between a logic puzzle and a guessing game is whether the answer can be reasoned out. For grid puzzles that means a unique solution: a sudoku or nonogram with two valid fillings forces you to guess at some point. Nonograms Katana says each of its puzzles is tested by a program for exactly that. Tare (ours) goes one step further for its own genre: before release, a solver checks that each of its 80 levels has exactly one balanced arrangement, that the clue cards alone are enough to name every hidden weight, and that every decoy piece fits nowhere. How to solve a balance puzzle, and how Tare proves all 80 levels walks through a real level. Millrace (also ours) takes a different line: its levels can be solved more than one way, but an exhaustive search proves each level's shortest solution and shows it to you afterwards, as described in how Millrace finds and proves its puzzles. Endless games are a different pleasure. Threes!, Two Dots and Suika Game all have random elements, so there is no single answer to find, only a better or worse score.

Ads, lives and what the privacy label tells you

Pay once, play in peace
Baba Is You ($6.99) and Threes! ($5.99) charge up front, with no in-app purchases and a Data Not Collected label. Tare asks $2.99 once after 20 free levels. If a puzzle game is your evening wind-down, a one-time price is usually the calmest deal.
Free with ads
Flow Free, KAMI 2, Nonograms Katana, Good Sudoku and Threes! Freeplay are free because they show ads. Some sell a one-time purchase that removes them — $4.99 for Flow Free, a $2.99 VIP upgrade for Katana — and that is often worth it for a game you play daily.
Apple Arcade editions
Threes!+, Flow Free+ and Good Sudoku+ are ad-free, purchase-free editions included with an Apple Arcade subscription. Worth knowing if you already pay for Arcade.
Read the label before you install
Scroll to App Privacy on any App Store listing. "Data Used to Track You" (Flow Free, KAMI 2) means data from the app may be linked with other companies' data for advertising. "Data Not Collected" (Baba Is You, Simon Tatham's Puzzles, Tare, Millrace) means the developer says nothing leaves the app. Labels are self-reported, but they are the best quick signal there is.

For children, families and any language

Tare (ours) has no words in the puzzle itself and is rated 4+, with no ads, no chat and no account, so a child or a grandparent can play it in any language. If the goal is maths rather than play, DragonBox Algebra is the better tool: it teaches solving equations as a game and is recommended from age five, though it runs on a Kahoot! Kids account and subscription and collects data linked to that account. Baba Is You is wonderful for teenagers and adults, but its rules are written as words.

Popular, but a different kind of game

Two games turn up in every "puzzle" search that this guide leaves off the main list, because they are built around chance rather than deduction. Two Dots is a long-running saga with more than 5,000 levels and constant live events, played with five lives, power-ups and ads. Suika Game is a physics drop-and-merge chase for the watermelon, $2.99 with optional skins, or Suika Game+ on Apple Arcade. Both are great at what they do; neither has an answer to work out.

FAQ

What is the best logic puzzle game for iPhone?
It depends on the kind of puzzle. Baba Is You is the best premium puzzle game — $6.99 once, no ads, no in-app purchases. Good Sudoku is the best sudoku app, Nonograms Katana the best for nonograms, and Simon Tatham's Puzzles the best free collection. For a puzzle where every level has one answer you can prove, Tare and Millrace (both ours) are machine-checked level by level.
What is the best free puzzle game with no ads?
Simon Tatham's Puzzles — free, open source, 38 puzzle types, no in-app purchases, Data Not Collected. Millrace (ours) is free in full with no ads, and Tare (ours) has 20 free levels and no ads. With Apple Arcade, Threes!+, Flow Free+ and Good Sudoku+ are ad-free too.
Which puzzle games work offline on iPhone?
Tare and Millrace have no networking code, so everything works in airplane mode. Most puzzle games play installed levels offline, but daily puzzles, leaderboards, player-made puzzles and cloud saves usually need a connection — KAMI 2's daily challenge and player puzzles, for example.
Which puzzle games have exactly one solution per level?
Nonograms Katana tests each puzzle by program for a unique solution. Tare checks all 80 levels with a solver: one balanced arrangement, hidden weights fixed by the clue cards alone, decoys that fit nowhere. Millrace proves each level's shortest solution. Endless games like Threes!, Two Dots and Suika Game have random elements, so there is no single answer.
What is a good puzzle game for kids or for people who don't read English?
Tare has no words in the puzzle, is rated 4+ and has no ads, chat or account. DragonBox Algebra teaches equations from age five, on a Kahoot! Kids subscription. Baba Is You is a classic for teenagers and adults, but its rules are written as words.
Are Tare and Millrace free?
Millrace is free in full with no ads; its only purchases are optional themes, undo bundles and a tip. Tare's first 20 levels are free for good, and one $2.99 purchase unlocks the other 60, with Family Sharing. Both are ours, and both are labelled Data Not Collected.
