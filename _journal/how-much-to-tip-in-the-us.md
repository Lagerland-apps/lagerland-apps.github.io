If you are unsure how much to tip, you are in the majority. In a 2023 survey of 11,945 US adults by the [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/2023/11/09/tipping-culture-in-america-public-sees-a-changed-landscape/), only about a third said it was extremely or very easy to know whether to tip (34%) or how much (33%). And 72% said tipping is expected in more places than it was five years earlier.

The rules themselves are short. Here they are, followed by the arithmetic for the cases where they get awkward.

## How much to tip, by situation

The ranges are the Emily Post Institute's, from its [general tipping guide](https://emilypost.com/advice/general-tipping-guide). The last column applies them to example bills.

| Situation | Usual guidance | Worked example |
|---|---|---|
| Sit-down restaurant | 15–20% of the bill before tax | $60 of food: $9–$12 |
| Buffet | 10% before tax | $30: $3 |
| Takeout | No obligation; 10% for curbside service or a large, complicated order | $45 order: $0, or $4.50 |
| Food delivery | 10–15% of the bill; $2–5 for pizza, by size and difficulty | $32 order: $3.20–$4.80 |
| Bartender | $1–2 per drink, or 15–20% of the tab | See the bar tab below |
| Tip jar | No obligation; tip now and then for extra effort, or if you are a regular | Your call |
| Taxi | 15–20% of the fare, at least $1, plus $2 for the first bag and $1 for each one after | $38 fare, 2 bags: $8.70–$10.60 |
| Hair stylist | 15–20%, shared among everyone who worked on you | $70 cut: $10.50–$14 |
| Hotel housekeeping | $2–5 per day, left daily | 4 nights: $8–$20 |
| Valet | $2–5 when the car comes back | |
| Bellhop | $2 for the first bag, $1 for each additional bag | 3 bags: $4 |

The guide covers tip jars, not the tip screens now common at counters. A screen asks the same question as a jar, so the jar row is the closest guide.

## Why the restaurant row is different

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, an employer can pay a tipped worker a cash wage of $2.13 an hour and count up to $5.12 an hour of tips toward the $7.25 federal minimum wage, according to the Department of Labor's [Fact Sheet #15](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/15-tipped-employees-flsa). Some states, including California, Oregon and Washington, require the full state minimum wage before tips, as the Department's [state-by-state table](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/state/minimum-wage/tipped) shows. Where the tip credit applies, the restaurant tip is counted toward the server's wage itself.

## What Americans actually tip

Pew asked how often people tip in different settings. The share who say they always or often tip:

| Setting | Always or often tip |
|---|---|
| Sit-down restaurant | 92% |
| Haircut | 78% |
| Food delivery | 76% |
| Drink at a bar | 70% |
| Taxi or rideshare | 61% |
| Buying coffee | 25% |
| Fast casual restaurant | 12% |

The amounts sit lower than the etiquette range. A majority would tip 15% or less for an average meal at a sit-down restaurant, and only about a quarter would tip 20% or more. So 15% is where guidance and habit overlap, and 20% is the top of the range rather than the norm.

## Pre-tax or post-tax? The arithmetic

The Emily Post guide calculates the restaurant tip on the bill before tax. If you tip on the after-tax total instead, the gap is always the tip rate times the tax.

Dinner for four, $148.00 of food, and a sales tax of 8% (an example rate; yours depends on where you eat): $11.84 of tax, $159.84 in total.

| Tip | On $148.00 before tax | On $159.84 after tax | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15% | $22.20 | $23.98 | $1.78 |
| 18% | $26.64 | $28.77 | $2.13 |
| 20% | $29.60 | $31.97 | $2.37 |

At an 18% tip and an 8% tax, tipping on the total adds 1.44% of the food bill: $2.13 here, about 53 cents a head. Neither choice is rude. Picking one and using it every time is the useful habit.

## The bill that already includes a tip

Some restaurants add an automatic gratuity, often for large parties, so read the bill before you reach for a percentage. Under the Fair Labor Standards Act a compulsory charge for service is not a tip ([DOL Fact Sheet #15](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/15-tipped-employees-flsa)). The IRS lists a large dining party's automatic gratuity as a service charge, because a tip has to be voluntary and its amount set by the customer ([IRS fact sheet FS-2017-08](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/employer-reminder-reporting-tips-versus-service-charges-key-differences-between-categories-affect-employees-tax-reporting)).

Missing the line is expensive. A party of eight, $420 of food, an 18% automatic gratuity of $75.60. Add a reflexive 20% ($84.00) on top and service has cost $159.60, or 38% of the food. Leaving something extra for exceptional service is still your choice; doing it by accident is the thing to avoid.

## The bar tab: per drink or percentage?

The bartender row offers two rules, and which one is more generous depends on the price of the drink.

| Round | $1 a drink | $2 a drink | 15–20% of the tab |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 beers at $7 ($42) | $6 (14%) | $12 (29%) | $6.30–$8.40 |
| 6 cocktails at $15 ($90) | $6 (7%) | $12 (13%) | $13.50–$18.00 |

The rules meet at about $10 a drink, where $1.50–$2 is 15–20%. Below that, $2 a drink runs well above 20%. Above it, $1 a drink falls well under 15%, and the percentage is the rule to use.

## When a group splits the tip

The tip follows the food. If everyone ordered about the same, divide the total with tip by the number of people. If not, give each person the same percentage on what they ordered: at 20%, a $16 salad carries $3.20 of tip and a $52 steak carries $10.40. ## TL;DR

- **Sit-down restaurants: 15–20% of the bill before tax.** Buffets 10%. Most Americans tell Pew they would leave 15% or less.
- **Delivery 10–15%, taxis and haircuts 15–20%, housekeeping $2–5 a day, valets $2–5.** Takeout and tip jars carry no obligation.
- **Tipping after tax adds the tip rate times the tax:** $2.13 on an 18% tip for $148 of food at 8% tax.
- **At the bar, $1–2 a drink and 15–20% meet at about $10 a drink.** On pricier cocktails, use the percentage.
- **An automatic gratuity is a service charge under DOL and IRS rules.** Read the bill before adding 20% on top.
