Clippet Terms of Service

Last updated: 5 November 2026

These terms add to Apple's Standard License Agreement for App Store apps (EULA), which governs your licence to use Clippet together with the App Store Terms of Service. Where the two differ, Apple's EULA controls the licence and the download; these terms add what is specific to Clippet: Clippet Pro, refunds, your clips, and liability. Clippet is made by Lagerland Apps.

1. Clippet Pro

Clippet is free to download and use. Clippet Pro is an optional upgrade that shows your whole history and adds boards, Quick Paste and pasting into other apps on the Mac, your clips in the Clippet keyboard, and custom actions. It is sold in three plans:

Plan Billing Renews automatically Free trial
MonthlyEvery monthYes, until cancelledNone
YearlyEvery yearYes, until cancelledYes, for new subscribers
LifetimeOne paymentNo, one-time purchaseNone

The App Store sets each plan's price in your country and currency. The purchase screen in Clippet shows the price, the billing period and any free trial before you confirm, and the App Store charges what it shows.

One purchase, every device: Clippet Pro unlocks on iPhone, iPad and Mac for the Apple Account that bought it. All three plans work with Family Sharing, so the members of your family group get Clippet Pro too.

Auto-renewal (Monthly and Yearly): a subscription renews at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, and the renewal is charged to the payment method on your Apple Account. Manage or cancel it in Clippet under Settings › Clippet Pro › Manage Subscription, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad, or in the App Store on the Mac. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Clippet Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Free trial (Yearly): new subscribers to Yearly get a free trial, and the purchase screen shows how long it lasts. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged. If you don't, the Yearly price is charged when the trial ends and at each renewal after that. Apple decides who is eligible for a trial; an Apple Account that has subscribed to Clippet Pro before may not be offered one.

Lifetime: a single non-consumable purchase that keeps Clippet Pro for as long as Apple supports the App Store account that bought it, with no renewal. If you already subscribe, buying Lifetime does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in Manage Subscription so it stops renewing.

Restore Purchases: on a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases in Settings › Clippet Pro or on the purchase screen, and Clippet asks the App Store which plan your Apple Account holds.

2. When you don't have Clippet Pro

Without Clippet Pro, Clippet keeps saving everything, and the library shows your pinned clips and the clips from the last 30 days. Older clips stay saved on your device, in your iCloud and in your backups, hidden until you have Clippet Pro again. If a subscription ends, your boards and the clips on them are hidden the same way; nothing is deleted because Clippet Pro ended. Clippet deletes clips only when you delete them, set them to delete themselves, or choose a Keep History period.

3. Refunds

Lagerland Apps cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases. Apple handles them under its own refund policy. To ask for one, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, find the Clippet charge and choose "Request a refund". Apple decides each request.

4. Your clips

What you copy, save and share with Clippet is yours, and you are responsible for it, including any rights or permissions it needs. Your clips stay on your devices and in your own iCloud; Lagerland Apps has no access to them. How Clippet handles your data is described in the Clippet privacy policy.

Clippet is not a password manager or an encrypted vault. It skips copies that password managers mark as private, but only when the source app marks them, and backup files are not encrypted. Don't rely on Clippet as the only place you keep something you cannot afford to lose: keep your own copies of important data.
