That information stays on your device and in your own iCloud. The app sends a small amount of information to Lagerland's own server, and nothing else: a random install identifier created on first launch (not tied to your name, email or Apple ID), the install date, and a referral code if you arrived through a coach's link or picked one; and, when you buy Pro, the transaction ID, the product and the subscription start or end date, so the purchase can be verified and a referring coach credited. No workout, health or contact data is sent, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking. data_storage: > By default, all workout data and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is securely stored in your personal iCloud account and synced across your devices using Apple’s infrastructure. GymLogger X does not store workout data on its own servers. third_parties: > GymLogger X does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Apple processes limited data as part of services you choose to use, such as iCloud sync, App Store purchases, Apple Health, and Apple Watch connectivity. sensitive_data: > If you choose to connect Apple Health or use Apple Watch features, GymLogger X reads heart rate and active energy, and saves your finished workouts to Apple Health as strength-training sessions, through Apple’s frameworks.
