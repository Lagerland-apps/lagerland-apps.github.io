Where program delivery goes wrong

PDF friction
Good programs lose momentum when delivery is clumsy: downloads, folders, instructions, and clients unsure what to do today.
Unnecessary messages
“Where do I find the workout?” “Which week am I on?” These questions come from the file format, and answering them is not coaching.
Overbuilt platforms
Many coaching apps come with dashboards, check-ins, analytics, and admin work you never asked for.
Low follow-through
When starting feels complicated, clients put it off, and clients who put it off drop out, even on a good program.

What GymLogger X does

Program delivery as an app
Share your program as a spreadsheet: week, day, exercise, sets and reps, with RIR or RPE if you use them. Clients import it once with GymLogger X Pro, then open the app, see today's workout, log it, and move on.
Self-guided by design
There is no coach dashboard and no live monitoring. Clients follow the plan on their own, and that is intended.
Training-first logging
Sets, reps, load, supersets, and progress tracking, without social feeds or gamification.
Fits your existing system
Keep your payments, session tracking, and coaching style. GymLogger X replaces the PDF and leaves the rest of your business alone.

Coach FAQ

Is this a full coaching platform?
No. GymLogger X has no dashboards, client management, check-ins, or messaging, by design. It does program delivery and logging only.
Can I track my clients inside GymLogger X?
No. Clients use the app on their own, which keeps your workload low.
Do clients have to pay?
Logging is free. Importing your program from a spreadsheet needs Pro ($1.99 a month, $17.99 a year, or $39.99 once), so clients who want your plan inside the app will need it.
Does this replace PDFs completely?
No, and it doesn’t need to. Many coaches keep selling PDFs as they do today. GymLogger X is another delivery format for clients who would rather follow a program inside an app than manage files.
Can I stop recommending it later?
Yes. There is no contract or lock-in, so recommend it only as long as it fits your coaching.
