This policy explains what OneHander stores on your iPhone, what the websites you visit can see, and the choices you have. data_collection: none data_storage: > OneHander keeps a small amount of data on your iPhone, inside the app's own container. Your settings (search engine, One-Hand Side, Portrait Controls, Scrubber Speed and the on/off switches) are stored there. While Reopen Tabs at Launch is on, the addresses of your open tabs and which tab was selected are stored too, so they can reopen the next time you start the app; turning the setting off deletes them. Websites you visit can store cookies, cached files and logins in the iPhone's standard web-data store for OneHander, as they would in any browser. Apart from loading the pages and searches you ask for, OneHander makes no network requests, and it never contacts Lagerland Apps. Data leaves your iPhone in these cases only. Websites you open: OneHander is a browser built on WebKit, Apple's browser engine. Each website you open receives the requests a browser sends, including your IP address, and can set its own cookies and run its own analytics or advertising under its own privacy policy. OneHander does not share anything about your browsing with those sites beyond what a browser sends to load a page. Searches: when you type something into the address bar that is not a web address, OneHander sends it as a search to the search engine chosen in Settings (DuckDuckGo unless you change it). That search engine's privacy policy then applies. Sharing: Share Page hands the page's address to the iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it. Purchase: OneHander is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and Lagerland Apps never sees your payment details. your_choices: - > Clear Website Data in Settings removes the cookies, cache and logins that websites have stored in OneHander.
