Privacy here is not a setting you switch on — it is how the game is built. Tare has no account system, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and no networking code: the game never sends or receives anything itself. This policy explains exactly what Tare keeps on your device, what Apple handles when you choose to use its services, and what happens when you tap a link that leads outside the game. data_collection: none data_storage: > Everything Tare records stays on your iPhone or iPad. For each level you have solved, it stores the level, the date you first solved it, and the best star grade you earned; for a level you left part-way, it stores where the pieces were so you can pick up where you stopped. If you buy the chapter unlock, it keeps a single record that Chapters II–IV are unlocked — no price, no date, and no receipt — so the chapter wall draws correctly when the game starts. Your settings (sound, haptics, high contrast, and your chosen canvas) are saved in the game's local preferences. This is stored with Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults on the device only. It is not uploaded, not synced to iCloud, and not shared with anyone, and deleting Tare removes it. Some things Tare deliberately never writes down at all: how often you use the Lantern hint, and how many levels you have solved in a session (used only to decide whether to show Apple's review prompt), exist in memory while the game is open and are gone when it closes. third_parties: > Tare contains no third-party software of any kind — no analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs — and every framework it uses is Apple's. A few Apple services are involved only when you use them. If you buy the chapter unlock or restore it, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy; Tare never sees your payment details or your Apple Account, and receives only Apple's confirmation that the unlock belongs to you. Family Sharing of that purchase is managed by Apple in the same way. After you finish certain chapters, Tare may ask iOS to show Apple's standard rating prompt; iOS decides whether it appears, and Tare learns nothing about what you do with it. Finally, four links in the game open outside it: Rate Tare opens the App Store, Privacy opens this page in your browser, Support opens your own mail app addressed to our support email (nothing is pre-filled and nothing is sent unless you write and send it yourself), and the unlock screen's Terms of Use opens Apple's standard licence agreement. Once you leave the game through one of those links, that app or website's own privacy practices apply. sensitive_data: > Tare asks for no permissions at all. It does not access your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendars, or health data, and it collects no health, financial, or biometric information. There is no account to create, no email address to hand over, and no sign-in of any kind. To give the game board a gentle sense of depth, Tare reads your device's orientation from its motion sensor while the game is on screen. That reading is used live, on the device, to shift the background by a few points; it is never stored, never sent anywhere, and the sensor is switched off entirely when Tare is in the background or when Reduce Motion is turned on in your device settings. children: > Tare is a wordless puzzle suitable for players of any age.
