If you choose to add a photo to an event, Soon. accesses only the photo or photos you explicitly select using Apple’s system photo picker. The copies that widgets use and any cached weather information stay on your device. If iCloud device backups are enabled, Apple may also include app data in your personal iCloud backup according to your device settings. Soon. does not operate its own servers, and we cannot read your data. “Delete All My Data” in Settings removes your events from your device and from iCloud. icloud_sync: - > iCloud Sync uses your own iCloud account and works only while you are signed in to iCloud. Your events, with their photos, plans, notes, and memories, are kept in the private CloudKit database of that account and sync to your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy, and Lagerland Apps has no access to it. In Settings, iCloud Sync shows the sync status and when your events last synced. third_parties: > Soon. does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it contains no third-party SDKs. When you search for a place, the text you type goes to Apple Maps search. If the weather forecast is on for an event, Soon. sends the coordinates of the place you picked to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to get the forecast, along with the standard technical data required for internet communication.
