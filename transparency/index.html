What you're looking at

Privacy Manifests & Required Reason APIs

Since iOS 17, Apple has asked apps and the SDKs inside them to ship a Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy): a machine-readable file that says what data is collected, whether it is used for tracking, and which "Required Reason" APIs the app calls. The App Privacy section (the Privacy Nutrition Label) on an App Store page is a separate set of answers the developer gives Apple in App Store Connect.

NSPrivacyTracking is the most important key: false declares that the app does not track you as Apple defines it, which means it does not link your data with other companies' data for advertising or advertising measurement, or share it with data brokers. NSPrivacyTrackingDomains lists every domain the binary may contact for tracking purposes; in every Lagerland app it is an empty array. Required Reason APIs are categories of system APIs (UserDefaults, file timestamps, disk space, system boot time) that can be misused for fingerprinting, so Apple asks apps to state why they use them. The declaration for each Lagerland app follows below.

What's not on this list

What no Lagerland app does

  • No advertising SDKs. That means nothing from Meta, Google Mobile Ads, AppLovin, Unity Ads, IronSource, AdMob, or MoPub, and nothing else that loads creatives or matches users.
  • No third-party analytics. None of Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, Heap, or PostHog SaaS, and no Sentry telemetry that identifies users.
  • No attribution / MMP. None of Adjust, AppsFlyer, Branch, Singular, Kochava, or Tenjin.
  • No IDFA request. None of the apps calls ATTrackingManager.requestTrackingAuthorization or includes the NSUserTrackingUsageDescription key the tracking prompt needs, so no Lagerland app can ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • No account systems. Where an app syncs, it uses your own iCloud. Where an app uses App Store Connect (AppMeta, AppMeta Pulse, and Mockly's upload), it uses your API key and sends it only to Apple; AppMeta and AppMeta Pulse keep it in the Keychain.
  • No studio server. No app sends your data to a server run by Lagerland Apps. Apps that need the internet for their job connect straight to that service, such as Wikipedia for WanderWiki.
  • Purchases through Apple only. Trials and subscriptions use Apple's purchase sheet, which shows the price and the renewal before you confirm, and you cancel them in Settings like any other App Store subscription.
