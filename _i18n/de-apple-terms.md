# Apple's own German names (de)

Part of the language sheet `_i18n/de.md`. Verified 2026-10-26 on Apple's German pages (source per row). Use these exactly; never invent an Apple name.


Checked 2026-10-26 against Apple's live German pages (fetched directly). The iPhone and Apple Watch user guides on support.apple.com/de-de are for iOS 27 and watchOS 27.

How Apple writes them in running text:
- **App names** go in German quotes after "App": die App „Health“, die App „Fitness“, die App „Training“, die App „Dateien“. On marketing pages (apple.com/de) Apple drops the quotes and writes "Health App", "Fitness App", "EKG App", "Trainingsapp". Support pages also use the hyphenated "EKG-App".
- **Features used as nouns** often get quotes too: „Bildschirmzeit“ aktivieren, „Nicht stören“, Fokus „Schlafen“.
- Quotation marks are German low-high: „…“ (U+201E … U+201C).

Abbreviations: ap = apps.apple.com/de, ah = https://www.apple.com/de/health/, ig = iPhone user guide https://support.apple.com/de-de/guide/iphone/welcome/ios (table of contents), wg = Apple Watch user guide https://support.apple.com/de-de/guide/watch/welcome/watchos.

In-app purchase price format: `2,99 €`. Comma decimal, then a **NO-BREAK SPACE (U+00A0, bytes C2 A0)**, then €. Checked in the raw HTML. Other Observa rows: Premium Jahresabo `24,99 €`, Premium Einmalkauf `59,99 €`, Premium Monatsabo `3,99 €`, Entwickler unterstützen `1,00 €`. Apple's own IAP-type nouns seen there: Jahresabo, Monatsabo, Einmalkauf (these are the developer's IAP display names, not Apple UI).
