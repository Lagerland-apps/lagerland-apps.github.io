---

Millrace is a tile logic puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Equal tiles merge when you slide them together, but each of its 64 levels is a closed problem with a goal, a move limit, and a known best solution.

A level gives you a **set board**, a **fixed queue of arriving tiles**, and a **move limit**. Tiles arrive one at a time at the **arrival cell**, and your job is to build one exact number on the **marked cell**. Nothing inside a level is random, so you can plan it all the way through.

You can lose in two ways, and both come from your own moves. Block the arrival cell and the next tile has nowhere to land. Build past the target, a 128 when the level asks for 64, and the goal is lost. After a loss you can undo that move or retry.

## Every level has a proven best solution

Nobody draws Millrace's levels by hand. A generator **finds them by search**, and each one then has to pass automated checks before it ships:

- A **breadth-first solver** finds the level's shortest solution. A level without a proven solution does not ship.
- That shortest solution becomes the level's **best possible**. When you solve a level, the result card shows your move count, your best, and the best possible, so you know how close to perfect you came.
- An automated test checks that **random play rarely wins**.

The full story of the generator and the solver is in the journal: [how Millrace's levels are made](/journal/how-millrace-levels-are-made/).

The 64 levels sit in six chapters: **Warm-up**, which teaches the rules, then **Tactics**, **Pressure**, **Precision**, **Deep Water**, and **Mastery**. The app opens on the level list, with a Continue button for your next unsolved level.

## Freeplay, the Daily Challenge, and help when you want it

**Freeplay** is the endless board, one tap from the level list. It has no goal and no move limit, and it keeps your stats and streaks. The **Daily Challenge** gives every player the same board each day.

**Hint** draws a strong next move on the board and tells you why. In a puzzle level the move comes from the solver and always leads to a solution; in Freeplay it is a suggestion. **Coaching notes** appear on the game-over card and name one habit that keeps showing up in your recent games. They never interrupt play, and you can switch them off.

## What it costs, and what it collects

The game is free in full, with no ads and no subscription. The optional purchases are theme packs, the Complete Collection of all sixteen paid themes, undo bundles for Freeplay, and a tip. You can try any theme on your own board before you buy it.

There is no account. The app has no tracking and no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs, and your progress stays on your device. The whole game works offline; the shop, Game Center, and links you open are the only parts that need a connection. The [full privacy policy](/apps/millrace/privacy/) sets out what is stored and what Apple handles.

Millrace runs on iPhone and iPad, requires iOS or iPadOS 18, and is available in 40 localizations.
