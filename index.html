One person makes every app

Lagerland Apps is a one-person Apple studio in Finland building {{ site.data.catalog.meta.live_app_count }} privacy-first applications for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac. Every app is free of third-party trackers, ships zero advertising SDKs and requires no account. The software pays for itself through one-time purchases, optional Pro subscriptions or free tiers, and never through ads, data brokers or growth-hack dark patterns.

Categories: health analytics, daily planning, bill tracking, workout logging, reading, board games and puzzles, Apple developer tools and utilities. The apps are for people who refuse ad-supported apps, want their data to stay local, and prefer single-purpose tools to bloated platforms.

Frequently asked

Who makes Lagerland Apps?
Lagerland Apps is a one-person, independent Apple studio based in Finland, shipping privacy-first apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac since 2025. The same developer designs, codes, and supports every app, with no VC, advertisers, or data brokers involved. The apps are built under EU jurisdiction with GDPR as the default.
Do Lagerland apps track me or collect personal data?
No. Every app passes Apple's App Tracking Transparency check with no IDFA request, ships zero advertising SDKs, and includes no analytics that identify users. Data stays on-device wherever possible, any cloud sync uses your own iCloud account (compatible with Advanced Data Protection), and you can verify the claim on each app's App Store Privacy Nutrition Label.
Do the apps require accounts or a login?
No. None of the apps require an account or email sign-up. You download from the App Store and start using them, with nothing to register and no account to lose access to.
How do you make money if there are no ads?
The people who use the apps pay for them, through one-time purchases, optional Pro subscriptions, and freemium models where the core experience is free. No advertiser pays for anything.
Do the apps work on iPhone, iPad, and Mac?
Most apps run on iPhone and iPad, several also support Apple Watch, and developer tools like AppMeta and Mockly are native Mac apps. Each app page lists its supported platforms.
What are the best alternatives to mainstream tracker-heavy apps?
Lagerland Apps has privacy-first alternatives in several categories: Taskful Day (now native on iPhone, iPad, and Mac) instead of feed-based planners, GymLogger X instead of subscription-locked workout loggers, Observa as a private Apple Health analytics alternative, and AllPaid as a calm bill tracker alternative to ad-supported finance apps.
