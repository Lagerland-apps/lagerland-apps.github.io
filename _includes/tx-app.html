{%- comment -%} For another app shown on the current page (card, stamp, list, footer link): finds that app's translated page in the current page's language. Words (tagline, description, URL) come from tx_app, facts from the master doc. app the master document from site.apps Sets tx_app (the translated page, or the master on English pages and when no translation exists) and tx_en (true when a non-English page has no translation of this app: lists skip it, links fall back to English). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign tx_app = include.app -%} {%- assign tx_en = false -%} {%- if page.lang and page.lang != "en" -%} {%- unless i18n_apps_lang == page.lang -%} {%- assign i18n_apps = site.pages | where: "lang", page.lang | where: "layout", "app" -%} {%- assign i18n_apps_lang = page.lang -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- assign _txa = i18n_apps | where: "slug", include.app.slug | first -%} {%- if _txa -%}{%- assign tx_app = _txa -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign tx_en = true -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
